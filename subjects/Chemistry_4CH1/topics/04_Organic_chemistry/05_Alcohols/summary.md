# Alcohols — Summary

Specification points 4.29C–4.33C (Paper 2 only) · Topic 4 Organic chemistry.

## Essential facts

- Functional group **–OH**.
- Methanol CH₃OH · ethanol CH₃CH₂OH · propan-1-ol CH₃CH₂CH₂OH · butan-1-ol CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂OH.
- Oxidation of ethanol:
  - burning: C₂H₅OH + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 3H₂O;
  - microbial oxidation by oxygen in air → ethanoic acid (vinegar);
  - heat with potassium dichromate(VI) in dilute sulfuric acid → ethanoic acid (orange → green).
- Manufacture:
  - ethene + steam: C₂H₄ + H₂O → C₂H₅OH; phosphoric acid catalyst, ~300 °C, 60–70 atm;
  - fermentation: C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂; yeast enzymes, ~30 °C, no air.
- No air: stops ethanol being oxidised to ethanoic acid. ~30 °C: enzymes work fastest; colder is too
  slow, hotter denatures them.

## Common traps

- Alcohols are not alkalis: –OH is covalent.
