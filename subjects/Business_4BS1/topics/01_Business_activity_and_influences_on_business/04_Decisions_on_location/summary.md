## Essentials

- Location balances **costs** against access to **customers**.
- Proximity to: **market** (delivery costs, customer access), **labour** (skills, wage rates), **materials** (transport of heavy inputs), **competitors** (+ draw customers to area; − shared customers, price competition).
- Nature of activity: primary → resource; heavy inputs → materials; bulky/perishable output → market; skilled work → labour; visited services → busy areas; online services → low-cost areas.
- **E-commerce:** + low-cost location, worldwide 24/7 reach; − delivery/returns, can't try products. **Fixed premises:** + see/try, passing trade; − high rent, limited reach.
- **Legal controls:** planning permission, environmental laws. **Trade blocs:** locate inside to avoid tariffs.
- **Relocation:** + lower costs, new markets, bigger site; − moving costs, lost staff, bad publicity.
