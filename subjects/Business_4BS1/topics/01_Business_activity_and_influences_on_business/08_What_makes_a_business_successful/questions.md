## Practice

1. **[1]** Define the term *revenue*.
2. **[2]** A bakery sells 1 200 cakes at £2.50 each. Calculate its revenue.
3. **[1]** State one way a business could measure customer satisfaction.
4. **[3]** Explain one reason why a profitable business may fail.
5. **[3]** Explain one reason why a social enterprise may not use profit as its main measure of success.
6. **[6]** A small bookshop has seen sales fall as more customers buy books online. Analyse two reasons why the bookshop may fail.

## Marking guidance (not an official mark scheme)

1. The money a business receives from selling its goods or services.
2. 1 200 × £2.50 (1) = £3 000 (1).
3. E.g. customer surveys; online reviews; number of repeat customers; number of complaints.
4. Profit may be tied up in inventory or money owed by customers (1) → so it lacks cash (1) → cannot pay suppliers or wages and may be forced to close (1).
5. Its main objectives are social (1) → so success is judged by its social or environmental impact (1) → profits are reinvested in the cause rather than paid to owners (1).
6. Two reasons, three linked strands each: failure to adapt — no online sales → customers switch to online sellers → revenue falls → losses; not competitive — higher prices than online rivals → fewer sales → cash-flow problems → cannot pay rent or suppliers.
