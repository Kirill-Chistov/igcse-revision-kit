# Alkanes — Questions

Specification points 4.19–4.22 · Topic 4 Organic chemistry. Marks in brackets.

> All questions and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository; they are not
> official Pearson questions or mark schemes.

## 1. Recall

1. Give the general formula of the alkanes. (1)
2. Explain why alkanes are described as saturated hydrocarbons. (2)
3. Give the molecular formula of the alkane with 20 carbon atoms. (1)

## 2. Formulae and names

4. Draw the displayed formula of propane. (1)
5. Name the alkane CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂CH₃ and give its molecular formula. (2)
6. Draw the displayed formulae of the three isomers of C₅H₁₂. (3)

## 3. Reactions with halogens

7. State the condition needed for methane to react with chlorine. (1)
8. Write the equation for the reaction of methane with chlorine to form chloromethane. (2)
9. Ethane reacts with bromine in UV light. Give the name and displayed formula of the organic product
   and name the other product. (3)
10. What type of reaction is this? Explain your answer. (2)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ (1).
2. Contain carbon and hydrogen only (hydrocarbon) (1); only single C–C bonds / no C=C double bonds (1).
3. C₂₀H₄₂ (1).
4. Three C in a chain, each with four single bonds, eight H (1).
5. Pentane (1); C₅H₁₂ (1).
6. Pentane (1); 2-methylbutane (1); 2,2-dimethylpropane (1) — every C with four bonds, 12 H each.
7. Ultraviolet (UV) light (1).
8. CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl — CH₃Cl (1), rest correct (1).
9. Bromoethane (1); displayed C₂H₅Br with Br on one carbon (1); hydrogen bromide (1).
10. Substitution (1); a hydrogen atom is replaced by a bromine atom (1).
