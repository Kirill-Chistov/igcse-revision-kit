## Ratios

| Ratio | Formula | Good sign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gross profit margin | gross profit ÷ revenue × 100 | higher |
| Operating profit margin | operating profit ÷ revenue × 100 | higher |
| Markup | profit per item ÷ cost per item × 100 | higher |
| ROCE | operating profit ÷ capital employed × 100 | higher; above bank interest |
| Current ratio | current assets ÷ current liabilities | about 1.5–2 |
| Acid test | (current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities | about 1 |

- Raise GPM: raise price or cut cost of sales. OPM also rises if expenses are cut.
- Markup ÷ cost; margins ÷ revenue. Liquidity ratios are not percentages.

## Liquidity

- Ability to pay current liabilities when due. Too low → can't pay suppliers/wages → insolvency.
  Too high → idle cash/inventory. Acid test excludes inventory (slowest to turn into cash).
- Compare with previous years and similar businesses. % change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100.

## Using financial documents

- Assess performance against past years, targets, rivals; inform decisions (suppliers, prices,
  overheads, overdraft, expansion, dividends; investors, banks, suppliers outside).
- Limits: past data; ignores non-financial factors; comparisons mislead if firms differ.
