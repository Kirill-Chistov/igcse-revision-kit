## Essentials

- **External factors:** outside influences a business cannot control that affect its decisions.
- **Social:** population, lifestyles, tastes, attitudes → demand changes → product range, marketing.
- **Technological:** automation, new products, internet, online selling → lower costs, new products; − investment, training, faster obsolescence.
- **Environmental:** pollution, climate change, waste → sustainable materials, less waste and packaging.
- **Political:** policies, laws, taxes, stability → costs change; instability = risk.
- Going greener: + customers, lower waste costs, avoids fines, reputation; − higher costs, slow payback.
