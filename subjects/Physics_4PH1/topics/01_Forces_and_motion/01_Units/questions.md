# Forces and motion — units — Questions

Specification points 1.1–1.2P · Topic 1 Forces and motion. Marks in brackets. Questions 5–6 are
Paper 2 only.

> **All questions, answers and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository and
> are not official Pearson questions or mark schemes.**

## 1. Recall

1. Give the unit of: (a) acceleration, (b) gravitational field strength, (c) force. (3)
2. Convert: (a) 250 g to kg, (b) 36 cm to m, (c) 3 minutes to seconds. (3)

## 2. Application

3. Convert 90 km/h to m/s. (2)
4. Show that the unit of acceleration is m/s² using acceleration = change in velocity ÷ time. (2)
5. **[P2]** Give the unit of (a) the moment of a force, (b) momentum. (2)
6. **[P2]** A student writes the momentum of a 400 g ball moving at 5 m/s as "2000 kg m/s". Explain
   the mistake and give the correct value. (2)

## Answers and marking guidance

> Marking guidance written for this repository, not an official Pearson mark scheme.

1. (a) m/s² (1); (b) N/kg (1); (c) N (1).
2. (a) 0.25 kg (1); (b) 0.36 m (1); (c) 180 s (1).
3. 90 000 m ÷ 3600 s or 90 ÷ 3.6 (1) = 25 m/s (1).
4. Velocity in m/s divided by time in s (1) gives (m/s) ÷ s = m/s² (1).
5. (a) N m (1); (b) kg m/s (1).
6. Used the mass in grams instead of kilograms (1); 0.4 × 5 = 2 kg m/s (1).
