This is the **linear Mathematics A (Specification A)** qualification — *not* Mathematics B and
*not* a modular qualification.

## Qualification at a glance

| Tier | Papers | Duration each | Marks each | Grades targeted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | 1F and 2F | 2 h | 100 | 5–1 |
| **Higher** | **1H and 2H** | **2 h** | **100** | **9–4** |

Both papers of a tier are sat; each is externally assessed and carries 50% of the
qualification. A calculator, ruler, compasses and protractor may be used; a tier-specific
formulae sheet is provided in the examination.

The student's tier is recorded in `me/profile.md`. Higher Tier assumes all Foundation Tier
content as prior knowledge.

## Assessment objectives

| AO | Description | Weighting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AO1 | Number and algebra | 57–63% |
| AO2 | Shape, space and measures | 22–28% |
| AO3 | Handling data | 12–18% |

Students must also demonstrate **problem-solving** (translating problems into mathematical
processes) and **mathematical reasoning** (deductions, chains of reasoning, arguments and
proofs, accurate communication).

## How the tiers interact — this matters for study planning

| Marker | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Foundation` | Content listed at Foundation Tier only; Higher Tier says *"See Foundation Tier"*. Still assumed knowledge and still examinable at Higher. |
| `Higher extends` | Foundation content **plus** additional Higher Tier requirements. |
| `Higher only` | Content that appears **only** in the Higher Tier list. |

## Content

Specification points are given at sub-section level (e.g. `1.4 Powers and roots`). Within each
sub-section the official specification lists lettered requirements (A, B, C …) with worked
notes; those lettered requirements are the detail an AI agent must read from
`subjects/Mathematics_4MA1/Specification.pdf` before writing a topic.


## How to read this file

- This file is a **curriculum representation**, not revision notes. It exists so that AI
  agents can plan and trace study material without re-parsing the official PDF.
- Official identifiers (section numbers, specification point numbers, topic names) are
  preserved so every statement can be traced back to `subjects/Mathematics_4MA1/Specification.pdf`.
- Requirement wording is condensed/paraphrased from the official objective statements.
  **The official PDF is always authoritative** where this file is ambiguous.
- `specification_map.csv` maps each specification point to its planned folder in `Mathematics_4MA1/`.
- `coverage_checklist.md` tracks which points have study material written.
