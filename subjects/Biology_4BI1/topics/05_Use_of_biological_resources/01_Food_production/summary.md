# Food production — Summary

**Specification points 5.1–5.9B** · 5.9B is Paper 2 only

- **Glasshouses / polythene tunnels** control limiting factors (heat, light, CO₂, water) and keep
  pests out → faster photosynthesis → higher yield, longer season. Tunnels: cheaper, larger areas,
  less control.
- **More CO₂** → more raw material → faster photosynthesis, while CO₂ is limiting. **Higher
  temperature** → faster enzymes up to the optimum, then denaturing. Raise both; only worth it if
  extra yield pays for the cost.
- **Fertiliser** replaces minerals removed at harvest: nitrate → amino acids → proteins; magnesium
  → chlorophyll.
- **Pest control**: pests eat, compete with or infect crops. Pesticides: fast, but kill non-target
  species, accumulate in food chains, resistance, reapply. Biological control: specific, no
  chemicals or resistance, self-sustaining, but slow, incomplete, agent may become a pest.
- **Bread**: yeast respires anaerobically → CO₂ makes dough rise; baking kills yeast, removes ethanol.
- **Yoghurt**: pasteurise milk, cool, add Lactobacillus; lactose → lactic acid; low pH clots
  protein, sour, preserves.
- **Yeast practical**: boiled/cooled glucose + yeast under oil in a water bath; count bubbles per
  minute at each temperature; rate peaks ~35–40 °C. Control: boiled yeast. Better: gas syringe.
- **Fermenter**: aseptic (no competitors or toxins), nutrients, optimum temperature (water jacket)
  and pH (probe + acid/alkali), sterile air (aerobic respiration), stirring (even mixing and heat).
- **Fish farming (P2)**: clean aerated water; separate by size; nets; disease control; remove waste;
  small frequent high-protein feeds; selective breeding.
