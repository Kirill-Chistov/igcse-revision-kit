# Biology 4BI1 — Subject Rules

**Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Biology (4BI1)** — the **separate (single-award) Biology**
qualification. Not Double Award Science, and not the 4SD0 route.

## Paper structure

| Paper | Code | Duration | Marks | Weighting | Assesses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper 1 | 4BI1/1B | 2 h | 110 | 61.1% | Content **without** a `B` reference |
| Paper 2 | 4BI1/2B | 1 h 15 min | 70 | 38.9% | **All** content, including `B` |

Questions on either paper may come from any topic area. Calculators are allowed.

## The `B` rule — the single most important distinction

Specification statements printed in bold with a `B` reference are **Paper 2 only**. In this
repository they are marked with a trailing `B` (e.g. `2.40B`) and tagged in
`specification_map.csv`.

- Never present `B` content as required for Paper 1.
- Never omit `B` content when preparing for Paper 2.
- `B` statements usually take an already-covered sub-topic into greater depth, so they belong in
  the same topic pack, clearly labelled — not in a separate silo.

## No tiering

There is no Foundation/Higher split. Every student sits both papers.

## Required practicals

Points beginning `practical:` are the specification's required practical work. Method,
apparatus, variables, controls, expected results, sources of error and evaluation are all
assessable under AO3. The `B` rule applies to practicals too. Practical material lives inside
the relevant topic folder (no separate practicals folder).

## Assessment objectives

AO1 knowledge and understanding 38–42% · AO2 application, analysis and evaluation 38–42% ·
AO3 experimental skills, analysis and evaluation of data and methods 19–21%.

Roughly 60% of marks are *not* recall. Study material must build application and evaluation, not
just facts.

## Maths in biology

The specification's maths-skills appendix (arithmetic, handling data, algebra, graphs, geometry)
is assessed in biology contexts: magnification, surface area to volume ratio, percentage change,
rates, genetic ratios and probability, sampling and quadrat calculations.

## Watch out for

- Material written for AQA/OCR/Edexcel **UK GCSE** Biology — a different specification. Check
  the code 4BI1.
- Double Award Science material, which covers less.
