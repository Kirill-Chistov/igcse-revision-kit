## Practice

1. **[1]** Define the term *tariff*.
2. **[1]** Define the term *infrastructure*.
3. **[2]** A product costs $40 before a sales tax of 15% is added. Calculate the selling price.
4. **[2]** A business borrows €120 000 for one year at an interest rate of 5%. Calculate the total amount it repays.
5. **[3]** Explain one impact on a business of a fall in interest rates.
6. **[3]** Explain one disadvantage to a business of new environmental legislation.
7. **[6]** A furniture retailer sells most of its sofas to customers who pay using credit. Analyse the impact on the retailer of an increase in interest rates.

## Marking guidance (not an official mark scheme)

1. A tax on imported goods.
2. The basic facilities a country needs, e.g. transport, power and water supplies, telecommunications.
3. 40 × 1.15 (1) = $46 (1).
4. 120 000 × 1.05 (1) = €126 000 (1).
5. Borrowing is cheaper (1) → the business may take a loan to invest, e.g. in new machinery (1) → higher output or lower costs (1). Or: consumers spend more → higher sales.
6. It may need new equipment or processes to comply (1) → costs rise (1) → so it must raise prices or accept lower profit (1).
7. Credit repayments rise → customers have less disposable income → fewer sofa sales → lower revenue; if the retailer has loans, interest payments rise → costs up → profit falls.
