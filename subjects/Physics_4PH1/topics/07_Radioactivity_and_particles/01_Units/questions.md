# Radioactivity and particles — units — Questions

Specification point 7.1 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1.

> These questions and the marking guidance are **written for this repository by AI**. They are not
> official Pearson questions and the guidance is not an official mark scheme.

1. State the unit of activity and what one unit means. (2)
2. A source has a half-life of 20 min. How many half-lives pass in 3 h? (2)
3. A detector records 240 counts in 2 minutes. Calculate the count rate in counts per second. (2)
4. Explain why the count rate measured by a Geiger–Müller counter is less than the activity of the source. (1)
5. Convert a half-life of 1.5 h into seconds. (1)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. Becquerel, Bq (1); one nucleus decaying per second (1).
2. 3 h = 180 min (1); 180 ÷ 20 = 9 half-lives (1).
3. 240 ÷ 2 = 120 counts per minute (1); 120 ÷ 60 = 2 counts per second (1).
4. Only some of the radiation emitted by the source travels towards / enters the detector (1).
5. 1.5 × 3600 = 5400 s (1).
