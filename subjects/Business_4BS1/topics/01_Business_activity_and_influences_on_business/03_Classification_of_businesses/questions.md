## Practice

1. **[1]** Define the term *secondary sector*.
2. **[2]** State the sector in which each operates: (a) a fishing company; (b) a hotel.

4. **[3]** Explain one reason why a clothing factory depends on businesses in the tertiary sector.

## Marking guidance (not an official mark scheme)

1. Businesses that convert raw materials into finished or semi-finished goods.
2. (a) primary (1); (b) tertiary (1).
3. It converts raw materials (wood) (1) into finished goods such as tables and chairs (1).
4. It needs transport (1) → to receive cotton and deliver clothes to shops (1) → without it the factory cannot get materials or sell its products (1). Also banking, insurance, retailers.
