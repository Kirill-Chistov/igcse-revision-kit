# Density and pressure — Summary

Specification points 5.3–5.7 · Topic 5 · both papers.

## Equations (all three must be recalled — not given)

| Equation | Units |
| --- | --- |
| ρ = *m* / *V* | kg/m³ = kg ÷ m³ (or g/cm³) |
| *p* = *F* / *A* | Pa = N ÷ m² |
| *p* = *h* × ρ × *g* | Pa = m × kg/m³ × N/kg |

## Essential facts and ideas

- **Density** = mass per unit volume; the same for any sample of one material.
- **Measuring density:** mass on a balance; volume by *l* × *w* × *h* (regular), displacement
  can / measuring cylinder (irregular), measuring cylinder (liquid, mass by difference). Read the
  bottom of the meniscus at eye level.
- **Pressure** = force per unit area, at right angles to the surface. Small area → large pressure
  (knife, pin); large area → small pressure (snowshoes, wide tyres).
- In a fluid at rest, pressure at a point acts **equally in all directions** (particles hit from
  every direction).
- Pressure increases with **vertical depth**; *h*ρ*g* uses the density of the **fluid**; total
  pressure = atmospheric + *h*ρ*g*.

## Common traps

- cm² → m²: ÷ 10 000.
- *h* is vertical depth, not distance along a slope.
