# Co-ordination and response — Summary

**Specification points 2.80–2.95B** · 2.95B is Paper 2 only

- Stimulus → receptor → co-ordinator (CNS) → effector (muscle/gland) → response.
- Homeostasis: constant internal environment — e.g. body water content, body temperature.

## Plants

- Tropisms = growth responses. Shoot: +phototropic, −geotropic. Root: +geotropic, −phototropic.
- Auxin made in shoot tip → moves to shaded side → cells there elongate more → shoot bends to light.

## Nervous system

- Nervous: electrical impulses, neurones, fast, short-lived, precise. Hormonal: chemicals in blood, slower, longer-lasting, widespread.
- CNS = brain + spinal cord; linked to sense organs by nerves. Sensory → relay → motor neurones.
- Synapse: impulse → neurotransmitter released → diffuses across gap → binds receptors → new impulse; one direction only.
- Reflex arc (hot object): skin receptor → sensory → relay (spinal cord) → motor → biceps contracts. Fast, automatic, protective.

## Eye

- Cornea refracts; iris controls pupil; lens focuses; retina receptors; optic nerve to brain; ciliary muscle + suspensory ligaments change lens shape; sclera protects.
- Near: ciliary contract, ligaments slack, lens fat. Far: ciliary relax, ligaments tight, lens thin.
- Bright: circular muscles contract → pupil small. Dim: radial muscles contract → pupil large.

## Skin

- Hot: sweat evaporates (removes heat); vasodilation → more blood near surface → more heat lost.
- Cold: vasoconstriction → less blood near surface; little sweat.

## Hormones

- Adrenaline (adrenal glands): fight or flight — heart rate, blood glucose up.
- Insulin (pancreas): lowers blood glucose — glucose → glycogen in liver and muscles.
- Testosterone (testes), oestrogen and progesterone (ovaries): sex characteristics, uterus lining.
- (P2) Pituitary: ADH (water reabsorption), FSH (egg matures, oestrogen), LH (ovulation).
