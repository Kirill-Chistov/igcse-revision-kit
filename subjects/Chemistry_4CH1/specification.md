## Qualification at a glance

| Paper | Code | Duration | Marks | Weighting | Content assessed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chemistry Paper 1 | 4CH1/1C | 2 h | 110 | 61.1% | Core content only — statements **without** a `C` reference |
| Chemistry Paper 2 | 4CH1/2C | 1 h 15 min | 70 | 38.9% | **All** content, including `C` statements |

## Assessment objectives

| AO | Description | Weighting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AO1 | Knowledge and understanding of chemistry | 38–42% |
| AO2 | Application of knowledge and understanding, analysis and evaluation of chemistry | 38–42% |
| AO3 | Experimental skills, analysis and evaluation of data and methods in chemistry | 19–21% |

## Course distinctions that affect what can be assessed

- **`C` statements are Paper 2 only.**
- **No tiering** — all students sit the same two papers.
- Points beginning *practical:* are the **required practical work** and are assessable.
- Calculations, formulae, equations (including ionic equations) and the Periodic Table are
  used throughout; the maths-skills appendix applies in chemistry contexts.


## How to read this file

- This file is a **curriculum representation**, not revision notes. It exists so that AI
  agents can plan and trace study material without re-parsing the official PDF.
- Official identifiers (section numbers, specification point numbers, topic names) are
  preserved so every statement can be traced back to `subjects/Chemistry_4CH1/Specification.pdf`.
- Requirement wording is condensed/paraphrased from the official objective statements.
  **The official PDF is always authoritative** where this file is ambiguous.
- `specification_map.csv` maps each specification point to its planned folder in `Chemistry_4CH1/`.
