# Physics 4PH1 — Subject Rules

**Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics (4PH1)** — the **separate (single-award) Physics**
qualification, not Double Award Science.

## Paper structure

| Paper | Code | Duration | Marks | Weighting | Assesses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper 1 | 4PH1/1P | 2 h | 110 | 61.1% | Content **without** a `P` reference |
| Paper 2 | 4PH1/2P | 1 h 15 min | 70 | 38.9% | **All** content, including `P` |

## The `P` rule

Bold statements carrying a `P` reference are **Paper 2 only**, marked with a trailing `P` in
`specification.md` and tagged in `specification_map.csv`.

## No tiering

No Foundation/Higher split; all students sit both papers.

## Units come first

Every section of the specification opens with a `(a) Units` sub-topic. Correct SI units, prefixes
and unit conversions are assessable throughout and are a common source of lost marks. Every topic
must state the units of every quantity it introduces.

## Equations: recalled vs given

Some equations are provided in the examination; others must be **recalled**. The official
specification is the authority on which is which — check it before telling the student an
equation will be supplied. Every topic should state, for each equation: the equation, what each
symbol means, its units, and whether it must be recalled.

## Required practicals

Points beginning `practical:` are required practical work, assessable under AO3. Practical material lives
inside the relevant topic folder (no separate practicals folder).

## Assessment objectives

AO1 38–42% · AO2 38–42% · AO3 19–21%.

## Watch out for

- Material for Cambridge IGCSE Physics (0625/0972) or UK GCSE Physics — different specifications.
  Check the code 4PH1.
- `4PH1` includes **Astrophysics** (section 8), which several other IGCSE physics courses do not.
