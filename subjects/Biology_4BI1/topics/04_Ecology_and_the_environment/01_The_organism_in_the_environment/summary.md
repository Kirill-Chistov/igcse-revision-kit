# The organism in the environment — Summary

Specification points 4.1–4.5 (4.3B, 4.4B Paper 2 only).

- **Population:** one species, one area, same time. **Community:** all populations in an area.
  **Habitat:** where an organism lives. **Ecosystem:** community + abiotic environment, interacting.
- **Population size (4.2):** grid of two tapes → random coordinates → count species in ≥ 10
  quadrats → mean → population = mean × (total area ÷ quadrat area). Same quadrat size and number
  in both areas. Random = no bias, representative.
- **Biodiversity (P2):** variety of different species in an area; measure as number of species in
  the quadrats.
- **Distribution (P2):** transect with quadrats at regular intervals; record species and abundance
  (count or % cover); measure an abiotic factor at each quadrat; repeat transects. Shows a
  correlation, not proof of cause.
- **Abiotic:** light, temperature, water, O₂/CO₂, mineral ions, pH. **Biotic:** food, predation,
  competition (within and between species), disease. They change birth/death rates → size, and
  decide where organisms can survive and breed → distribution.
- **Predator–prey:** prey ↑ → predators ↑ → prey ↓ → predators ↓; predator peaks lag.
