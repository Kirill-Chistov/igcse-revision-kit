# Acids, bases and salt preparations — Summary

Specification points 2.34–2.43C · Topic 2 Inorganic chemistry. **2.40C, 2.41C, 2.43C are Paper 2
only.**

## Solubility rules

- Soluble: all Na⁺, K⁺, NH₄⁺ compounds; all nitrates; chlorides (not Ag, Pb); sulfates (not Ba,
  Ca, Pb).
- Insoluble: carbonates (except Na, K, NH₄); hydroxides (except Na, K; Ca slightly).

## Acids and bases

- Acid = **proton donor**; base = **proton acceptor** (proton = H⁺).
- Bases: metal oxides, metal hydroxides, ammonia. **Alkali = soluble base.**
- HCl → chlorides; H₂SO₄ → sulfates; HNO₃ → nitrates.
- acid + metal → salt + H₂ (not HNO₃); + base → salt + water; + carbonate → salt + water + CO₂;
  + ammonia → ammonium salt.

## Preparations

- **Soluble salt, insoluble reactant** (CuSO₄·5H₂O from CuO + H₂SO₄): warm acid; add CuO in
  **excess** (all acid used); **filter** (remove excess); heat to **crystallisation point** (not
  dryness); **cool**; filter, rinse, dry between filter papers.
- **Acid + alkali** (P2): titrate with indicator; repeat same volumes **without indicator**;
  crystallise.
- **Insoluble salt** (P2, PbSO₄): mix Pb(NO₃)₂(aq) + Na₂SO₄(aq); Pb²⁺ + SO₄²⁻ → PbSO₄(s);
  filter, **wash residue** with distilled water, dry.

## Common traps

- Excess of the **solid**, not the acid. Keep the **residue** in precipitation. Not all bases are
  alkalis.
