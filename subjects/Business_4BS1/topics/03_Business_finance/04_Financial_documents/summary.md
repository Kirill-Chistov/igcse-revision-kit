## Statement of comprehensive income (a period)

Revenue − cost of sales = **gross profit** − other operating expenses = **operating profit**.

- Used by managers (judge performance, spot rising costs, fund expansion), investors (invest?
  dividends?), banks (can a loan be repaid?).
- Profit: reward for risk; pays owners, becomes retained profit, attracts lenders/investors, needed
  to survive. High gross but low operating profit → expenses too high.

## Statement of financial position (one date)

- **Non-current assets** kept > 1 year; **current assets** (inventory, trade receivables, cash)
  turn into cash < 1 year.
- **Current liabilities** due < 1 year (trade payables, overdraft); **non-current liabilities** > 1 year (loans).
- Assets = liabilities + equity. **Capital employed** = equity + non-current liabilities
  (= total assets − current liabilities).
- Read it for: liquidity, how much is borrowed, change over time (growth, retained profit, inventory).
