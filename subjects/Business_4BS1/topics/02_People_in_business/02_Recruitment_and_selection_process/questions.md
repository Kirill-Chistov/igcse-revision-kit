## Marking guidance

*Written for this repository — not an official Pearson mark scheme.*

1. One full-time job divided between two (or more) part-time employees.
2. Any two: qualifications, skills, experience, personal qualities (essential/desirable).
3. D.
4. Any two: gender, race, disability, religion, sexual preference, age.
5. Identify (no new ideas / creates another vacancy / small pool) + two links, e.g. small pool →
   best person may be outside the business → lower-quality work.
6. Identify (only suitable applicants interviewed) → saves managers' time → lower recruitment
   cost / fairer, consistent selection.
7. Demand only high May–October → seasonal staff only paid when needed → lower wage costs in
   winter.
8. Higher wage costs → must raise prices or accept lower profit; or: better-paid staff more
   motivated → better service, lower labour turnover.
