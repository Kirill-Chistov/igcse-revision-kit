# Excretion — Summary

**Specification points 2.70–2.79B** · 2.72B–2.79B are Paper 2 only

- Excretion: removing waste products of metabolism. Faeces = egestion, not excretion.
- Plants: CO₂ (respiration) and O₂ (photosynthesis) diffuse out through stomata.
- Lungs: CO₂. Kidneys: urea, excess water and ions (urine). Skin: sweat — water, ions, a little urea.

## Kidney (P2)

- Excretion (urea from the liver) + osmoregulation (blood water content).
- Kidney → ureter → bladder (stores) → urethra. Renal artery in, renal vein out.
- Nephron: glomerulus in Bowman's capsule → proximal convoluted tubule → loop of Henle → distal convoluted tubule → collecting duct.
- Ultrafiltration: high pressure (wide arteriole in, narrow out); filtrate = water, glucose, urea, ions. No proteins, no cells.
- All glucose reabsorbed at proximal convoluted tubule (needed for respiration).
- Water reabsorbed by osmosis from collecting duct.
- Urine: water, urea, ions.
- ADH from pituitary: blood too concentrated → more ADH → ducts more permeable → more water reabsorbed → less, concentrated urine. Opposite if too dilute. Negative feedback.
