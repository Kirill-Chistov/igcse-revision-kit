   practical method used to carry it out (1).

   slum-upgrading programme; green belt / urban growth boundary; SDG 11 / UN New Urban Agenda. (1 each)
3. The authority provides plots of land (1) with basic services — water, sewer, electricity, a road (1);

4. Buses running in dedicated lanes (1) with raised stations and payment before boarding, so they move

5. A zone of protected land around a city where building is restricted (1).
6. Investment in irrigation, rural schools, clinics, roads or microcredit (1) reduces push factors (1),
   so fewer people need to migrate to the city (1).
7. Upgrading keeps residents' homes, jobs and community networks in place (1); it costs a fraction of
   rebuilding (1); legal land titles give residents security, so they invest in their homes (1);
   clearance destroys homes and livelihoods and relocated people often drift back to new informal
   sites (1).
8. Strength: low cost / builds skills and a sense of ownership (1) + explanation (1). Weakness: slow /

9. Building is restricted in the belt (1), so farmland and habitats are protected and land inside the
   city is reused (1). Problem: land and house prices rise inside (1) / building leaps beyond the belt,
   lengthening commutes (1).
10. Drivers need an alternative way to travel, otherwise the charge only makes journeys more expensive

11. It turns waste into income for poor residents (1); more waste is recycled, so less goes to landfill
