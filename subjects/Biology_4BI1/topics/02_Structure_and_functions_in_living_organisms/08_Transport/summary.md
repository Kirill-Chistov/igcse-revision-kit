# Transport — Summary

**Specification points 2.51–2.69** · 2.55B–2.58B, 2.63B, 2.64B are Paper 2 only

## Why

- Unicellular: large SA:V, short distances → diffusion is enough.
- Multicellular: small SA:V, long distances → need exchange surfaces + transport system.

## Plants

- Xylem: water + mineral ions, roots → leaves, upwards only.
- Phloem: sucrose + amino acids, leaves → growing tips, roots, storage; up and down.
- (P2) Root hair: large SA; water in by osmosis (sap more concentrated); ions by active transport.
- (P2) Transpiration = evaporation from plant surface; out through stomata; pulls water up xylem.
- (P2) Faster with: lower humidity, more wind, higher temperature, brighter light (stomata open).
- (P2) Potometer: cut shoot under water; bubble distance ÷ time = water uptake.

## Blood

- Red cells, white cells (phagocytes, lymphocytes), platelets, plasma.
- Plasma carries CO₂, digested food, urea, hormones, heat.
- Red cell: biconcave (large SA), no nucleus (more haemoglobin), haemoglobin → oxyhaemoglobin.
- Phagocytes ingest and digest pathogens; lymphocytes release specific antibodies.
- (P2) Vaccine → memory cells → antibodies sooner, faster, more.
- (P2) Platelets → fibrinogen to fibrin mesh → clot: stops blood loss and microbe entry.

## Heart and vessels

- Vena cava → RA → RV → pulmonary artery → lungs → pulmonary vein → LA → LV → aorta.
- Valves prevent backflow; LV wall thickest; septum separates sides; coronary arteries feed heart muscle.
- Exercise and adrenaline → faster heart rate → more O₂ and glucose to muscles.
- CHD risk: saturated fat/cholesterol, smoking, high blood pressure, stress, inactivity, obesity, genes, age, male.
- Artery: thick muscular elastic wall, narrow lumen, high pressure. Vein: thin wall, wide lumen, valves. Capillary: one cell thick.
- Liver: hepatic artery in, hepatic vein out, hepatic portal vein from gut. Kidneys: renal artery, renal vein.
- Double circulation: through heart twice per circuit.
