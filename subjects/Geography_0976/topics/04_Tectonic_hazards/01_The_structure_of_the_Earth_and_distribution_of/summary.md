## Layers (outside → in)

| Layer | Key facts |
| --- | --- |
| Crust | solid rock; oceanic 5–10 km (dense, sinks), continental 25–70 km (light, does not sink) |
| Lithosphere | crust + rigid top of mantle (≈100 km); broken into plates |
| Mantle | ≈2 900 km; hot rock, mostly solid but flows slowly; semi-molten near the top |
| Outer core | liquid iron and nickel, ≈4 500 °C |
| Inner core | solid iron and nickel, ≈5 500 °C — solid because of pressure |

## Plates and movement

- Main plates: Pacific, North American, South American, Eurasian, African, Indo-Australian,
  Antarctic; smaller: Nazca, Cocos, Caribbean, Philippine, Arabian, Juan de Fuca, Scotia.
- Move a few cm a year: **convection currents** (core heat → hot mantle rises, spreads, cools,
  sinks, dragging plates) + **ridge push** + **slab pull**.

## Boundaries

| Type | Movement | Example | EQ | Volcanoes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Divergent / constructive | apart | Mid-Atlantic Ridge, Iceland | small, shallow | gentle |
| Convergent / destructive | oceanic sinks | Nazca under South American | strong, shallow–deep | explosive |
| Convergent / collision | continental meet | Himalayas | strong, shallow | none |
| Conservative / transform | slide past | California; Haiti | strong, shallow | none |

## Distribution

Narrow belts along plate boundaries; **Pacific Ring of Fire** has most; lines along mid-ocean
ridges; collision and conservative zones: earthquakes only; exceptions over **hotspots** (Hawaii).

## Common traps

- Lithosphere ≠ crust. Inner core is solid. Only divergent and destructive boundaries have volcanoes.
