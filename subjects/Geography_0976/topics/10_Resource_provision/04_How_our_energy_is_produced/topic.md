## 1. Types of energy (10.4.1)

**Definition — Renewable energy:** energy from a source that is naturally replaced as it is used,
so it will not run out (a *flow* resource).

**Definition — Non-renewable energy:** energy from a finite *stock* that formed over millions of
years or is mined, so it will run out once used.

**Fuelwood** — wood (and charcoal made from it) burned for cooking and heating, the main energy
source for many rural households in LICs. It is **renewable** only if trees regrow or are replanted
as fast as they are cut; where cutting is faster, as around many growing settlements, it is
**non-renewable** and causes deforestation (→ 10.3.5).

::: traps
**Nuclear is non-renewable** — it is low-carbon, but uranium is a mined, finite stock.
**Biomass is renewable** only because the plants can be regrown.
:::
