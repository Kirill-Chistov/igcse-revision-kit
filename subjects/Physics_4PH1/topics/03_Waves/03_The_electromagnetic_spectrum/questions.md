## 1. Recall

1. List the EM spectrum in order of increasing frequency.
2. List the colours of visible light from longest to shortest wavelength.
3. What do all EM waves have in common in free space?

## 2. Understanding

4. Explain why microwaves are used to cook food.
5. Explain how an X-ray image of a broken arm is formed.
6. Explain why gamma rays can sterilise surgical instruments sealed in plastic bags.

## 3. Application

7. A radio signal and a gamma ray leave a distant star at the same time. Which arrives first?
   Explain.
8. Security cameras at night use infrared. Suggest why.
9. EM wave X has a longer wavelength than visible light but a shorter wavelength than
   microwaves. Name it and give one use.

## 4. Exam-style prompts

10. **[4 marks]** For ultraviolet radiation, *describe* one harmful effect of excessive exposure
    and *describe* two simple ways to reduce the risk.
11. **[3 marks]** *Explain* why gamma rays are more dangerous to the body than infrared.
12. **[2 marks]** *Explain* why the door of a microwave oven contains a metal mesh.

## Answers and marking guidance

*Not an official mark scheme.*

1. Radio, microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma.
2. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet.
3. The same speed (3.0 × 10⁸ m/s); all are transverse.
4. Microwaves are absorbed by water molecules in the food (1), which gain energy so the food heats
   up (1).
5. X-rays pass through soft tissue (1) but are absorbed by bone (1); the detector behind shows the
   bone as a shadow, revealing the break (1).
6. Gamma rays kill bacteria/microorganisms (1) and penetrate the packaging (1), so the
   instruments stay sterile until opened.
7. Both together (1): all EM waves travel at the same speed in free space (1).
8. Warm bodies emit more infrared than the cooler surroundings, so they show up in the dark.
9. Infrared; heaters or night vision equipment.
10. Damage to surface (skin) cells / skin cancer / blindness (1–2); sunscreen, cover skin, UV
    sunglasses or goggles, limit time in strong sun (1 each, max 2).
11. Gamma rays have a higher frequency so carry more energy (1); they penetrate deep into the
    body (1); they damage DNA, causing mutations / cancer, whereas infrared only heats the skin (1).
12. Microwaves cause internal heating of body tissue (1); the metal mesh stops them escaping (1).
