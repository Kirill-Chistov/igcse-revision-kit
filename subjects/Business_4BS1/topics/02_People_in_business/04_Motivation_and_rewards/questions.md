## Marking guidance

*Written for this repository — not an official Pearson mark scheme.*

1. Payment to an employee based on the value or number of sales they make.
2. Any two: pay, working conditions, company policies, supervision, relationships with
   colleagues, job security.
3. B.
4. Any one: staff discount, company car, health insurance, pension, free meals, gym membership.
5. Identify (lower labour turnover) → less spent on recruitment and training → lower costs;
   or experienced staff kept → higher productivity/quality.
6. Piece rate / close supervision / simple tasks → workers produce more to earn more → higher
   output and lower cost per unit.
7. Identify (no extra responsibility / training cost / output falls while learning) + two links.
8. Job enrichment / autonomy / job rotation, linked to the repetitive work (e.g. rotation adds
   variety → less boredom → fewer staff leave).
