# Solids, liquids and gases — units — Summary

Specification points 5.1–5.2P · Topic 5 · 5.2P is Paper 2 only.

## Units

- °C and **K** (temperature) · J (energy) · kg (mass) · **kg/m³** (density) · m, m², m³
- m/s (speed) · m/s² (acceleration; *g* = 10 m/s² = 10 N/kg) · N (force)
- **Pa** (pressure): 1 Pa = 1 N/m²
- **J/kg °C** (specific heat capacity) — Paper 2 only

## Conversions

- 1 kPa = 1000 Pa · 1 g = 0.001 kg
- cm² → m²: **÷ 10 000** · cm³ → m³: **÷ 1 000 000**
- 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³
- A change of 1 °C = a change of 1 K.

## Common traps

- Dividing cm² by 100 instead of 10 000.
- Mixing g with m³ in a density.
