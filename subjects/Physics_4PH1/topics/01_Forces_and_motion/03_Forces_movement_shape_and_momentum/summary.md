## Forces

- Forces change **speed**, **direction** or **shape**. Types: gravitational, electrostatic,
  magnetic, friction, air resistance, tension, normal contact, upthrust, thrust.
- **Friction opposes motion.** Air resistance rises with speed.
- **Scalar** = size only (distance, speed, mass, time, energy). **Vector** = size and direction
  (displacement, velocity, force, acceleration, momentum). Force is a vector: arrow length = size.
- Along a line: add same-direction forces, subtract opposite ones. Resultant 0 → at rest or
  constant velocity; resultant ≠ 0 → accelerates in its direction.
- **Mass** (kg, scalar, same everywhere) vs **weight** (N, force, depends on *g*).

## Stopping distance

**Stopping = thinking + braking distance.**

| Factor | Affects |
| --- | --- |
| speed | both (braking ×4 when speed ×2) |
| reaction time (tiredness, alcohol, drugs, distraction) | thinking |
| mass | braking (smaller deceleration) |
| road condition (wet, icy), worn tyres or brakes | braking (less friction) |

## Terminal velocity

Start: *R* = 0, resultant = *W*, accelerates → speed up, *R* up, resultant and acceleration down →
*R* = *W*, resultant 0 → **terminal velocity**. Parachute: area up → *R* > *W* → decelerates →
*R* falls to *W* → lower terminal velocity.

## Extension practical and Hooke's law

- Extension = new length − original length; add 100 g (1 N) steps; read pointer at eye level;
  long thin wire for metals; goggles.
- **Hooke's law:** extension ∝ force up to the limit of proportionality — the initial straight
  line through the origin. Spring and wire obey it at first; rubber band is curved.
- **Elastic:** recovers its original shape when the deforming forces are removed.

## Paper 2

- **Conservation of momentum:** total before = total after (no external force). Pick a positive
  direction; start from rest → total 0.
- **Safety features** (crumple zones, seat belts, airbags, helmets): increase the time of the
  collision → smaller rate of change of momentum → smaller force.
- **Newton's third law:** A on B = equal and opposite B on A; same type, different bodies.
- **Moments:** balanced → Σ clockwise = Σ anticlockwise about the pivot; weight acts at the
  **centre of gravity** (middle of a uniform beam); upward forces = downward forces.
- **Beam on two supports:** *R*A + *R*B = *W*; moments about one support; the nearer support
  takes more; middle → half each.

## Common traps

- "Weight = 60 kg" is wrong — weight is in N.
- Balanced ≠ stationary; terminal velocity = balanced but moving.
- Weight and the normal contact force on one object are **not** a third-law pair.
