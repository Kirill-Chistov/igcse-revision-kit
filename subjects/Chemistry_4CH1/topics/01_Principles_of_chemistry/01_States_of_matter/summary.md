# States of matter — Summary

Specification points 1.1–1.7C · Topic 1 Principles of chemistry. 1.5C–1.7C are **Paper 2 only**.

## Essential facts

- **Solid**: regular, touching, vibrate in fixed positions, least energy. **Liquid**: irregular,
  touching, slide past each other. **Gas**: far apart, random, fast, most energy.
- Heating → particles gain energy → overcome forces of attraction → **melting**, **boiling /
  evaporating**, **sublimation** (solid → gas). Cooling → **condensing**, **freezing**.
- Evaporation: surface only, below bp. Boiling: throughout the liquid, at bp.
- Dilution of KMnO₄: colour still seen after many dilutions → huge number of tiny particles.
- Diffusion: net movement from high to low concentration by random motion. NH₃/HCl ring forms
  nearer HCl: lighter NH₃ particles move faster.

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Solute / solvent | substance that dissolves / liquid it dissolves in |
| Solution | mixture of solute dissolved in solvent |
| Saturated solution | no more solute can dissolve at that temperature |
| Solubility (P2) | g of solute that dissolve in 100 g of solvent at a stated temperature |

## Paper 2: curves and practical

- Plot solubility (y) against temperature (x); smooth curve.
- Crystals formed on cooling = difference in solubility (per 100 g water); scale for other masses.
- Practical: saturate at set temperature → weigh basin, basin + solution, basin + dry solid (heat
  to constant mass) → solubility = solid ÷ water × 100.

## Common traps

- Particles do not expand or melt; their spacing and motion change.
- Solubility is per 100 g of **solvent**, not solution.
