# Elements, compounds and mixtures — Questions

Specification points 1.8–1.13 · Topic 1 Principles of chemistry. Marks in brackets.

> All questions and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository; they are not
> official Pearson questions or mark schemes.

## 1. Recall

1. State what is meant by a *compound*. (2)
2. Classify each as element, compound or mixture: carbon dioxide, sea water, oxygen, air. (2)
3. Name the technique used to obtain pure water from salt water. (1)
4. State the formula for calculating an Rf value. (1)

## 2. Understanding and explanation

5. Two samples of a solid are heated. Sample P melts at exactly 122 °C; sample Q melts between
   115 °C and 119 °C. Explain which sample is pure. (2)
6. Describe how to obtain dry crystals of copper(II) sulfate from copper(II) sulfate solution. (3)
7. Explain how fractional distillation separates a mixture of ethanol (bp 78 °C) and water
   (bp 100 °C). (4)
8. In the condenser, cold water enters at the bottom. Explain why the condenser is needed. (1)

## 3. Chromatography and the practical

9. Give two reasons why the baseline is drawn in pencil and placed above the solvent level. (2)
10. A dye spot moves 4.2 cm while the solvent front moves 6.0 cm. Calculate the Rf value. (2)
11. A food colouring gives three spots. Two match the heights of dyes E102 and E133 run on the same
    paper; one matches neither. What can you conclude? (3)
12. Describe how to carry out paper chromatography to find out whether a black ink is a mixture. (4)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. Two or more elements (1) chemically bonded / combined (in fixed proportions) (1).
2. Compound, mixture (1 for both); element, mixture (1 for both).
3. (Simple) distillation (1).
4. Rf = distance moved by substance ÷ distance moved by solvent (front) (1).
5. P is pure (1); a pure substance has a fixed / sharp melting point, a mixture melts over a range
   (1).
6. Heat / evaporate some of the water until the solution is saturated / crystals start to form (1);
   leave to cool so crystals form (1); filter off and dry the crystals (1).
7. Mixture heated; ethanol has the lower boiling point (1); ethanol vapour reaches the top of the
   column first / water condenses in the column and runs back (1); vapour condensed in the
   condenser and collected while the temperature stays at 78 °C (1); when the temperature rises,
   change the collecting vessel / water distils at 100 °C (1).
8. To cool / condense the vapour back into a liquid (1).
9. Pencil does not dissolve in the solvent / would not move up the paper (1); if the solvent were
   above the baseline the spots would dissolve into the solvent in the beaker (1).
10. 4.2 ÷ 6.0 (1) = 0.70 (1).
11. It is a mixture of (at least) three substances (1); it contains E102 and E133 (1); the third
    substance is neither of these / unknown (1).
12. Pencil baseline, spot of ink on it (1); paper in solvent with solvent below the baseline (1);
    remove when solvent near the top, dry (1); more than one spot → mixture (1).
