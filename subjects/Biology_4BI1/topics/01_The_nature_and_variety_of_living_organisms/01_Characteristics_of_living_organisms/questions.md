# Characteristics of living organisms — Questions

**Specification point 1.1** · Papers 1 and 2 (no B content)

Marking guidance below is written for this repository; it is not an official mark scheme.

## 1. Recall

1. List the eight characteristics shared by living organisms.
2. Define *excretion*.
3. What is released by respiration, and where in an organism does respiration happen?
4. Define *growth*.

## 2. Understanding

5. Explain why faeces are not an example of excretion.
6. Explain why an organism that stopped respiring could not move or grow.
7. Give one way a plant shows (a) movement, (b) response to its surroundings, (c) excretion.

## 3. Application

8. A student says: "A car is alive because it moves, takes in fuel, uses the fuel's energy and
   gives out waste gases." Evaluate this statement.
9. A dry bean seed is soaked in water overnight and gets bigger. Has it grown? Explain.

## 4. Exam-style

10. **[3 marks]** *Describe* how a plant carries out nutrition, excretion and respiration.
11. **[2 marks]** *Explain* the difference between breathing and respiration.

## Answers and marking guidance

1. Nutrition, respiration, excretion, response (to surroundings), movement, control of internal
   conditions, reproduction, growth and development.
2. Removal of the waste products of metabolism (chemical reactions in cells) from the organism.
3. Energy is released (not "made"); in every living cell.
4. A permanent increase in size and dry mass.
5. Faeces are mainly undigested food; it never entered the body's cells / was not made by
   metabolism, so its removal is egestion.
6. Respiration releases the energy from food (1); movement and growth both need that energy (1).
7. (a) e.g. flowers open and close / leaves turn; (b) e.g. shoots grow towards light;
   (c) carbon dioxide (from respiration) or oxygen (from photosynthesis) released from leaves.
8. The car shows some characteristics — movement, taking in "food", releasing energy, giving out
   waste (1). It does not show all eight: it cannot reproduce / grow and develop / respond to its
   surroundings by itself / control its internal conditions (1 for any two). Living organisms must
   show all eight, so the car is not alive (1).
9. No (1): the increase is due to water uptake; growth is a permanent increase in dry mass (1).
10. Nutrition: makes its own food by photosynthesis (1). Excretion: gets rid of waste products of
    metabolism, e.g. carbon dioxide / oxygen, through the leaves (1). Respiration: every cell
    releases energy from glucose, all the time / day and night (1).
11. Breathing moves air into and out of the lungs (1); respiration is chemical reactions in cells
    that release energy from food (1).
