# Magnetism and electromagnetism — units — Questions

Specification point 6.1 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1.

> These questions and the marking guidance are **written for this repository by AI**. They are not
> official Pearson questions and the guidance is not an official mark scheme. No 4PH1 past papers
> are stored in the repository yet.

1. Give the unit of: (a) the current in a motor coil; (b) the voltage induced in a generator; (c) the power output of a transformer. (3)
2. Convert: (a) 11 kV to V; (b) 250 mA to A; (c) 1.5 MW to W. (3)
3. A transformer's secondary coil gives 12 V and a current of 2.5 A. Calculate the output power and give the unit. (2)
4. A power line carries 50 MW at 400 kV. Calculate the current in the line. (3)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. (a) ampere, A (1); (b) volt, V (1); (c) watt, W (1).
2. (a) 11 000 V (1); (b) 0.25 A (1); (c) 1 500 000 W (1).
3. *P* = *V* × *I* = 12 × 2.5 (1) = 30 W (1).
4. 50 MW = 50 000 000 W and 400 kV = 400 000 V (1); *I* = *P* ÷ *V* = 50 000 000 ÷ 400 000 (1) = 125 A (1).
