# Group 7 (halogens) — Summary

Specification points 2.5–2.8C · Topic 2 Inorganic chemistry. **2.8C is Paper 2 only.**

## Essential facts

| Halogen | State | Colour | In water |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cl₂ | gas | pale yellow-green | very pale green |
| Br₂ | liquid | red-brown | orange |
| I₂ | solid | grey-black (purple vapour) | brown |

- Down the group: colour **darker**; m.p./b.p. **increase** (bigger molecules → stronger
  intermolecular forces); **reactivity decreases**.
- Predict: fluorine — pale yellow gas, most reactive; astatine — black solid, least reactive.

## Displacement

- More reactive halogen displaces less reactive one from its halide solution.
- Cl₂ + KBr → orange; Cl₂ or Br₂ + KI → brown; I₂ displaces nothing → **Cl > Br > I**.
- Cl₂ + 2Br⁻ → 2Cl⁻ + Br₂  Br₂ + 2I⁻ → 2Br⁻ + I₂. Halogen reduced (oxidising agent); halide
  oxidised; K⁺ spectator.

## Why reactivity decreases (Paper 2)

Gain **one** electron → X⁻. More shells → outer shell further, more shielded → **weaker attraction
for an incoming electron** → gained **less easily**.

## Common traps

- Iodine solid is grey-black, not purple. Chlorine (Cl₂) ≠ chloride (Cl⁻).
