# Movement and position — Summary

Specification points 1.3–1.10 · Topic 1 Forces and motion · both papers.

## Equations

| Equation | Units | In the exam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| average speed = distance moved ÷ time taken | m/s, m, s | recall |
| *a* = (*v* − *u*) / *t* | m/s², m/s, s | recall |
| *v*² = *u*² + (2 × *a* × *s*) | m/s, m/s², m | given |

Acceleration = rate of change of velocity. Negative acceleration = deceleration.

## Graphs

| | Distance–time | Velocity–time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gradient | speed | acceleration |
| Horizontal line | stationary | constant velocity |
| Straight sloping line | constant speed | constant acceleration (or deceleration if down) |
| Curve | speed changing | acceleration changing |
| Area under line | — | distance travelled (rectangles + ½ × base × height triangles; count squares for curves) |

**Plotting:** time on *x*; axes labelled with quantity / unit; even scale filling over half the
grid; accurate crosses; one line of best fit.

## Practical (1.5)

- Toy car: distance (metre rule) ÷ time (stopwatch) → speed; repeat and average.
- Stopwatch reaction time ≈ 0.2 s → use longer distances or **light gates** (speed = card length ÷
  time beam blocked; *a* = change in speed between gates ÷ time between gates).
- Tennis ball: video beside a metre rule, frame by frame; time between frames = 1 ÷ frame rate.
- Change one factor only (e.g. ramp height); keep car, release point and distance the same.

## Common traps

- Check the axis before reading a horizontal line.
- *v*² = *u*² + 2*as* gives *v*² — square-root it.
- Convert km, minutes and km/h to m, s and m/s first.
