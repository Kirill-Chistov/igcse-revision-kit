# Acids, alkalis and titrations — Summary

Specification points 2.28–2.33C · Topic 2 Inorganic chemistry. **2.33C is Paper 2 only.**

## Essential facts

| Indicator | Acid | Alkali |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Litmus | red | blue |
| Phenolphthalein | colourless | pink |
| Methyl orange | red | yellow |

- pH: **0–3 strongly acidic, 4–6 weakly acidic, 7 neutral, 8–10 weakly alkaline, 11–14 strongly
  alkaline**.
- Universal indicator: add, compare colour with chart → approximate pH.
- Acids → **H⁺(aq)**; alkalis → **OH⁻(aq)**. Alkalis neutralise acids: **H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O**.

## Titration (Paper 2)

Pipette 25.0 cm³ alkali → conical flask; few drops phenolphthalein / methyl orange (not UI); white
tile; acid in burette, initial reading (eye level, meniscus); add with swirling, dropwise near
end-point, until colour **just** changes permanently; final reading; titre = final − initial;
repeat → **concordant** (within 0.10 cm³), mean.

## Common traps

- Phenolphthalein colourless in acid. 8–10 is **weakly** alkaline. No UI in titrations.
