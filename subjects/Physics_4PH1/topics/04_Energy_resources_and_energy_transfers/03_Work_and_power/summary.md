| KE = ½ × *m* × *v*² | J, kg, m/s | recall |
| *P* = *W* / *t* | W, J, s | given |

## Essential facts

- Power = rate of transfer of energy = rate of doing work; 1 W = 1 J/s.
- No resistance: GPE lost = KE gained → *v* = √(2*gh*), independent of mass.
- With resistance: GPE lost = KE gained + work done against resistance (→ thermal store).
- Braking: ½*mv*² = braking force × braking distance.
- Doubling speed → KE × 4. *v* = √(2KE ÷ *m*).

## Common traps

- Ramp length used for *h*; horizontal distance used when lifting.
- Squaring ½*mv* instead of *v* only; forgetting the square root.
- Mass in g, time in minutes.
