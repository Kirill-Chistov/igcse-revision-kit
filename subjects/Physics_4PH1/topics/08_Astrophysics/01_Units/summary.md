# Units — Summary

Specification point 8.1 · Topic 8 Astrophysics · both papers.

- mass → **kg** · orbital radius, distance → **m** · orbital speed, galaxy velocity → **m/s** ·
  acceleration → **m/s²** · gravitational force, weight → **N** · time period → **s** ·
  gravitational field strength → **N/kg** (1 N/kg = 1 m/s²).
- Convert before substituting: km × 10³ → m · km/s × 10³ → m/s · h × 3600 → s ·
  day × 86 400 → s · year ≈ 3.15 × 10⁷ s.
- *g* is in N/kg, weight in N.
