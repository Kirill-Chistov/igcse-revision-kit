# Synthetic polymers — Summary

Specification points 4.44–4.50C · Topic 4 Organic chemistry. 4.48C–4.50C Paper 2 only.

## Essential facts

- **Addition polymer**: many small molecules (monomers) joined up. Monomers are alkenes; C=C opens;
  polymer is the only product.
- Poly(ethene) from ethene · poly(propene) from propene · poly(chloroethene) from chloroethene ·
  poly(tetrafluoroethene) from tetrafluoroethene.
- Monomer → repeat unit: C=C → C–C, bonds out through brackets, n.
- Repeat unit → monomer: two chain carbons, put back C=C.
- Disposal: inert, non-biodegradable (landfill, litter); burning gives toxic gases (CO, HCl from
  poly(chloroethene)).
- **Condensation polymerisation** (P2): dicarboxylic acid + diol → polyester + water.
  n HOOC–COOH + n HOCH₂CH₂OH → –(OC–COO–CH₂CH₂–O)ₙ– + 2n H₂O.
- **Biopolyesters** (P2): biodegradable polyesters.

## Common traps

- Repeat unit has no C=C; only two carbons in the chain.
- Condensation releases 2n H₂O.
