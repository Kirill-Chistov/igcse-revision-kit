# Characteristics of living organisms — Summary

**Specification point 1.1** · Papers 1 and 2 (no B content)

## Essentials

- All living organisms show **all eight**: nutrition, respiration, excretion, response, movement,
  control of internal conditions, reproduction, growth and development (**MRS C GREN**).
- Nutrition → food → **respiration releases energy** → used for movement, growth, reproduction,
  response, control. Metabolism makes waste → **excretion**.
- Non-living things may show one or two (a car moves; a crystal grows) — never all eight.
  Viruses show none outside a living cell.

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Respiration | chemical reactions in every living cell that release energy from food |
| Excretion | removal of the waste products of metabolism |
| Growth | permanent increase in size and dry mass |
| Development | increase in complexity as cells become specialised |

## Plants do it too

Photosynthesis (nutrition) · respire day and night · excrete CO₂ and O₂ · shoots grow towards
light (response) · flowers open and close (movement) · stomata control water content.

## Traps

- Breathing ≠ respiration. Energy is **released**, not made.
- Faeces are **egested**, not excreted.
- Taking up water is not growth — growth needs a permanent increase in **dry** mass.
