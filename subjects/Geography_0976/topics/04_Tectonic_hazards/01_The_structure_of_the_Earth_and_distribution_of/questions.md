## Questions

1. **[2]** *Define* the lithosphere.
2. **[4]** *Describe* the characteristics of the inner core and the outer core.
3. **[3]** *Compare* oceanic crust with continental crust.
4. **[4]** *Explain* how convection currents cause tectonic plates to move.
5. **[2]** *Identify* the plate boundary type at which two continental plates meet, and give an
   example.
6. **[3]** Using a map of plate boundaries, *identify* the plates on either side of the
   Mid-Atlantic Ridge and state the direction in which they move.
7. **[4]** *Describe* the global distribution of volcanoes.
8. **[3]** *Explain* why there are earthquakes but no volcanoes at conservative plate boundaries.
9. **[2]** *Give* two differences between a divergent and a convergent/destructive boundary.

## Marking guidance

1. Crust + rigid upper part of the mantle (1); broken into tectonic plates (1).
2. Inner: solid (1), iron and nickel (1), ≈5 500 °C / hottest, solid due to pressure (1).
   Outer: liquid (1), iron and nickel, ≈4 500 °C. Max 4.
3. Oceanic thinner (5–10 km v 25–70 km) (1); denser / basalt v granite (1); oceanic sinks and is
   destroyed at convergent boundaries, continental does not (1).
4. Core heats base of mantle (1); hot rock less dense, rises (1); spreads sideways under plates,
   cools and sinks (1); currents drag plates along, a few cm a year (1). Credit ridge push / slab
   pull (1).
5. Convergent/collision (1); Indo-Australian and Eurasian plates / Himalayas (1).
6. North American (1) and Eurasian (1); moving apart (1).
7. Along plate boundaries / in narrow belts (1); around the Pacific — Ring of Fire (1); along
   mid-ocean ridges, e.g. Mid-Atlantic Ridge / Iceland (1); none at collision zones, e.g.
   Himalayas (1); some away from boundaries over hotspots, e.g. Hawaii (1). Max 4.
8. Plates slide past; friction locks them, pressure builds and is released suddenly → earthquake (1–2);
   no crust is destroyed / no subduction, so no magma forms (1).
9. Divergent: plates move apart, destructive: together (1); divergent creates crust / gentle
   eruptions, destructive destroys crust / explosive eruptions / deep earthquakes (1).
