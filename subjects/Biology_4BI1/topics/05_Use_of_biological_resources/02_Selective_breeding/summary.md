# Selective breeding — Summary

**Specification points 5.10–5.11**

- Choose parents with the desired characteristic → breed (plants: hand cross-pollinate; animals:
  mate) → select offspring that show it most → breed those → repeat for many generations.
- Works because the characteristic is genetic: its alleles become more common each generation.
- Plants: high yield, short stems, disease resistance, drought tolerance, taste.
  Animals: milk, meat, eggs, wool, disease resistance, calm temperament.
- Drawbacks: less genetic variation (vulnerable to new disease), inbreeding problems, slow.
