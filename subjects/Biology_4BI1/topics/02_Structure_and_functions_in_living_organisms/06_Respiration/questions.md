# Respiration — Questions

**Specification points 2.34–2.39** · Papers 1 and 2

Marking guidance below is written for this repository; it is not an official mark scheme.

## Questions

1. Give two uses of ATP in a cell.
2. Write the balanced symbol equation for aerobic respiration.
3. Write the word equation for anaerobic respiration in (a) a muscle cell, (b) a yeast cell.
4. State three differences between aerobic and anaerobic respiration.
5. Explain why a sprinter's muscles respire anaerobically during a race.
6. In the vacuum-flask experiment, explain why (a) vacuum flasks are used, (b) the control seeds
   are boiled, (c) the control seeds are washed in disinfectant.
7. Predict the colour of hydrogencarbonate indicator after 2 hours above germinating seeds, and
   explain.
8. **[4 marks]** *Describe* an experiment to show that germinating peas release carbon dioxide.
9. **[3 marks]** Muscle cells contain many mitochondria. *Explain* why.

## Answers and marking guidance

1. Any two: muscle contraction; active transport; making proteins / large molecules; cell
   division; nerve impulses; keeping warm.
2. C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O.
3. (a) glucose → lactic acid; (b) glucose → ethanol + carbon dioxide.
4. Any three: aerobic needs oxygen, anaerobic does not; aerobic releases much more energy / ATP;
   aerobic products CO₂ + water, anaerobic lactic acid (or ethanol + CO₂); glucose completely vs
   incompletely broken down; aerobic mainly in mitochondria, anaerobic in cytoplasm.
5. Oxygen cannot be supplied to the muscles fast enough (1) for aerobic respiration to release all
   the energy needed (1).
6. (a) Insulate / reduce heat loss, so temperature rise can be detected (1). (b) To kill them so they
   cannot respire (1). (c) To kill microorganisms, whose respiration would release heat (1).
7. Yellow (1); the seeds respire and release carbon dioxide (1).
8. Place germinating peas on gauze in a tube above hydrogencarbonate indicator (or limewater)
   and seal (1); set up an identical tube with boiled peas as a control (1); leave for a set time at
   the same temperature (1); indicator turns yellow (limewater cloudy) only with live peas (1).
9. Mitochondria are the site of aerobic respiration (1); muscles need a lot of energy / ATP (1) for
   contraction (1).
