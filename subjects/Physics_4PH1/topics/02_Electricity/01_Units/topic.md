## 1. The units (2.1)

| Quantity | Symbol | Unit | Unit symbol | Link to other units |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| current | *I* | ampere (amp) | A | 1 A = 1 C/s |
| charge | *Q* | coulomb | C | |
| voltage (potential difference) | *V* | volt | V | 1 V = 1 J/C |
| resistance | *R* | ohm | Ω | 1 Ω = 1 V/A |
| energy transferred | *E* | joule | J | |
| power | *P* | watt | W | 1 W = 1 J/s |
| time | *t* | second | s | |

The letter V is both a quantity and a unit: *V* = 12 V means "the voltage is 12 volts".

## 2. Prefixes and time

```
m (milli) = ÷ 1000        k (kilo) = × 1000        M (mega) = × 1 000 000
1 min = 60 s              1 h = 60 min = 3600 s
```

Every equation in this topic needs A, C, V, Ω, J, W and **seconds**, so convert before substituting.

::: note | Examples
250 mA = 0.25 A · 2.2 kW = 2200 W · 4.7 kΩ = 4700 Ω · 3.6 MJ = 3 600 000 J · 5 min = 300 s · 2 h = 7200 s
:::

::: traps
| Wrong | Right |
| --- | --- |
| Substituting 5 min, 2.2 kW or 250 mA straight into an equation. | Convert first: 300 s, 2200 W, 0.25 A. |
| Using C for current. | *I* is current; C (coulomb) is the unit of **charge**. |
:::
