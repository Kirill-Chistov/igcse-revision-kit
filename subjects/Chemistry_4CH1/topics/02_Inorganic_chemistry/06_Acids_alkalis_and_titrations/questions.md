# Acids, alkalis and titrations — Questions

Specification points 2.28–2.33C · Topic 2 Inorganic chemistry. **Q8 is Paper 2 only.** Marks in
brackets.

> All questions and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository; they are not
> official Pearson questions or mark schemes.

## 1. Recall

1. Give the colour of (a) phenolphthalein in alkali; (b) methyl orange in acid; (c) litmus in
   alkali. (3)
2. A solution has pH 9. Classify it. (1)
3. Describe how to find the approximate pH of a solution using universal indicator. (2)
4. Which ion do all acids release in aqueous solution? Which ion do alkalis release? (2)
5. Write the ionic equation for neutralisation. (1)

## 2. Application

6. Solution P has pH 2, Q pH 7, R pH 13. Which is strongly alkaline, and what colour is universal
   indicator in Q? (2)
7. Write the equation for the reaction of potassium hydroxide with nitric acid. (1)

## 3. Paper 2

8. Describe how to carry out a titration to find the volume of dilute hydrochloric acid needed to
   neutralise 25.0 cm³ of sodium hydroxide solution. (6)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. (a) pink (1); (b) red (1); (c) blue (1).
2. Weakly alkaline (1).
3. Add a few drops of universal indicator / use UI paper (1); compare the colour with a pH chart (1).
4. H⁺ (1); OH⁻ (1).
5. H⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → H₂O(l) (1).
6. R (1); green (1).
7. KOH + HNO₃ → KNO₃ + H₂O (1).
8. Pipette 25.0 cm³ NaOH into a conical flask (1); add a few drops of phenolphthalein / methyl
   orange (1); acid in burette, record initial reading (1); add acid with swirling, dropwise near
   the end-point (1); stop when the indicator just changes colour, record final reading (1);
   repeat until concordant titres and find the mean (1).
