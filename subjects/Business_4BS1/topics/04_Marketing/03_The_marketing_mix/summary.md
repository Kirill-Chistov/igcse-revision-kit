# The marketing mix — Summary

Specification points 4.3.1–4.3.4 · both papers (apply to a small or a large business)

**Marketing mix** = product, price, place, promotion (4Ps).

## Product (4.3.1)

- New product stages: idea generation → screening → development (prototype) → test marketing
  → launch. + new revenue, competitive advantage, spreads risk; − costly, many fail, slow.
- **Goods** tangible, storable. **Services** intangible, not storable, quality varies.
- Packaging: protects, informs, attracts/brands, convenient; − cost, environmental image.
- **Life cycle** (by sales): development → introduction → growth → maturity → decline.
- **Extension strategies** (in maturity): update product, new markets, new uses, repackage or
  rebrand, new advertising, lower price/offers. + cheaper than a new product; − may only delay decline.
- **Boston matrix** (market share × market growth): star (invest), question mark (invest
  selectively or drop), cash cow (milk to fund others), dog (withdraw). Aim for a balanced
  portfolio. − only two factors, a snapshot.

## Price (4.3.2)

- Cost plus: cost + mark-up → simple, covers cost; ignores rivals.
- Penetration: low then raised → market share fast; low profit at first.
- Competition: at/near rivals → keeps customers; may not cover costs.
- Skimming: high then lowered, new/unique product → high profit per unit; attracts rivals.
- Promotional: short-term cut → quick sales; lower profit, cheapens brand.
- Selling price = cost × (1 + mark-up% ÷ 100). New price = original × (1 − discount% ÷ 100).
  2 d.p., keep the currency.

## Place (4.3.3)

- **Distribution channel** = route from producer to consumer.
- **Retailer** (shop): see/try, advice, impulse buys; − rent, local, takes a share of price.
- **E-tailer** (e-commerce): global 24/7, lower costs, customer data; − returns, delivery,
  website costs, price comparison.

## Promotion (4.3.4)

- Match method and media to the segment.
- Advertising (mass reach, costly) · sponsorship (image, risk if sponsored party misbehaves) ·
  product trials (first purchase, cost of free goods) · special offers (quick boost, lower profit).
- **ATL** = paid mass media, wide reach, costly. **BTL** = not mass media, targeted, cheaper,
  fewer people.
- **PR** = building a good public image: cheap, credible; no control over media.
- Technology: targeted online ads (little waste, privacy) · viral via social media (cheap,
  uncontrollable) · e-newsletters (cheap, loyalty, often ignored).
- **Brand** = name/logo/design that differentiates → recognition, loyalty, premium price;
  − costly to build, one scandal hits all products.

## Common traps

- Life cycle = sales, not profit. Boston matrix = share and growth.
- Skimming/penetration = launch strategies; promotional = temporary cut.
