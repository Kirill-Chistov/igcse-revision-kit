# Group 1 (alkali metals) — Summary

Specification points 2.1–2.4C · Topic 2 Inorganic chemistry. **2.4C is Paper 2 only.**

## Essential facts

- With water, all: float, fizz, move, disappear → **metal hydroxide (alkaline) + hydrogen**.
  2M + 2H₂O → 2MOH + H₂. Same reaction = evidence of a **family**.
- Vigour increases Li < Na < K: Li fizzes steadily; Na melts into a ball; K melts, **lilac** flame.
- In air: shiny cut surface **tarnishes** (oxide, 4Na + O₂ → 2Na₂O) — faster down the group.
  Stored under oil.
- Down the group: **reactivity ↑**, **melting point ↓**, softer.
- Predict Rb, Cs: even more violent (explosive), RbOH / Rb₂O / Rb⁺, lower m.p.

## Why reactivity increases (Paper 2)

Lose **one** outer electron → M⁺. More shells → outer electron **further** from nucleus, **more
shielded** → **weaker attraction** → lost **more easily**.

## Common traps

- Product with water is the **hydroxide**, not the oxide.
- Not "more electrons to lose" and not "stronger nuclear attraction".
