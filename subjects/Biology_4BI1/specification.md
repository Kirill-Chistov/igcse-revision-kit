## Qualification at a glance

| Paper | Code | Duration | Marks | Weighting | Content assessed |

| Biology Paper 1 | 4BI1/1B | 2 h | 110 | 61.1% | Core content only — statements **without** a `B` reference |
| Biology Paper 2 | 4BI1/2B | 1 h 15 min | 70 | 38.9% | **All** content, including `B` statements |

Both papers are externally assessed, available in November and June, and may draw questions
from any topic area. A calculator may be used.

## Assessment objectives

| AO | Description | Weighting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AO1 | Knowledge and understanding of biology | 38–42% |
| AO2 | Application of knowledge and understanding, analysis and evaluation of biology | 38–42% |
| AO3 | Experimental skills, analysis and evaluation of data and methods in biology | 19–21% |

## Course distinctions that affect what can be assessed

- **`B` statements are Paper 2 only.** They cover some sub-topics in greater depth. A student
  sitting Paper 1 is never assessed on them; a student sitting Paper 2 is assessed on everything.
- There is **no tiering** — all students sit the same two papers.
- Points beginning *practical:* are the **required practical work**; experimental method,
  apparatus, variables, results and evaluation are assessable (AO3) and may be examined on
  either paper according to the same `B` rule.
- Appendix: maths skills required (arithmetic, handling data, algebra, graphs, geometry) are
  assessed through biology contexts.
