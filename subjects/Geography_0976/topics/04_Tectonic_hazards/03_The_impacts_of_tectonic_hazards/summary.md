## Why people live at risk (4.3.1)

- **Offers:** fertile volcanic soil, geothermal energy, minerals, tourism, jobs in big cities.
- **Stay / can't leave:** poverty, family ties, low perceived risk (rare events), confidence in
  monitoring and safe buildings.

## Impacts (4.3.2, 4.3.3)

- **Primary** = caused directly by the hazard. **Secondary** = caused by the primary impacts.
- **Earthquakes:** collapse → deaths, injuries, trapped; broken pipes and cables → fires, disease;
  homelessness; aid delayed; landslides, tsunami; lost jobs, rebuilding costs.
- **Volcanoes:** deaths (pyroclastic flows), homes and crops buried; evacuation, ash health
  problems, flights cancelled; lahars, acid rain, global cooling. **Positives:** fertile soil,
  tourism, geothermal, minerals, new land.
- Categorise: social / economic / environmental; local → global.

## Why impacts vary

Magnitude/VEI · depth of focus · distance · population density · building quality · **level of
development** (LICs: more deaths; HICs: higher economic cost) · time of day · preparation.

## Measuring (4.3.4)

| Scale | Measures | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Richter | wave amplitude (magnitude) | logarithmic |
| Moment magnitude | total energy released (magnitude) | logarithmic; standard for big quakes |
| Mercalli | intensity: effects at a place | I–XII |
| VEI | explosivity: volume erupted, column height | 0–8 |

+1 magnitude = 10× bigger waves, ≈32× more energy.

## Common traps

- Magnitude = one value per quake; intensity varies by place. Scales are logarithmic.
