# Reversible reactions and equilibria — Summary

Specification points 3.17–3.22C · Topic 3 Physical chemistry. 3.19C–3.22C are **Paper 2 only**.

## Essential facts

- Reversible reaction: goes both ways; symbol **⇌**.
- CuSO₄·5H₂O (blue) ⇌ CuSO₄ (white) + 5H₂O: heat → white, steam off (endothermic); add water →
  blue, gets hot (exothermic).
- NH₄Cl(s) ⇌ NH₃(g) + HCl(g): decomposes at the hot end, white solid re-forms at the cool end.
- Endothermic one way = exothermic the other way, same amount.

## Equilibrium (P2)

- Sealed container → **dynamic equilibrium**: forward rate = reverse rate; concentrations
  **constant** (not equal).
- Catalyst: speeds up forward and reverse equally → equilibrium faster, **position unchanged**.
- Temperature ↑ → endothermic direction; ↓ → exothermic direction.
- Pressure ↑ → fewer moles of **gas**; ↓ → more moles of gas; equal gas moles → no effect.

## Common traps

- ΔH is for the forward reaction. Reactions don't stop at equilibrium.
