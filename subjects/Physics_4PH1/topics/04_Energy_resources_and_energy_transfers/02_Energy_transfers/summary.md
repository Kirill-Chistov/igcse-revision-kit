## Essential facts

- **Stores:** chemical, kinetic, gravitational, elastic, thermal, magnetic, electrostatic, nuclear.
- **Transfers:** mechanically, electrically, by heating, by radiation (light and sound).
- Describe = start store → transfer → end store, plus the wasted part.
- **Conservation:** energy is never created or destroyed; total in = useful out + wasted out.
  Waste is dissipated, mostly to the thermal store of the surroundings.
- **Sankey:** width ∝ energy; useful straight on, waste bends away; outputs add up to input.
- **Conduction** (solids): vibrating particles pass energy on; metals best (free electrons).
- **Convection** (fluids only): heated fluid expands → less dense → rises; cooler, denser fluid
  sinks → convection current.
- **Radiation:** infrared waves; no medium needed, crosses a vacuum.
- Convection examples: radiator heats a room; kettle element at the bottom; fridge cooling unit at
  the top; sea breeze by day.
- Hotter object → emits more per second. Dark matt = best emitter and absorber; light shiny = poorest
  (best reflector). Emits = absorbs → constant temperature.
- Reduce transfer: trapped air (conduction + convection), lids/stoppers (convection), shiny
  surfaces (radiation), vacuum (conduction + convection). Lubrication cuts friction heating.

## Practical (4.9)

- Conduction: rods, wax + pins, time to fall → copper fastest.
- Convection: potassium manganate(VII) crystal in heated water → coloured current.
- Radiation: Leslie cube + infrared detector at a fixed distance → matt black highest.

## Common traps

- "Heat rises" → hot **fluid** rises (less dense). Particles don't expand.
- Shiny surfaces are poor emitters **and** poor absorbers.
- Energy is transferred, never "used up".
