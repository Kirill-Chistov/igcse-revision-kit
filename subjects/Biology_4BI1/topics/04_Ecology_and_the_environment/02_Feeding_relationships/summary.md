# Feeding relationships — Summary

Specification points 4.6–4.9.

- **Levels:** producer (photosynthesis) → primary consumer (eats producers) → secondary → tertiary.
  Decomposers (bacteria, fungi) feed on dead matter and waste from every level.
- **Food chain:** energy transfer by feeding, from a producer; arrow points to the eater.
  **Food web:** interlinked chains; one animal can be at more than one level. Predict changes by
  following each arrow, with a reason.
- **Pyramids:** numbers (any shape — ignores size); biomass (dry mass per m², usually a pyramid);
  energy transfer (kJ/m²/year, always a pyramid).
- **Energy** enters by photosynthesis, passes as chemical energy in food, flows one way, ends as
  heat. **Substances** pass on in food and are recycled by decomposers.
- **≈10% passed on** because: not all eaten; faeces (egestion); excretion (urea); most used in
  respiration → movement, warmth → heat. So ≤ 4–5 levels.
- **Efficiency** = energy in next level ÷ energy in level below × 100.
