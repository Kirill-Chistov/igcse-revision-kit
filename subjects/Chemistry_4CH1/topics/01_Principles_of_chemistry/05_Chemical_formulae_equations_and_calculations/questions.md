# Chemical formulae, equations and calculations — Questions

Specification points 1.25–1.36 · Topic 1 Principles of chemistry. Questions 15–19 are
**Paper 2 only**. Marks in brackets. Ar: H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, Na 23, Mg 24, S 32, Cl 35.5,
Ca 40, Fe 56, Cu 63.5.

> All questions and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository; they are not
> official Pearson questions or mark schemes.

## 1. Equations

1. Balance: Na + H₂O → NaOH + H₂. (1)
2. Balance: Fe₂O₃ + CO → Fe + CO₂. (1)
3. Solid magnesium carbonate reacts with dilute hydrochloric acid to form magnesium chloride
   solution, water and carbon dioxide. Write the balanced equation with state symbols. (3)

## 2. Mr, moles, reacting masses, yield

4. Calculate the Mr of (NH₄)₂SO₄. (1)
5. Calculate the amount, in moles, in 8.8 g of carbon dioxide. (2)
6. What mass of iron is produced from 32.0 g of iron(III) oxide? Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂ (3)
7. In question 6 only 17.9 g of iron was obtained. Calculate the percentage yield. (2)
8. Give two reasons why the actual yield of a reaction is less than the theoretical yield. (2)

## 3. Formulae

9. Define *empirical formula*. (1)
10. A compound contains 2.4 g of carbon and 0.6 g of hydrogen. Its Mr is 30. Find its empirical
    and molecular formulae. (4)
11. 1.27 g of copper combines with oxygen to form 1.59 g of copper oxide. Find the empirical
    formula of the oxide. (3)
12. 4.92 g of hydrated magnesium sulfate, MgSO₄·xH₂O, is heated to constant mass, leaving 2.40 g of
    anhydrous MgSO₄. Find x. (4)

## 4. The practical

13. Describe how to find the formula of magnesium oxide by burning magnesium. Include how you make
    sure the reaction is complete. (5)
14. In the reduction of copper(II) oxide by hydrogen, explain why (a) hydrogen is passed through
    the tube before heating and (b) the hydrogen is kept flowing while the copper cools. (2)

## 5. Paper 2 — solutions and gases

15. Calculate the concentration, in mol/dm³, of a solution containing 0.050 mol in 200 cm³. (2)
16. How many moles of hydrochloric acid are in 20.0 cm³ of 0.500 mol/dm³ acid? (1)
17. 5.85 g of sodium chloride is dissolved to make 500 cm³ of solution. Calculate the
    concentration in mol/dm³. (3)
18. Calculate the volume, in cm³ at rtp, of 0.020 mol of oxygen. (1)
19. Calculate the volume of hydrogen, in dm³ at rtp, produced when 4.8 g of magnesium reacts with
    excess acid. Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂ (3)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. 2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂ (1).
2. Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂ (1).
3. Correct formulae MgCO₃ + HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂ (1); balanced with 2HCl (1); states (s), (aq),
   (aq), (l), (g) (1).
4. 2 × (14 + 4) + 32 + 64 = 132 (1).
5. Mr CO₂ = 44 (1); 8.8 ÷ 44 = 0.20 mol (1).
6. Mr Fe₂O₃ = 160; 32.0 ÷ 160 = 0.200 mol (1); × 2 = 0.400 mol Fe (1); × 56 = 22.4 g (1).
7. 17.9 ÷ 22.4 × 100 (1) = 79.9% (1).
8. Any two: reaction incomplete / reversible (1); product lost during separation / transfer (1);
   side reactions / other products (1).
9. Simplest whole-number ratio of atoms of each element in a compound (1).
10. C 2.4 ÷ 12 = 0.2; H 0.6 ÷ 1 = 0.6 (1); ratio 1 : 3 → CH₃ (1); CH₃ = 15, 30 ÷ 15 = 2 (1);
    C₂H₆ (1).
11. O = 0.32 g (1); Cu 1.27 ÷ 63.5 = 0.020, O 0.32 ÷ 16 = 0.020 (1); CuO (1).
12. Water = 2.52 g (1); MgSO₄ Mr 120, 2.40 ÷ 120 = 0.020 mol (1); H₂O 2.52 ÷ 18 = 0.14 mol (1);
    x = 7 (1).
13. Weigh crucible (+ lid) empty and with magnesium (1); heat strongly (1); lift lid occasionally to
    let in air / oxygen, without letting product escape (1); cool and weigh (1); reheat and reweigh
    until the mass is constant (1).
14. (a) To remove air, because a hydrogen–air mixture could explode when heated (1). (b) To stop the
    hot copper reacting with oxygen in the air / being re-oxidised (1).
15. 200 cm³ = 0.200 dm³ (1); 0.050 ÷ 0.200 = 0.25 mol/dm³ (1).
16. 0.500 × 0.0200 = 0.0100 mol (1).
17. Mr NaCl = 58.5; 5.85 ÷ 58.5 = 0.100 mol (1); 500 cm³ = 0.500 dm³ (1); 0.200 mol/dm³ (1).
18. 0.020 × 24 000 = 480 cm³ (1).
19. 4.8 ÷ 24 = 0.20 mol Mg (1); 1 : 1 so 0.20 mol H₂ (1); 0.20 × 24 = 4.8 dm³ (1).
