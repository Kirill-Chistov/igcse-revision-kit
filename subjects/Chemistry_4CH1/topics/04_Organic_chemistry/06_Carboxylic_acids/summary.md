# Carboxylic acids — Summary

Specification points 4.34C–4.37C (Paper 2 only) · Topic 4 Organic chemistry.

## Essential facts

- Functional group **–COOH** (C=O plus O–H on the same carbon).
- Methanoic HCOOH · ethanoic CH₃COOH · propanoic CH₃CH₂COOH · butanoic CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH. The COOH carbon
  counts.
- Acid + metal → salt + hydrogen: 2CH₃COOH + Mg → (CH₃COO)₂Mg + H₂. Fizzing, metal disappears.
- Acid + carbonate → salt + water + CO₂: 2CH₃COOH + Na₂CO₃ → 2CH₃COONa + H₂O + CO₂. Fizzing, limewater
  milky.
- Salts: ethanoates (CH₃COO⁻).
- **Vinegar**: aqueous solution containing ethanoic acid.

## Common traps

- Magnesium ethanoate is (CH₃COO)₂Mg.
