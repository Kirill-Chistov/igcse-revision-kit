## Essential facts

- Angles *i*, *r*, *c*: degrees (°), measured from the **normal**.
- Frequency: Hz (1 Hz = 1 wave per second). Wavelength, amplitude: m. Speed: m/s. Period: s.
- Convert before substituting: kHz/MHz/GHz × 10³/10⁶/10⁹; cm/mm/nm × 10⁻²/10⁻³/10⁻⁹; ms/µs × 10⁻³/10⁻⁶.
- Calculator in **degree** mode for sin and sin⁻¹.
