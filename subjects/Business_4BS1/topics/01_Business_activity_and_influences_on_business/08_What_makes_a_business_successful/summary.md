## Measuring success

- **Revenue** = price × quantity sold. **Profit** = revenue − total costs.
- Measures: revenue, market share, customer satisfaction (surveys, reviews, repeat purchases), profit, growth, owner/shareholder satisfaction (dividends, share price), employee satisfaction (surveys, labour turnover, absence).
- Each has limits; the right measure depends on the business's **objectives**.

## Reasons for failure

- **Cash-flow problems / lack of finance** → can't pay suppliers, wages, loans → legal action.
- **Not competitive** → customers switch to rivals → revenue falls.
- **Failure to adapt** to market changes → products outdated → demand falls.

## Traps

- A profitable business can still fail from lack of cash. Rising revenue ≠ rising profit.
