# 5.2 Digital currency — summary

Cambridge IGCSE (9–1) Computer Science 0984 · points 5.2.1–5.2.2 · Paper 1

- **Digital currency** = a currency that only exists electronically (no notes or coins). Used to pay
  for goods and services and send money by transferring value electronically between accounts or
  digital wallets. **Cryptocurrency** = decentralised digital currency recorded on a blockchain.
- **Blockchain** = a digital ledger: a time-stamped series of records that cannot be altered.
  Decentralised — every computer in the network holds a copy.
- **Block** = transaction data + timestamp + **hash** (fingerprint of the block) + **previous hash**
  (links to the block before).
- **Tracking**: new transaction → new block → sent to every computer and checked → added to every
  copy → traceable back through the chain.
- **Can't be altered**: change a block → its hash changes → next block's previous hash no longer
  matches → chain broken, change detected.
