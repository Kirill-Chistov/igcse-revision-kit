# Esters — Summary

Specification points 4.38C–4.43C (Paper 2 only) · Topic 4 Organic chemistry.

## Essential facts

- Functional group **–COO–**.
- Ethanol + ethanoic acid → **ethyl ethanoate** + water, acid catalyst (conc. H₂SO₄):
  CH₃COOH + CH₃CH₂OH → CH₃COOCH₂CH₃ + H₂O.
- Acid loses –OH, alcohol loses H.
- Name: alcohol → **-yl**, acid → **-oate** (propan-1-ol + ethanoic acid → propyl ethanoate).
  Formula: acid part first (CH₃COOCH₂CH₂CH₃).
- Reverse: split at the single-bonded O; the C=O side is the acid.
- Esters: volatile, distinctive (fruity) smells → food flavourings, perfumes.
- Practical: ethanol + ethanoic acid + few drops conc. H₂SO₄ → warm in water bath → pour into sodium
  carbonate solution (neutralises acids) → oily layer → waft to smell.
- Safety: no naked flame (ethanol flammable); conc. H₂SO₄ corrosive.

## Common traps

- "Ethyl", not "ethanol", ethanoate.
- H₂SO₄ is a catalyst, not a reactant.
