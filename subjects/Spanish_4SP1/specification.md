## Vocabulary — Appendix 1 (spec pp. 45–88)

Minimum core list organised by sub-topic (A1 p.46 … E5 p.74 — page per sub-topic in
`specification_map.csv`), then non-topic-specific vocabulary (pp. 77–88: abbreviations, social
conventions, prepositions, adjectives, verbs, adverbs, colours, quantities, conjunctions, time
expressions, question words, other expressions, countries, nationalities, geographical areas).
Numbers, times, days and months are also expected. Cognates and English loanwords are not listed.
Tasks for grades 1–5 are based on the list; higher-grade tasks may include unfamiliar words.

## Skills (local IDs)

P1 Paper 1 Listening · P2R Paper 2 Reading · P2W Paper 2 Writing and grammar task · P3 Paper 3
Speaking. Listening and reading abilities (spec pp. 15, 17): identify and note main points;
deduce meaning from context; extract specific details; identify points of view; show some
understanding of unfamiliar language; recognise attitudes, emotions and opinions.
