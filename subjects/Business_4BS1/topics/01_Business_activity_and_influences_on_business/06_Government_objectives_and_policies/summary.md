## Essentials

- Government spends on **public services**, infrastructure and contracts, paid for by **taxation** and borrowing — so spending is constrained.
- Higher taxes: profit tax → less profit kept; income tax → less disposable income → lower demand; sales tax → higher prices → lower demand.
- **Infrastructure** (transport, power, water, internet) → lower costs, faster deliveries, attracts businesses.
- **Legislation** protects consumers, employees, environment, public health: + fair competition, reputation; − compliance costs, fines.
- **Trading bloc:** members trade without tariffs → bigger market, cheaper materials; − more competition, shared rules.
- **Tariff** = tax on imports: + protects domestic firms; − dearer imported materials, higher prices, retaliation against exports.
- Interest rates ↑ → borrowing dearer → costs ↑, profit ↓, investment put off; repayments ↑, saving more attractive → consumer spending ↓ (esp. credit goods). Fall → opposite.

## Calculations

- Price + 20% sales tax = price × 1.2. Interest for a year = amount borrowed × rate; total repaid = loan + interest.
