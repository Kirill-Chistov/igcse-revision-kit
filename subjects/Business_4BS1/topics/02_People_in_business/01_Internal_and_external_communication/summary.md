# Internal and external communication — Summary

Specification points 2.1.1–2.1.2.

- **Communication** = sender → message → medium → receiver (→ feedback), understood as intended.
- **Internal:** inside the business (staff, managers, departments). **External:** customers,
  suppliers, banks, shareholders, government.
- **Good communication →** fewer mistakes, higher productivity, better motivation, faster
  decisions, satisfied customers, on-time supplies.
- **Ineffective →** mistakes and waste, low morale / staff leave, poor decisions, complaints and
  lost sales, late or wrong deliveries.

## Methods (benefit / limitation)

- Face-to-face: instant feedback / no record.
- Telephone: quick over distance / no record, time zones.
- Written (letters, memos, notices, reports): permanent record / slow, one-way, may be ignored.
- Email (IT): fast, many receivers / overload, misread tone.
- Text / instant messaging (IT): very fast / short, easily misread.
- Video conferencing (IT): face-to-face across countries, saves travel / needs reliable internet.
- Intranet (IT): same up-to-date info for all staff / staff must check it.
- Website, social media (IT): reach many customers 24/7 / public negative comments.

## Barriers → removal

- Sender: jargon, unclear message → keep it short, clear, in the receiver's language.
- Medium: wrong method, technical failure, long chain of command → right method, reliable IT,
  shorter chain.
- Receiver: not listening, overload, language/culture, distance/time zones → relevant info only,
  translation, video calls at shared times.
- No feedback → use two-way methods.

**Trap:** barrier = cause; problem = consequence.
