# Motion in the universe — Summary

Specification points 8.2–8.6 · Topic 8 Astrophysics · both papers.

## Essential facts and ideas

- **Universe** = billions of galaxies · **galaxy** = billions of stars · our solar system is in the
  **Milky Way**.
- *g* depends on the **mass** of the planet/moon (more mass → larger *g*) and the **distance from
  its centre** (further → smaller *g*). Earth 10 N/kg, Moon 1.6 N/kg. Mass never changes; weight
  does.
- **Gravity causes orbits:** the force points to the centre, at 90° to the motion, so it changes
  the **direction**, not the speed. Moons ← planet's gravity; planets and comets ← Sun's gravity;
  artificial satellites ← Earth's gravity.

## Orbits compared

| | Planets | Moons | Comets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Orbit | the Sun | a planet | the Sun |
| Shape | nearly circular | nearly circular | highly elliptical |
| Speed | ~constant | ~constant | fastest near Sun, slowest far away |

Comet orbits can be at any angle to the planets' plane.

## Equation (given in the exam)

*v* = 2π*r* / *T* — *v* in m/s, *r* in m from the **centre**, *T* in s.

## Common traps

- "No gravity in space" — wrong: *g* is smaller, not zero.
- Convert km → m and days → s before using *v* = 2π*r*/*T*.
