# Cloning — Questions

**Specification points 5.17B–5.20B** · Paper 2 only

Marking guidance below is written for this repository; it is not an official mark scheme. No
Biology past papers are stored in the repository yet.

## Questions

1. **[P2] [4 marks]** *Describe* how micropropagation is used to produce new plants from a parent
   plant.
2. **[P2] [2 marks]** *Explain* why explants and equipment must be sterile during micropropagation.
3. **[P2] [3 marks]** A grower wants 10 000 orchids with the same flower colour as one parent
   plant. *Explain* why micropropagation is better than growing plants from seed.
4. **[P2] [5 marks]** *Describe* the stages used to produce Dolly the sheep.
5. **[P2] [2 marks]** Dolly was born to a Scottish Blackface sheep but had a white face like the
   Finn Dorset sheep that provided the udder cell. *Explain* why.
6. **[P2] [3 marks]** *Explain* why cloned transgenic goats are used, rather than breeding, to
   produce a human protein in goats' milk.

## Answers and marking guidance

1. Small pieces / explants cut from the parent, e.g. shoot tips (1); sterilised (1); placed on agar
   / growth medium containing nutrients and plant hormones (1); cells divide by mitosis into
   plantlets / shoots and roots form (1); transferred to compost. Max 4.
2. Microorganisms would grow on the nutrient medium (1) and compete with / infect / kill the
   explants (1).
3. All plants genetically identical to the parent / produced by mitosis (1), so all have the same
   flower colour (1); plants from seed show variation (1); many plants produced quickly /
   all year round (1). Max 3.
4. Nucleus removed from an egg cell of sheep B / enucleated egg (1); diploid nucleus taken from a
   body / udder cell of sheep A (1); nucleus inserted into the enucleated egg (1); electric shock
   to fuse / stimulate division (1); cell divides by mitosis to form an embryo (1); embryo
   implanted in uterus of surrogate mother (1). Max 5.
5. The nucleus / all the chromosomes came from the Finn Dorset (1); the egg's nucleus had been
   removed, so the Blackface contributed no genes / the surrogate only provided the uterus (1).
6. Making a transgenic animal is difficult / rarely successful (1); sexual reproduction means only
   some offspring inherit the gene (1); cloning gives many identical animals that all have the gene
   / all make the protein (1).
