# Metallic bonding — Summary

Specification points 1.52C–1.54C · Topic 1 Principles of chemistry. **All Paper 2 only.**

## Essential facts

- Diagram: regular rows of **positive ions** (+) with **delocalised electrons** (e⁻) between them;
  label both.
- **Metallic bonding**: strong electrostatic attraction between positive metal ions and
  delocalised electrons.
- **Conduct electricity**: delocalised electrons move through the structure and carry charge.
- **Malleable**: layers of ions slide over each other; delocalised electrons keep holding them.
- **High mp/bp**: many strong attractions to overcome.

## Common traps

- Electrons move, not ions. Draw ions, not neutral atoms.
