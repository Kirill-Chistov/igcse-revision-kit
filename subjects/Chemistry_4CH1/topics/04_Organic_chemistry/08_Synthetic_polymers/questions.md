# Synthetic polymers — Questions

Specification points 4.44–4.50C · Topic 4 Organic chemistry. 4.48C–4.50C Paper 2 only. Marks in
brackets.

> All questions and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository; they are not
> official Pearson questions or mark schemes.

## 1. Recall

1. What is an addition polymer? (2)
2. Name the polymer made from chloroethene. (1)

## 2. Repeat units and monomers

3. Draw the repeat unit of poly(propene). (2)
4. Draw the repeat unit of poly(tetrafluoroethene). (2)
5. The repeat unit of a polymer is –(CH₂–CH(C₆H₅))ₙ–. Draw the displayed formula of the monomer. (1)
6. Write an equation for the formation of poly(ethene) from ethene. (2)

## 3. Disposal

7. Explain two problems in disposing of addition polymers. (4)

## 4. Condensation polymers (Paper 2 only)

8. What two types of monomer react to form a polyester, and what else is produced? (3)
9. Draw the repeat unit of the polyester formed from ethanedioic acid, HOOC–COOH, and ethanediol,
   HOCH₂CH₂OH. (2)
10. Give one difference between addition and condensation polymerisation. (1)
11. What is a biopolyester? (2)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. A large molecule formed by joining many small molecules (1) called monomers (1).
2. Poly(chloroethene) (1).
3. Two C in the chain with single C–C; one C has two H, the other has H and CH₃ (1); extension bonds
   through brackets and n (1).
4. Two C each with two F, single C–C (1); brackets, extension bonds and n (1).
5. CH₂=CH–C₆H₅: C=C with two H on one C, H and C₆H₅ on the other (1).
6. n CH₂=CH₂ → –(CH₂–CH₂)ₙ– — monomer with n (1), repeat unit with n (1).
7. Inert / unreactive (1) so not biodegradable — not broken down by microorganisms, stays in landfill
   for a long time (1); burning (1) gives toxic gases such as CO or HCl (1).
8. A dicarboxylic acid (1) and a diol (1); water (1).
9. –(OC–COO–CH₂CH₂–O)– with C=O groups shown (1); brackets, extension bonds and n (1).
10. Condensation also produces water (a small molecule); addition gives the polymer only (1).
11. A polyester (1) that is biodegradable (1).
