# Portuguese 1PG0 — Subject Rules

**Pearson Edexcel GCSE (9–1) Portuguese (1PG0)** — a **Level 1/Level 2 GCSE**, *not* an
International GCSE. Do not substitute International GCSE Portuguese, and do not treat this as
a native-speaker Portuguese course.

## Four assessed skills, four papers, 25% each

| Paper | Code | Skill | Foundation | Higher | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1PG0/1F, 1H | Listening | 35 min (incl. 5 min reading) | 45 min (incl. 5 min reading) | 50 |
| 2 | 1PG0/2F, 2H | Speaking | 7–9 min + 12 min prep | 10–12 min + 12 min prep | 70 |
| 3 | 1PG0/3F, 3H | Reading | 45 min | 1 h | 50 |
| 4 | 1PG0/4F, 4H | Writing | 1 h 15 min | 1 h 20 min | 60 |

Paper 2 is conducted by the teacher within a prescribed window and marked externally by Pearson.

## Tiering — confirm before writing Higher-only material

Entry is at **Foundation or Higher for all four papers**; a student cannot mix tiers. The
student's tier is in `me/profile.md`. If it is not recorded, ask; the shipped map assumes Higher.

## Language of the question changes by tier

- Paper 1: Foundation — Section A in English, Section B in Portuguese. Higher — Section A in
  Portuguese, Section B in English.
- Paper 3: Section A in English, Section B in Portuguese, Section C translation into English.
- Paper 4: instructions in Portuguese throughout; word counts are specified per question.

## Themes are not tied to papers

All four papers draw on vocabulary and structures from **all five themes**. There is no
"listening topic" or "writing topic".

All topics must be studied in the context of **both** the student's home country **and**
countries and communities where Portuguese is spoken.

## Translation is assessed in both directions

Portuguese → English (Paper 3, Section C) and English → Portuguese (Paper 4). Both need
deliberate practice; they are not a by-product of reading and writing.

## Speaking has a fixed structure

Three tasks, always in this order:

1. **Role play** — one topic, allocated by Pearson.
2. **Picture-based questions** — one topic, allocated by Pearson.
3. **Conversation** on two themes — the first from a topic the student chooses in advance, the
   second allocated by Pearson.

## Grammar and vocabulary are assessable content

- **Appendix 2 (grammar list)** is examinable. Structures marked `(R)` require **receptive**
  knowledge only — the student must understand them but is not required to produce them. The
  list is split into Foundation and additional Higher Tier requirements.
- **Appendix 3 (vocabulary list)** is examinable. Both European (`EP`) and Brazilian (`BP`)
  variants appear; material should present both where the list does.

## Identifiers used here

Pearson numbers themes but names topics without numbers. This repository uses local references
`T4.2` (theme 4, topic 2), `G7` (grammar category 7) and `S3` (assessed skill 3). These are
**repository-local**, not Pearson codes — never quote them to a teacher or in an exam.

## Watch out for

Material for Edexcel International GCSE Portuguese, for Spanish/French GCSE structures assumed
to transfer, or for pre-2018 Portuguese specifications.
