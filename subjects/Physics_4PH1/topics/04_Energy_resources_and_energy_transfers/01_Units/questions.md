## 1. Recall

1. State the unit of (a) energy, (b) power, (c) force, (d) speed.
2. What is 1 watt in joules and seconds?

## 2. Application

1. Convert: (a) 3.6 kJ to J; (b) 2.5 MW to W; (c) 450 g to kg; (d) 4 minutes to s.
2. A student writes the power of a motor as "600 J". Correct the unit and explain what the number
   now means.

## 3. Exam-style prompts

1. **[2 marks]** Show that 1 J is the same as 1 N m.

## Answers and marking guidance

*Not an official mark scheme — written to the specification wording.*

**Recall.** 1. (a) joule, J (b) watt, W (c) newton, N (d) metre/second, m/s. 2. 1 W = 1 J/s — one
joule transferred per second.

**Application.** 1. (a) 3600 J (b) 2 500 000 W (2.5 × 10⁶ W) (c) 0.45 kg (d) 240 s. 2. 600 W: 600 J of energy transferred every second.

**Exam-style.** 1. Work done = force × distance (1) so J = N × m = N m (1).
