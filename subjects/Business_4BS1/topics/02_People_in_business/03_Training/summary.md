# Training — Summary

Specification point 2.3.1.

- **Induction** — new starters: people, layout, rules, health and safety → productive sooner,
  fewer accidents / delays real work, information overload.
- **On-the-job** — learn while working, coached by experienced staff → cheap, still producing,
  job-specific / trainer's output falls, bad habits, mistakes with real customers.
- **Off-the-job** — college, course, online → experts, new ideas, qualifications / costly, no
  output, may leave once qualified.
- **Health and safety laws:** safe workplace, equipment, protective clothing, risk assessment,
  safety training. Comply → fewer accidents, no fines or claims, reputation, motivation /
  cost of training, equipment, admin.
- **Training benefits:** business — productivity, quality, service, motivation, lower labour
  turnover, flexible staff; workers — skills, qualifications, promotion, pay, job security.
- **Limitations:** cost, lost output, poaching, may not work.

**Trap:** induction = new staff only; on-/off-the-job = where the training happens.
