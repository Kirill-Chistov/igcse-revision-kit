4. Exploration (1), involvement (1), development (1).

7. Decline: carrying capacity exceeded — congestion, pollution, dated image — so visitors go to

10. Tourist spending creates jobs in hotels (1); hotels buy from local farms/suppliers, which employ

11. Economic benefits, 1 each: jobs; income for local businesses; infrastructure. Social problems,

13. Aviation emits greenhouse gases (1), adding to climate change (1).

15. Strategy = plan, policy or agreement (1), e.g. national park laws / Code of Ethics (1);

17. Level-marked. Local techniques (zoning, footpaths, fees) are cheap, quick and visible but treat
