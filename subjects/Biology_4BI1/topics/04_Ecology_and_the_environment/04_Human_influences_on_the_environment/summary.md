# Human influences on the environment — Summary

Specification points 4.12–4.17, 4.18B (Paper 2 only).

- **SO₂:** burning sulfur-containing fossil fuels → acid rain → acidified lakes (fish, eggs die);
  damaged leaves, minerals leached → trees die. Also irritates airways.
- **CO:** incomplete combustion (engines, boilers, smoking) → binds haemoglobin, won't let go → less
  O₂ carried → tiredness, death.
- **Greenhouse gases:** water vapour, CO₂ (fossil fuels, deforestation), methane (cattle, rice,
  landfill), nitrous oxide (fertilisers, exhausts), CFCs (old aerosols, fridges).
- **Enhanced greenhouse effect:** more gases → more heat absorbed and re-radiated → global warming.
  May cause: sea-level rise, climate change, migration/extinction, crop and pest changes.
- **Eutrophication:** leached nitrates/phosphates → algal bloom → light blocked → plants die →
  bacteria decompose and respire → O₂ falls → fish die.
- **Sewage:** mineral ions (eutrophication) + organic matter (bacteria use O₂) + pathogens (disease).
- **Deforestation (P2):** leaching (infertile soil); erosion (no roots/canopy); less transpiration
  → less rain; less photosynthesis + burning → more CO₂, less O₂.
