# Fission and fusion — Summary

Specification points 7.17–7.26 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1 · both papers.

- **Fission, fusion and radioactive decay** all release energy.
- **Fission:** a **neutron** is absorbed by **U-235** → it splits into **two radioactive daughter nuclei** + **2–3 neutrons**; energy released as **kinetic energy** of the products.
- **Chain reaction:** those neutrons split more U-235 nuclei. One neutron per fission going on → steady; more → out of control.
- **Moderator** (graphite/water): **slows** neutrons so they can be absorbed and cause fission.
- **Control rods** (boron): **absorb** neutrons; lower → less fission, fully in → stops.
- **Shielding** (thick concrete): absorbs **neutrons and gamma** leaving the core.
- **Fusion:** small nuclei **join** into a larger one; products have **less mass** → **energy released**. Powers **stars** (hydrogen → helium).
- Needs **very high temperature** (nuclei fast enough to overcome **electrostatic repulsion** between protons) and **very high pressure** (collisions often enough).
- **Fission** = split a heavy nucleus, started by a neutron, radioactive products. **Fusion** = join light nuclei, needs extreme T and P, happens in stars.
