# Inheritance — Summary

**Specification points 3.14–3.39** · Papers 1 and 2 (3.16B–3.18B, 3.21B, 3.35B–3.37B Paper 2 only)

- Genome = all the DNA of an organism. Gene = section of DNA coding for one protein. Genes are on chromosomes in the nucleus; chromosomes in pairs.
- P2: DNA = two strands, double helix, bases A–T, C–G. RNA = one strand, U instead of T.
- P2: Transcription (nucleus): DNA unzips, complementary mRNA made. mRNA → ribosome. Translation: codon (3 bases) ↔ tRNA anticodon; tRNA brings specific amino acid; amino acids joined in codon order.
- Allele = alternative form of a gene. Genotype (alleles, Tt) → phenotype (characteristic). Dominant shows when present; recessive only as tt. Homozygous TT/tt, heterozygous Tt.
- P2: Codominance = both alleles expressed in heterozygote (roan cattle C^R C^W).
- Genetic diagram: phenotypes → genotypes → gametes → Punnett → offspring genotypes → phenotypes. Tt × Tt = 3 : 1 (¼ recessive). Tt × tt = 1 : 1. Probability as ¼ / 0.25 / 25%, never 1 : 4. Each child independent.
- Pedigree: unaffected parents with affected child → recessive, both parents Nn; affected = nn.
- XX female, XY male. Eggs all X; sperm X or Y → 50% each sex.
- Mitosis: 1 division, 2 identical diploid cells; growth, repair, cloning, asexual reproduction. Meiosis: 2 divisions, 4 genetically different haploid gametes. Humans 46 / 23.
- Random fertilisation of different gametes → variation. Variation: genetic, environmental or both. Most features polygenic → continuous.
- Mutation: rare, random, inheritable change in genetic material.
- P2: base change → codon → amino acid → protein shape → phenotype. Most no effect, some small, rarely significant. Mutagens: gamma, X-rays, UV; tobacco chemicals.
- Natural selection: variation → competition → best adapted survive → reproduce, pass on alleles → allele frequency rises over generations.
- Antibiotic resistance: random mutation → antibiotic kills non-resistant → resistant survive and reproduce fast → hard-to-treat infections (MRSA). Use antibiotics only when needed; finish the course.
