## 2. Explain (3 marks: point + two linked developments)

5. Explain one disadvantage to a business of using an overdraft to buy new machinery. (3)
6. Explain one benefit to a business of using venture capital. (3)
7. Explain one disadvantage of raising finance through a stock market flotation. (3)

## 3. Apply

8. Sam runs a small café as a sole trader. She needs $2,000 for two months to pay suppliers
   during a quiet season. State the most suitable source of finance and outline one reason. (2)
9. A large manufacturer needs $40 million to build a new factory. Give two suitable sources and
   one drawback of each. (4)

## 4. Justify (9 marks)

10. A new online clothing business needs $15,000 to buy equipment. Option 1: crowdfunding.
    Option 2: loan capital. Justify which option it should choose. (9)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. Profit kept in the business and reinvested rather than paid out to the owners.
2. Money owed to suppliers for goods/services received but not yet paid for.
3. Finance needed for more than one year, e.g. to buy non-current assets or expand.
4. Any two: personal savings, retained profit, selling assets.
5. Overdraft interest rates are high (1) → the cost of the machinery rises because it is repaid
   over a long period (1) → lower profit / the bank may demand repayment at short notice, leaving
   the business without funds (1).
6. Venture capitalists will invest in risky new businesses that banks refuse (1) → the business can
   obtain the finance it needs to start or grow (1) → and investors may also bring expertise and
   contacts that improve decisions (1).
7. New shareholders gain part-ownership (1) → the original owners lose some control over decisions
   (1) → and a plc's shares can be bought by others, risking takeover (1). *Also credit: high cost
   and time of flotation.*
8. Overdraft (or trade payables) (1); flexible — she borrows only for the two quiet months and pays
   interest only on the amount used (1).
9. E.g. loan capital — interest adds to costs / security needed; share capital — ownership and
   control diluted / dividends expected; retained profit — only if enough profit, lower dividends.
   (1 per source, 1 per drawback.)
10. Look for: analysis of both options in context (no interest and publicity/testing demand vs
    fees, risk of not reaching target, idea copied; loan gives a certain sum and keeps control but
    adds interest and needs security and a new business may be refused); a justified judgement
    specific to this business (e.g. a new business with no trading record may be refused a loan,
    and its young online customers suit crowdfunding). Structure: for → against → judgement.
