# Radioactivity and particles — units — Summary

Specification point 7.1 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1 · both papers.

- **Bq (becquerel)** = unit of **activity**; 1 Bq = 1 decay per second.
- **cm** — range in air, absorber thickness, source–detector distance.
- **h, min, s** — half-lives and counting times. 1 h = 60 min = 3600 s.
- A GM counter gives a **count rate** (counts/s or counts/min), not the activity — but it halves in the same time.
- Same time unit throughout **before** counting half-lives.
