# Magnetism — Summary

Specification points 6.2–6.7 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1 · both papers.

- **Like poles repel, unlike poles attract.** Magnets attract **iron, steel, nickel, cobalt** with either pole.
- **Induced magnetism:** a magnetic material in a field becomes a magnet; near end = opposite pole → always attracted. Only **repulsion** proves something is a magnet.
- **Soft (iron):** easy to magnetise, loses it easily → electromagnet and transformer cores. **Hard (steel):** hard to magnetise, keeps it → permanent magnets.
- **Field line:** direction of the force on a **north pole** (the way a compass points). N → S outside; closer lines = stronger field; lines never cross.
- **Practical:** plotting compass — dot at the needle tip, move the tail to the dot, repeat N → S, join, add arrows. Iron filings: whole pattern, no direction.
- Like poles facing → **neutral point** midway (no force).
- **Uniform field:** opposite poles (N facing S), close, flat faces parallel → straight, parallel, equally spaced lines in the gap.
