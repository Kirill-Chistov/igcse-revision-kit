# Reproduction — Questions

**Specification points 3.1–3.13** · Papers 1 and 2 (questions marked P2 are Paper 2 only)

Marking guidance below is written for this repository; it is not an official mark scheme.

## Questions

1. Define fertilisation.
2. Give two differences between sexual and asexual reproduction.
3. Suggest one advantage to a plant species of reproducing sexually.
4. Grass is wind-pollinated. Describe three ways its flowers are adapted for wind pollination.
5. **[3 marks]** *Explain* how the stigma and the pollen of an insect-pollinated flower are adapted
   for pollination.
6. State what each part develops into after fertilisation: (a) ovule, (b) ovary, (c) zygote.
7. **[3 marks]** *Describe* what happens between a pollen grain landing on a stigma and fertilisation.
8. A student sets up four tubes of cress seeds: A on moist cotton wool at 20 °C; B on dry cotton
   wool at 20 °C; C in boiled, cooled water under a layer of oil at 20 °C; D on moist cotton wool in
   a fridge. (a) Explain why the water in tube C was boiled and covered with oil. (b) Give two variables the student should control.
   (c) Explain why tube A is needed.
9. **[3 marks]** *Explain* why the dry mass of a seedling falls during its first few days.
10. A gardener wants 50 plants identical to one prize plant. Describe how to produce them from
    cuttings, and explain why a plastic bag is placed over the pot.
11. Explain why the testes are held in the scrotum outside the body.
12. Name the part of the female reproductive system where (a) fertilisation happens, (b) the
    embryo implants.
13. **[2 marks]** Describe the roles of oestrogen and progesterone in the menstrual cycle.
14. **P2 · [4 marks]** *Describe* the roles of FSH and LH in the menstrual cycle.
15. **P2** Suggest why a woman does not ovulate while she is pregnant.
16. **[4 marks]** *Describe* how the placenta supplies the developing embryo with nutrients and
    how it is adapted for this.
17. Explain how amniotic fluid protects the embryo.
18. Give two secondary sexual characteristics caused by testosterone and two caused by oestrogen.

## Answers and marking guidance

1. Fusion of a male and a female gamete (1) to form a zygote (1).
2. Any two: sexual needs two parents / asexual one; sexual involves gametes and fertilisation,
   asexual does not; sexual offspring genetically different / asexual offspring identical (clones).
3. Variation (1), so some offspring may survive a disease or a change in the environment (1).
4. Any three: small / green / no petals; no scent or nectar; anthers hang outside on long
   filaments; large feathery stigmas hang outside; large amounts of small, light, smooth pollen.
5. Stigma sticky (1) inside the flower where the insect touches it (1); pollen sticky / spiky so
   it sticks to the insect's body (1).
6. (a) seed; (b) fruit; (c) embryo.
7. Pollen tube grows from the pollen grain down the style (1); enters the ovule through the
   micropyle (1); male nucleus fuses with the female nucleus in the ovule (1).
8. (a) Boiling removes dissolved oxygen (1); oil stops oxygen dissolving back in (1).
   (b) Any two: type of seed; number of seeds; temperature; time; volume of water.
   (c) Control: shows seeds germinate when all conditions are present (1), so any difference is
   due to the missing condition (1).
9. Stored food (starch) is broken down by enzymes (1) and used in respiration (1); carbon dioxide
   is lost / leaves have not yet opened so no photosynthesis (1).
10. Cut shoots from the prize plant (1); remove lower leaves and dip cut ends in rooting powder /
    hormone (1); plant in damp compost (1). Bag keeps the air humid so less water is lost by
    transpiration (1) while the cutting has no roots (1).
11. Slightly lower temperature (1) is best for sperm production (1).
12. (a) oviduct; (b) uterus lining.
13. Oestrogen repairs / thickens the uterus lining (1); progesterone maintains the lining (1).
14. FSH causes an egg to mature (1) and stimulates oestrogen production (1); LH triggers
    ovulation / release of the egg (1) at about day 14 (1).
15. Progesterone stays high (1), inhibiting FSH / LH (1), so no egg matures / is released.
16. Glucose / amino acids / minerals diffuse from mother's blood to embryo's blood (1), carried to
    embryo in umbilical cord (1); villi give large surface area (1); thin barrier / short diffusion
    distance (1); good blood supply keeps concentration gradient (1). Max 4.
17. Cushions the embryo / absorbs knocks (1) because liquid cannot be compressed (1); supports it /
    allows movement (1). Max 2.
18. Testosterone, any two: facial / body hair; deeper voice; muscles develop; penis / testes grow;
    sperm production. Oestrogen, any two: breasts develop; hips widen; menstrual cycle starts;
    pubic / underarm hair.
