## Practice

1. **[1]** Define the term *globalisation*.
2. **[1]** Define the term *depreciation* of a currency.
3. **[2]** €1 = US$1.10. A German business sells a machine for €4 000. Calculate the price of the machine in US dollars.
4. **[2]** £1 = ¥190. A UK retailer buys toys from Japan costing ¥95 000. Calculate the cost of the toys in pounds.
5. **[3]** Explain one threat of globalisation to a small business.
6. **[6]** A Malaysian furniture business sells most of its products in the USA. The Malaysian ringgit appreciates against the US dollar. Analyse the impact on the business.
7. **[6]** A multinational car manufacturer plans to build a factory in Thailand. Analyse the benefits to Thailand.

## Marking guidance (not an official mark scheme)

1. The process by which the world's economies become increasingly connected through trade and the movement of money, people and ideas.
2. A fall in the value of a currency against another currency.
3. 4 000 × 1.10 (1) = US$4 400 (1).
4. 95 000 ÷ 190 (1) = £500 (1).
5. Foreign competitors can sell in its market (1) → often at lower prices (1) → so it may lose customers and market share (1).
6. Furniture becomes dearer in US$ → US demand falls → lower revenue; or it cuts its prices to stay competitive → lower profit per item. Imported materials (if any) become cheaper → partly offsets.
7. Jobs → lower unemployment, more spending; tax revenue → more public services; training → higher skills; car exports → more income for Thailand.
