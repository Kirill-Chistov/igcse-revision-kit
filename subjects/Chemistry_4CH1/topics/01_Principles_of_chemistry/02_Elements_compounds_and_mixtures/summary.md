# Elements, compounds and mixtures — Summary

Specification points 1.8–1.13 · Topic 1 Principles of chemistry.

## Essential facts

- **Element**: one type of atom. **Compound**: two or more elements chemically bonded, fixed
  proportions. **Mixture**: substances not chemically bonded, separable by physical methods.
- Pure substance: **fixed** mp and bp. Mixture: melts / boils over a **range**.

## Which technique?

| To separate | Use |
| --- | --- |
| insoluble solid from liquid | filtration (residue + filtrate) |
| soluble solid from its solution | crystallisation: evaporate to saturation, cool, filter, dry |
| solvent from a solution | simple distillation |
| liquids with different boiling points | fractional distillation (lowest bp collected first) |
| dissolved coloured substances | paper chromatography |

## Chromatography

- Rf = distance moved by substance ÷ distance moved by solvent front, both from the baseline.
- Same Rf in same solvent → same substance. Number of spots → at least that many substances.
- Practical: pencil baseline, solvent below baseline, lid, small spots, mark solvent front.

## Common traps

- Air and salt water are mixtures, not compounds.
- Dissolved solids pass through filter paper.
