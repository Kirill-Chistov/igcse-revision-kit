## 1. Recall

1. State the unit of: (a) orbital speed, (b) gravitational field strength, (c) time period,
   (d) gravitational force. (4)
2. Explain why N/kg and m/s² are equivalent units for *g*. (1)

## 2. Conversions

3. Convert: (a) 42 000 km to metres, (b) 7.7 km/s to m/s, (c) 90 minutes to seconds,
   (d) 27 days to seconds. Give answers in standard form. (4)
4. Mars takes 1.9 Earth years to orbit the Sun. Give this time period in seconds. (1)

## Answers and marking guidance

> Marking guidance written for this repository, not an official Pearson mark scheme.

1. (a) m/s (1); (b) N/kg (1); (c) s (1); (d) N (1).
2. *g* is the force on each kg (N/kg); a 1 N force on 1 kg gives an acceleration of 1 m/s², so the
   two are the same (1).
3. (a) 4.2 × 10⁷ m (1); (b) 7.7 × 10³ m/s (1); (c) 5.4 × 10³ s (1); (d) 2.3 × 10⁶ s (1).
4. 1.9 × 3.15 × 10⁷ = 6.0 × 10⁷ s (1).
