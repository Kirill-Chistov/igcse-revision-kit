## 3. Explain and analyse

8. Explain one way a business could increase its gross profit margin. (3)
9. Using Figure 1, analyse the changes in the hotel group's performance from 2024 to 2025. (6)
10. Explain one limitation of using ratios to compare two businesses. (3)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. The ability of a business to pay its short-term debts when they are due.
2. (Current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities.
3. 423 ÷ 900 × 100 (1) = 47.00% (1).
4. 117 ÷ 900 × 100 (1) = 13.00% (1).
5. 117 ÷ 1,100 × 100 (1) = 10.64% (1).
6. Current: 90 ÷ 75 (1) = 1.20 (1). Acid test: (90 − 14) ÷ 75 (1) = 1.01 (1).
7. Profit per pair 52 − 32 = 20; 20 ÷ 32 × 100 (1) = 62.50% (1).
8. Find a cheaper supplier (1) → cost of sales falls while revenue stays the same (1) → so more
   of each $ of revenue is gross profit (1). *Also credit: raise prices if demand allows.*
9. 2024: GPM 50.00%, OPM 15.00%, ROCE 11.71%, current ratio 1.50, acid test 1.31. Look for:
   revenue up but GPM down (cost of sales rising faster) → OPM down → expenses also rising; ROCE
   down → less profit from capital invested; liquidity weaker (current ratio 1.50 → 1.20) but
   acid test still about 1 → can pay short-term debts. Linked, using the figures.
10. Businesses may differ in size or industry (1) → e.g. a supermarket normally has lower margins
    than a jeweller (1) → so a lower margin does not mean worse performance (1). *Also credit:
    different accounting dates; ratios use past data.*
