## Qualification at a glance

| Paper | Code | Duration | Marks | Weighting | Business context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper 1 | 4BS1/01 | 1 h 30 min | 80 | 50% | A **small** business (up to 49 employees), local/national market |
| Paper 2 | 4BS1/02 | 1 h 30 min | 80 | 50% | A **large** business (250+ employees), multinational/global market |

Each paper has four compulsory 20-mark questions mixing multiple-choice, short-answer,
data-response and open-ended items. Calculators may be used. Both papers can draw on the
**whole** specification — the five content sections are not split between papers.

## Assessment objectives

| AO | Description | Weighting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AO1 | Recall, select and communicate knowledge and understanding of business terms, concepts and issues | 27–28% |
| AO2 | Apply knowledge and understanding using appropriate business terms, concepts, theories and calculations in specific contexts | 38–39% |
| AO3 | Select, organise and interpret business information from sources to investigate and analyse issues | 22–23% |
| AO4 | Evaluate business information to make reasoned judgements and draw conclusions | 11–12% |

A maximum of 15% of total qualification marks assess knowledge recall — the majority of marks
require **application, analysis and evaluation in context**.

## Course distinctions that affect what can be assessed

- **No tiering**; both papers are taken by all students.
- The same content is examinable on both papers; only the **business context** differs.
- Quantitative work (cash flow, break-even, ratios, financial documents) is assessable and
  appears mainly in section 3.


## How to read this file
