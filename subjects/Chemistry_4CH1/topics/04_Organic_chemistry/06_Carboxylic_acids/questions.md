# Carboxylic acids — Questions

Specification points 4.34C–4.37C (Paper 2 only) · Topic 4 Organic chemistry. Marks in brackets.

> All questions and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository; they are not
> official Pearson questions or mark schemes.

## 1. Recall

1. Draw the functional group of the carboxylic acids. (1)
2. What is vinegar? (1)
3. Give the structural formula of propanoic acid. (1)

## 2. Formulae and names

4. Draw the displayed formula of ethanoic acid. (1)
5. Name CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH. (1)

## 3. Reactions

6. Describe what you would see when magnesium ribbon is added to ethanoic acid. (2)
7. Write the equation for the reaction of ethanoic acid with magnesium and name the salt. (3)
8. Ethanoic acid is added to sodium carbonate. Name the three products. (3)
9. Describe a test to identify the gas produced in question 8. (2)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. C double-bonded to O and single-bonded to O–H (1).
2. An aqueous solution containing ethanoic acid (1).
3. CH₃CH₂COOH (1).
4. CH₃ carbon with three H, bonded to C with =O and –O–H (1).
5. Butanoic acid (1).
6. Fizzing / bubbles (1); magnesium gets smaller / disappears (1).
7. 2CH₃COOH + Mg → (CH₃COO)₂Mg + H₂ — formulae (1), balancing (1); magnesium ethanoate (1).
8. Sodium ethanoate (1); water (1); carbon dioxide (1).
9. Bubble through limewater (1); limewater turns milky / cloudy (1).
