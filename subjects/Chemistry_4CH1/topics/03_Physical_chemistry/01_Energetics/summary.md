# Energetics — Summary

Specification points 3.1–3.8 · Topic 3 Physical chemistry. 3.5C–3.7C are **Paper 2 only**.

## Essential facts

- **Exothermic**: heat given out, temperature rises, ΔH **negative**.
  **Endothermic**: heat taken in, temperature falls, ΔH **positive**.
- Energy level diagram (P2): exothermic — products **below** reactants, arrow down;
  endothermic — products **above**, arrow up. Label reactants, products, ΔH.
- Bond **breaking** = endothermic; bond **making** = exothermic (P2).

## Calorimetry

- Solutions (dissolving, neutralisation, displacement): polystyrene cup + lid, record start
  temperature, add, stir, record highest (or lowest) temperature.
- Combustion: weigh spirit burner before/after; heat measured mass of water in a copper can.
- Value too small because of **heat loss** (and incomplete combustion). Reduce: lid, insulation,
  draught shield.

## Calculations

```
Q = m c ΔT         m = mass of water/solution heated (g), c = 4.2 J/g/°C
ΔH = −Q(kJ) ÷ n    n = mol of the stated reactant
ΔH = bonds broken − bonds made      (P2)
```

## Common traps

- m is the water, not the fuel. Divide J by 1000. Add the sign: temperature rise → ΔH negative.
- Count bonds × coefficients (2H₂O = 4 O–H).
