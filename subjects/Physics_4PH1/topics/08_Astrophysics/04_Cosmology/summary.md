# Cosmology — Summary

Specification points 8.13P–8.18P · Topic 8 Astrophysics · **all Paper 2 only**.

## Essential facts and ideas

- Source moving relative to observer → observed **frequency and wavelength change** (Doppler, → 3.8).
  Moving **away** → longer λ → **red-shift**; towards → blue-shift. Speed of light unchanged.
- Red-shift is measured from **absorption lines**: same pattern as in the lab, moved to longer λ.
- Almost all galaxies are red-shifted; **further away → bigger red-shift → moving away faster**
  (speed ∝ distance).
- Galaxies receding in every direction, faster when further → **space is expanding** → the
  universe is expanding.
- **Big Bang:** ≈ 13.8 billion years ago, everything in one tiny, hot, dense region; it has expanded
  and cooled since; atoms, then stars and galaxies, formed.
- **Evidence:** (1) red-shift → expansion → began at a point; (2) **CMB** — microwaves from every
  direction, almost uniform: leftover radiation from the hot early universe, stretched to
  microwaves by the expansion.

## Equation (given in the exam)

(λ − λ₀)/λ₀ = Δλ/λ₀ = *v*/*c* — λ₀ lab wavelength, λ observed, *c* = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s,
λ and λ₀ in the same unit.

## Common traps

- Red-shift ≠ light turning red; it is a shift to longer wavelength.
- The expansion is of space itself, not an explosion from a centre.
