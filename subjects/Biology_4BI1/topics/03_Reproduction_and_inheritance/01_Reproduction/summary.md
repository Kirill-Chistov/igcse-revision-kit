# Reproduction — Summary

**Specification points 3.1–3.13** · Papers 1 and 2 (3.10B Paper 2 only)

- Fertilisation: male + female gamete fuse → zygote → mitosis → embryo.
- Sexual: two parents, gametes, variation (survive change) but slower. Asexual: one parent, no gametes, clones, fast, no variation.
- Pollination = pollen from anther to stigma. Insect: big scented petals, nectar, anthers and sticky stigma inside, few sticky grains. Wind: no/small petals, anthers and feathery stigma hang outside, masses of small light pollen.
- Pollen tube grows down style → enters ovule via micropyle → male nucleus fuses with female nucleus. Ovule → seed, ovary → fruit, zygote → embryo.
- Germination needs water (enzymes, swelling), oxygen (respiration), warmth (enzymes). Not light. Boiled water + oil = no oxygen; control has all three.
- Germinating seed: enzymes digest stored starch/protein/lipid → used in respiration and growth. Dry mass falls until leaves photosynthesise, then rises.
- Runners (strawberry, natural) and cuttings (artificial: rooting powder, remove lower leaves, plastic bag) → clones.
- Testes (sperm, testosterone; scrotum cooler), sperm duct, glands (fluid → semen), urethra, penis. Ovaries (eggs, oestrogen, progesterone), oviduct (fertilisation, cilia), uterus (lining, implantation), cervix, vagina.
- Oestrogen repairs and thickens lining; progesterone maintains it. Progesterone falls → menstruation.
- P2: FSH (pituitary) → egg matures + oestrogen made; oestrogen inhibits FSH, triggers LH surge; LH → ovulation day 14; progesterone inhibits FSH and LH.
- Placenta: glucose, amino acids, O₂, water, ions → embryo; CO₂, urea → mother. Villi, thin, good blood supply, bloods don't mix. Amniotic fluid cushions and supports.
- Testosterone: hair, deep voice, muscles, sperm. Oestrogen: breasts, wider hips, hair, periods start.
