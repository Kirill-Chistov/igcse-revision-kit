# Mains electricity — Summary

Specification points 2.2–2.6 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1 · both papers.

## Wires and safety

- **Live** (brown, ≈ 230 V) · **neutral** (blue, ≈ 0 V) · **earth** (green/yellow, carries current only in a fault). Fuse and switch in the **live** wire.
- **Insulation:** plastic round wires — no current to the user.
- **Double insulation:** plastic case + insulated wires, no touchable metal that could go live → no earth wire needed.
- **Earthing:** live touches metal case → large current down low-resistance earth wire → fuse melts / breaker trips → case not live.
- **Fuse:** melts if current > rating; protects device and cable from overheating/fire; replace it. Protects the user only with earthing.
- **Circuit breaker:** automatic switch that trips; faster than a fuse; reset.

## Heating effect

- Electrons collide with metal ions → ions vibrate more → temperature rises. Energy transferred electrically → thermal store.
- Uses: kettle, toaster, heater, hairdryer, iron, oven, immersion heater; lamp filament glows.

## Equations

- **P = I × V** (recall) — W, A, V. Fuse: *I* = *P* ÷ *V*, then the lowest rating **above** it (3 A, 5 A, 13 A).
- **E = I × V × t** (given) — J, A, V, **s**. Also *E* = *P* × *t*.

## a.c. and d.c.

- **d.c.:** one direction only — cell or battery.
- **a.c.:** repeatedly reverses direction — mains (≈ 230 V, 50 Hz).
