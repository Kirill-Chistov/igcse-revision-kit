# Mathematics 4MA1 — Subject Rules

**Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1)** — the **linear** qualification
(Specification A). Not Mathematics B, and not a modular qualification. Do not substitute either.

## Tier — check `me/profile.md`

| Tier | Papers | Duration each | Marks each | Grades |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | 1F, 2F | 2 h | 100 | 5–1 |
| Higher | 1H, 2H | 2 h | 100 | 9–4 |

The student's tier is in `me/profile.md` (asked by `/setup`). The shipped material targets
**Higher**; for a Foundation student, skip Higher-only points (marked `(H)`/Higher in the map).

Consequences:

- **Higher Tier assumes all Foundation Tier content.** Foundation material is never "not needed";
  it is assumed knowledge and is examined at Higher.
- `specification.md` marks each sub-section as `Foundation`, `Higher extends` or `Higher only`.
  Prioritise `Higher only` and `Higher extends`, but never drop `Foundation`.
- Grade 9–4 questions require multi-step reasoning and problem solving, not just technique.

## Assessment

Both papers are 2 hours, 100 marks, 50% each. Calculator allowed on both. A tier-specific
formulae sheet is provided; ruler, compasses, protractor and tracing paper may be used.
Diagrams are not to scale.

## Assessment objectives

AO1 Number and algebra 57–63% · AO2 Shape, space and measures 22–28% · AO3 Handling data 12–18%.

Students must also demonstrate **problem solving** (translating a problem into a mathematical
process) and **mathematical reasoning** (deductions, chains of reasoning, arguments and proofs,
accurate communication). Both are examined; material must build them explicitly.

## How content is organised here

`specification.md` lists sub-sections (e.g. `1.4 Powers and roots`). Within each sub-section the
official specification lists lettered requirements (A, B, C …) with worked notes and worked
examples. **Read those lettered requirements in `subjects/Mathematics_4MA1/Specification.pdf` before writing a
topic** — the sub-section title alone does not tell you what is required.

## Watch out for

- UK GCSE Maths (Edexcel 1MA1) material: heavily overlapping but **not identical**. 4MA1
  includes set language and notation, and function notation and calculus at Higher, which 1MA1
  does not treat the same way.
- Mathematics B (4MB1) material.
