# Respiration — Summary

**Specification points 2.34–2.39** · Papers 1 and 2

- Respiration: enzyme-controlled breakdown of glucose in cells → energy → ATP. All living cells, all the time.
- ATP powers muscle contraction, active transport, protein synthesis, cell division, nerve impulses, keeping warm.
- Aerobic: glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water; C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O. Needs O₂, much ATP, mitochondria.
- Anaerobic animals: glucose → lactic acid. Plants/yeast: glucose → ethanol + carbon dioxide. No O₂, much less ATP.
- Practical: germinating seeds in vacuum flask warm up; boiled + disinfected seeds = control. Hydrogencarbonate indicator red → yellow with more CO₂ (limewater → cloudy).
- Trap: respiration ≠ breathing; plants respire at night and in the day.
