# Gas exchange — Summary

**Specification points 2.40B–2.50** · 2.40B–2.45B are Paper 2 only

## Plants (P2)

- Gases move by diffusion through stomata and air spaces.
- Photosynthesis: CO₂ in, O₂ out (light only). Respiration: O₂ in, CO₂ out (always).
- Leaf: broad, thin, many stomata (lower surface), air spaces, moist cell walls.
- Stomata: guard cells turgid → open (light); flaccid → closed (dark, water shortage).
- Dark: net O₂ in, CO₂ out. Dim: no net exchange. Bright: net CO₂ in, O₂ out.
- Hydrogencarbonate indicator: red (air), yellow (more CO₂), purple (less CO₂). Control: tube with no leaf.

## Humans

- Trachea (cartilage rings) → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli; ribs, intercostal muscles, pleural membranes, diaphragm.
- In: intercostals contract, ribs up/out; diaphragm contracts, flattens; volume ↑, pressure ↓, air in. Out: the reverse.
- Alveoli: huge SA, walls one cell thick, moist, dense capillaries, ventilation → steep gradients.
- Tar: cancer, cilia stop + mucus (bronchitis), emphysema. CO: binds haemoglobin, less O₂. Nicotine: addictive, heart rate + blood pressure ↑. CO + nicotine → fatty deposits in coronary arteries → CHD.
- Practical: exhaled air turns limewater cloudy quickly. Exercise ↑ breathing rate and depth; stays high in recovery.
