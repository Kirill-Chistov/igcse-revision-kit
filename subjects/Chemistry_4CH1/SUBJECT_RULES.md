# Chemistry 4CH1 — Subject Rules

**Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Chemistry (4CH1)** — the **separate (single-award)
Chemistry** qualification, not Double Award Science.

## Paper structure

| Paper | Code | Duration | Marks | Weighting | Assesses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper 1 | 4CH1/1C | 2 h | 110 | 61.1% | Content **without** a `C` reference |
| Paper 2 | 4CH1/2C | 1 h 15 min | 70 | 38.9% | **All** content, including `C` |

## The `C` rule

Bold statements carrying a `C` reference are **Paper 2 only**; they are marked with a trailing
`C` in `specification.md` and tagged in `specification_map.csv`. Do not present them as Paper 1
requirements, and do not omit them when preparing for Paper 2.

## No tiering

No Foundation/Higher split; all students sit both papers.

## Required practicals

Points beginning `practical:` are required practical work and are assessable under AO3
(apparatus, method, safety, observations, results, error and evaluation). Practical
material lives inside the relevant topic folder (no separate practicals folder).

## Assessment objectives

AO1 38–42% · AO2 38–42% · AO3 19–21%.

## Chemistry-specific requirements that material must build

- **Formulae and equations**: word equations, balanced symbol equations, state symbols, ionic
  equations (including half equations for electrolysis).
- **Calculations**: relative formula mass, moles, reacting masses, concentration, percentage
  yield, empirical formulae, gas volumes, titration calculations.
- **Chemical tests**: the specification's named tests for gases, cations and anions must be
  reproducible exactly, including reagent, observation and conclusion.
- **The Periodic Table** is provided in the examination; trends must still be explained.

## Watch out for

Material written for Cambridge IGCSE Chemistry (0620/0971) or UK GCSE Chemistry — different
specifications with different required practicals and tests. Check the code 4CH1.
