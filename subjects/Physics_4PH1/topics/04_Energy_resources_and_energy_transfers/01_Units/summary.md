## Essential facts

- kg (mass) · J (energy, work done) · m (distance, height) · m/s (speed) · m/s² (acceleration) ·
  N (force, weight) · s (time) · W (power).
- 1 J = 1 N m (from *W* = *F* × *d*); 1 W = 1 J/s (from *P* = *W* / *t*).
- *g* in N/kg; use the value given (usually 10 N/kg).

## Quick reminders

- k = × 10³, M = × 10⁶, G = × 10⁹.
- g → kg ÷ 1000; cm → m ÷ 100; min → s × 60; h → s × 3600.
- Convert **before** substituting.

## Common traps

- Grams in ½*mv*² or *mgh*; minutes in *P* = *W* / *t*.
- Power in J instead of W.
