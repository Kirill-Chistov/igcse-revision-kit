# Nutrition — Summary

**Specification points 2.18–2.33B** · 2.33B is Paper 2 only

## Plants

- carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (light, chlorophyll); 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂.
- Light energy → chemical energy in glucose.
- Light and CO₂: rate rises while limiting, then plateaus (another factor limits). Temperature: optimum, then enzymes denatured.
- Leaf: broad (light), thin (diffusion), transparent epidermis, palisade packed with chloroplasts near top, air spaces, stomata, xylem in, phloem out.
- Magnesium → chlorophyll (lack: yellow leaves). Nitrate → amino acids/proteins (lack: stunted).
- Practicals: pondweed bubbles vs lamp distance (control temperature with water tank, CO₂ with NaHCO₃); starch test = boil, ethanol (water bath), rinse, iodine; destarch first; variegated leaf, foil, sodium hydroxide bag.

## Humans

- Balanced diet: all 7 components in right proportions + right energy.
- Carbohydrate energy; protein growth/repair; lipid energy store; vit A vision; vit C skin/gums (scurvy); vit D calcium absorption (rickets); calcium bones/teeth; iron haemoglobin (anaemia); water solvent; fibre bulk for peristalsis.
- More energy: more activity, growth (teenagers), pregnancy.
- Route: mouth → oesophagus → stomach → duodenum → ileum → colon → rectum → anus.
- Stomach acid kills microorganisms, low pH for protease. Colon absorbs water. Ileum absorbs digested food.
- Peristalsis: circular muscles contract behind food.
- Amylase starch → maltose; maltase maltose → glucose; protease protein → amino acids; lipase lipid → fatty acids + glycerol.
- Bile: made in liver, stored in gall bladder; alkaline (neutralises acid); emulsifies fat (bigger SA for lipase). Not an enzyme.
- Villus: large SA (villi, microvilli), one cell thick, capillaries (glucose, amino acids), lacteal (fatty acids, glycerol); long intestine.
- (P2) Food energy: J = mass water (g) × 4.2 × temperature rise; ÷ mass of food. Underestimate — heat lost, incomplete burning.
