# Alkanes — Summary

Specification points 4.19–4.22 · Topic 4 Organic chemistry.

## Essential facts

- General formula **CₙH₂ₙ₊₂**.
- **Saturated**: only C–C single bonds, no C=C.
- Methane CH₄ · ethane CH₃CH₃ · propane CH₃CH₂CH₃ · butane CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₃ · pentane
  CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂CH₃. Every C has 4 single bonds.
- With a halogen in **UV light**: substitution of **one H** by one halogen atom.
  CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl; CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ + X₂ → CₙH₂ₙ₊₁X + HX.

## Common traps

- No UV light, no reaction.
- Two products: halogenoalkane **and** hydrogen halide.
