## Marking guidance

*Written for this revision pack — not an official Cambridge mark scheme.*

1. Energy from a source that is naturally replaced (1), so it will not run out (1).
2. Renewable: geothermal, tidal, biomass; non-renewable: nuclear, gas, coal. (3 for all six, 2 for four–five, 1 for two–three.)
3. Any two: HEP, tidal, wave.
4. Renewable if trees regrow or are replanted as fast as they are cut (1); non-renewable if cut faster than they regrow (1), e.g. around growing settlements in LICs, causing deforestation (1).
5. Coal is burned (1); the heat boils water to make steam (1); the steam turns a turbine which drives a generator (1).
6. It uses uranium (1), a mined, finite resource that will run out (1).
