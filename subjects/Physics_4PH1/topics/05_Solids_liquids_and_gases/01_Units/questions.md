## 1. Recall

1. State the SI unit of (a) pressure, (b) density, (c) specific heat capacity. (3)
2. Complete: 1 Pa = 1 ______ per ______. (1)

## 2. Application

3. Convert (a) 45 cm² to m², (b) 250 cm³ to m³, (c) 2.5 kPa to Pa. (3)
4. A liquid has a density of 0.80 g/cm³. Give its density in kg/m³. (1)
5. The temperature of some water rises from 18 °C to 30 °C. State the temperature change in K. (1)

## Answers and marking guidance

> Marking guidance written for this repository, not an official Pearson mark scheme.

1. (a) Pa or N/m² (1); (b) kg/m³ (1); (c) J/kg °C (1).
2. newton … square metre (1).
3. (a) 45 ÷ 10 000 = 0.0045 m² (1); (b) 250 ÷ 1 000 000 = 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ m³ (1); (c) 2500 Pa (1).
4. 800 kg/m³ (1).
5. 12 K (1) — a change of 1 °C equals a change of 1 K.
