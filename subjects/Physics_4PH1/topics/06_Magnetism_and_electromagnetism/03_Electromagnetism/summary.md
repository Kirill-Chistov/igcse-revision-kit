# Electromagnetism — Summary

Specification points 6.8–6.14 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1 · 6.9P–6.11P Paper 2 only.

- A **current produces a magnetic field** around the conductor; reverse the current → field reverses.
- **Field patterns (6.10P):** straight wire — concentric circles, wider apart further out; direction by the **right-hand grip rule**. Flat coil — circles round each side, lines through the centre all the same way. Solenoid — bar-magnet shape outside, **strong uniform** field inside; lines leave the **N** end.
- **Electromagnet (6.9P):** coil of **insulated** wire round a **soft iron core**, d.c. supply + switch. Soft iron → strong magnet that switches off. Stronger: more current, more turns.
- **6.11P:** a moving charged particle feels a force unless moving **parallel** to the field.
- **Why a wire feels a force:** current's own circular field + magnet's field → strong on one side, weak on the other → pushed strong → weak.
- **Left-hand rule:** **F**irst = **F**ield (N→S), se**C**ond = **C**urrent (+→−), thu**M**b = **M**otion.
- **Force bigger:** more current, stronger field. **Reverses:** reverse current or field (both → unchanged). Zero if current parallel to field.
- **d.c. motor:** sides carry opposite currents → up/down forces → turning effect; **split-ring commutator** reverses the coil current every half turn; **brushes** keep contact. Faster: more current, stronger field, more turns.
- **Loudspeaker:** a.c. in coil on a cone in a magnet's field → force reverses with the current → cone vibrates → sound. Bigger current → louder; higher frequency → higher pitch.
