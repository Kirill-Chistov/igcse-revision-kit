# Globalisation and TNCs — Summary

Specification points 9.2.1–9.2.6 · Paper 2.

## Essential facts and ideas

- **Driven by:** cheap, fast transport (containers, jets) · instant ICT · fewer trade barriers ·
  growth of TNCs.
- **Features:** growing trade · global supply chains · FDI and 24-hour finance · powerful TNCs ·
  flows of people and information · global culture · time–space compression · WTO and trade blocs ·
  uneven connection.
- **Impacts, + / −:**
  trade — jobs and cheaper goods / price dependence, HIC job losses, crises spread;
  transport — cheap supply chains and tourism / emissions;
  culture — exchange of ideas / homogenisation;
  communications — instant contact, offshored service jobs / digital divide;
  technology — technology transfer / automation, gains to the skilled.
- **TNC organisation:** HQ + R&D in the home country (HIC) → factories in MICs/LICs (cheap land
  and labour, incentives, ports) → outsourced suppliers → markets worldwide → profits back to HQ.
- **Host benefits:** jobs, FDI, tax, exports, multiplier, skills, infrastructure.
- **Host costs:** profits leave, low wages, top jobs to foreigners, tax breaks, local firms beaten,
  poor conditions, pollution, footloose.
- **Judgement turns on:** government power · local linkages · type of jobs · permanence.

## Definitions

**Globalisation** — the process by which the world's economies, cultures and people become
increasingly connected and interdependent through flows of goods, services, money, people and
information.

**Interdependence** — countries relying on each other, so a change in one affects the others.

**TNC** — a company that operates in more than one country, headquartered in its country of origin.

**FDI** — investment by a company in factories or businesses it owns or controls in another country.

**Time–space compression** — the world effectively shrinking as travel and communication take less
time and cost less.

## Detailed specific example (9.2.6)

**Mongolia + Rio Tinto (Oyu Tolgoi mine).**

- **Place:** landlocked between Russia and China; 3.3 million people; lowest density in the world
  (2.13/km²); >1.5 million in Ulaanbaatar (urban primacy).
- **Globalisation:** Soviet satellite → democracy 1990, free market 1992 → WTO 1997; World Bank,
  ADB ($38 m ecotourism loan 2019), Japan's aid; FDI almost all into mining; mining ≈21% of GDP;
  LIC → lower-middle-income 2020. Concerns: benefits unequal (rural areas), traditional values eroded.
- **Rio Tinto:** British–Australian, 2nd-largest mining TNC, 35 countries, $54 bn revenue (2023).
  Oyu Tolgoi copper–gold mine, southern Gobi, 80 km from China; **66% Rio Tinto / 34% government**;
  found 2001, open pit 2011, first copper 2013, underground 2023.
- **+** profit share; up to 33% of GDP; "Made in Mongolia" local spending $0.5 m (2010) → $888 m (2021);
  heritage protection; water-efficient, safe block caving.
- **−** dependence on mining; environmental impact; benefits seen as going to the TNC, not rural Mongolians.

## Common traps

- A TNC *operates* abroad; exporting alone does not make a company a TNC.
- Evaluate: give both sides, then say what the balance depends on.
