## Essentials

- **Primary:** extracting raw materials from the earth (farming, fishing, mining, oil).
- **Secondary:** converting raw materials into finished or semi-finished goods (manufacturing, construction).
- **Tertiary:** providing services (retail, banking, transport, tourism).
- Wheat farm → flour mill (semi-finished) → bakery → supermarket. Classify by what the business **does**; it can be in several sectors.
