# English — Subject Rules

Two Cambridge International qualifications, kept strictly separate in this folder.

---

## A — Cambridge IGCSE (9–1) First Language English (0990), 2027–2029 syllabus

### Components

| Component | Duration | Marks | Weighting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Paper 1 – Reading** (all candidates) | 2 h | 80 | 50% |
| **Paper 2 – Directed Writing and Composition** *or* **Component 3 – Coursework Portfolio** | 2 h / — | 80 | 50% |
| Component 4 – Speaking and Listening Test *(optional)* | ~10–12 min | 40 | separately endorsed |

> **Centre choice — `/setup` asks the student and records it in `me/profile.md`:** Paper 2 or Component 3? And is Component 4 taken?
> Paper 2 is a timed examination; Component 3 is three coursework assignments, internally
> assessed and externally moderated. The preparation is completely different. Record the answer
> in `me/profile.md`.

### What this means for study material

- **No set texts. No literature knowledge. Nothing to memorise.** Paper 1 texts are unseen.
  All preparation is skill practice, technique and timing.
- Paper 1 is 80% AO1 Reading / 20% AO2 Writing; Paper 2 and Component 3 are the reverse.
- Reading texts come from the **twentieth and/or twenty-first centuries** — literature, fiction,
  non-fiction, discursive essays, reviews, articles.
- Writing must cover **descriptive, narrative, discursive, argumentative and persuasive** styles
  and the text types **letter, report, article, journal, speech, interview, summary**.
- **Summary writing** is its own trainable skill (R5/W-accuracy) and is a common mark-loser.
- Component 4 marks never affect the 9–1 grade; it is separately certificated 1 (high) to 5 (low).
- Official identifiers `R1`–`R5`, `W1`–`W5`, `SL1`–`SL5` are used as specification points here.

## B — Cambridge IGCSE (9–1) Literature in English (0992), **2027** syllabus

### Components — three possible routes

All candidates take **Paper 1 (Poetry and Prose)**, then **one** of:

- **Route A:** Paper 2 – Drama (1 h 30 min, 50%)
- **Route B:** Paper 3 – Drama Open Text (25%) + Paper 4 – Unseen (25%)
- **Route C:** Paper 3 – Drama Open Text (25%) + Component 5 – Coursework (25%)

> **Centre choice — `/setup` asks the student and records it in `me/profile.md`:** which route, and **which set texts** the class is
> studying. Both are centre choices.

### What this means for study material

- **Set texts change every year.** This repository holds the **2027** list. A 2026 or 2028 text
  list is the wrong course.
- The student answers on **one** poetry text and **one** prose text in Paper 1, and on drama
  text(s) according to the route — not on all the options. Do not write material for texts the
  class is not studying.
- **Paper 3 is open text**: a clean copy may be taken in, with **no notes, highlighting or
  underlining**. Annotation practice must therefore happen on a separate copy.
- Editions: any edition is allowed where none is specified, **provided it is not an abridgement
  or simplified version**.
- All four AOs carry **25% each in every component** — knowledge with textual reference (AO1),
  meanings and context (AO2), language/structure/form (AO3), personal response (AO4). Material
  that teaches plot without AO3 and AO4 will not move the grade.
- Local identifiers `P1A.#`, `P1B.#`, `DR.#` are repository-only, not Cambridge codes.

---

## Watch out for (both)

- Material written for **UK GCSE English** (AQA/Edexcel/OCR) — different papers, different AOs,
  different set texts.
- Material for the A*–G Cambridge syllabuses **0500** (First Language English) and **0475**
  (Literature in English). The content overlaps heavily, but grading and some assessment details
  differ; the 9–1 codes **0990** and **0992** are the correct ones.
