# Forces and motion — units — Summary

Specification points 1.1–1.2P · Topic 1 Forces and motion · 1.2P is Paper 2 only.

## Units

| Quantity | Unit |
| --- | --- |
| mass | kg |
| distance, extension | m |
| time | s |
| speed, velocity | m/s |
| acceleration | m/s² |
| force, weight | N |
| gravitational field strength | N/kg (= m/s²; Earth 10 N/kg) |
| moment [P2] | N m |
| momentum [P2] | kg m/s |

## Conversions

g → kg ÷ 1000 · cm → m ÷ 100 · km → m × 1000 · min → s × 60 · h → s × 3600 · km/h → m/s ÷ 3.6

## Common traps

- Mass in kg, never g, in *F* = *ma* and *p* = *mv*.
- Moment is N m, not N/m or J; momentum is kg m/s.
