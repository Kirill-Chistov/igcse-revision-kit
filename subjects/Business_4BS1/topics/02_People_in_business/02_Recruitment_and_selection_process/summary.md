# Recruitment and selection process — Summary

Specification points 2.2.1–2.2.4.

## Types of employment (+ / −)

- **Full-time:** continuity, commitment / highest wage cost, inflexible.
- **Part-time:** covers busy times, lower cost / more staff to manage, less continuity.
- **Job share:** one job, two people — two skill sets, cover for absence / needs good communication.
- **Casual / seasonal / temporary:** matches demand, no long-term commitment / less trained,
  less loyal, repeated recruitment. Temporary = fixed end date; permanent = none.

## Process

Vacancy → job description + person specification → advert (internal/external) → applications
(form or CV) → **shortlisting** → **interviews** (tests) → offer (references) → induction.
Recruitment = attracting applicants; selection = choosing one.

## Documents

- **Job description** — the job: title, duties, reports to, pay, hours, place.
- **Person specification** — the person: qualifications, skills, experience, qualities
  (essential / desirable).
- **Application form** — business's questions; easy to compare.
- **CV** — applicant's own summary; harder to compare.

## Internal vs external

- **Internal:** cheap, quick, known person, motivates / no new ideas, leaves another vacancy.
- **External:** bigger pool, new ideas / costly, slow, unknown person.
- **Shortlisting** compares applications with the person specification → saves time and cost.
- **Interviews:** see personality and skills / costly, some interview well but work badly, bias.

## Legal controls

- **Equal opportunities:** no discrimination by gender, race, disability, religion, sexual
  preference, age → + merit, diversity, reputation / − adaptation and training costs,
  fines if broken.
- **Minimum wage:** lowest legal hourly pay → + motivation, recruitment / − higher wage costs →
  higher prices, lower profit, fewer staff.
