# Atomic structure — Summary

Specification points 1.14–1.17 · Topic 1 Principles of chemistry.

## Essential facts

- **Atom**: smallest particle of an element that can take part in a chemical reaction.
  **Molecule**: two or more atoms joined by covalent bonds.
- Proton: nucleus, mass 1, charge +1. Neutron: nucleus, mass 1, charge 0. Electron: shells,
  mass 1/1836, charge −1. Atoms are neutral (protons = electrons).
- **Atomic number** = protons. **Mass number** = protons + neutrons. Neutrons = mass − atomic
  number.
- **Isotopes**: same protons, different neutrons; same chemical properties.
- **Ar**: weighted mean mass of an element's atoms relative to 1/12 of a carbon-12 atom.

## Calculation

```
Ar = Σ(mass number × % abundance) ÷ 100      e.g. Cl: (35×75 + 37×25) ÷ 100 = 35.5
```

## Common traps

- Divide by the total abundance if it is not 100.
- Ar is usually not a whole number; it lies nearer the most abundant isotope.
