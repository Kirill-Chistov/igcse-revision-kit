## Why cash matters

- Pays suppliers, overheads, employees; without it → **insolvency** (cannot pay debts when due) → failure.
- **Cash ≠ profit:** profit is counted when the sale is made; cash when money moves. Credit sales,
  inventory and new machinery can leave a profitable business short of cash; loans bring cash but no profit.

## Calculations

- Net cash flow = total inflows − total outflows
- Closing balance = opening balance + net cash flow
- Next month's opening balance = this month's closing balance
- Brackets = negative.

## Interpreting

- Negative closing balance → overdrawn → arrange overdraft/loan in advance, or delay spending, cut
  costs, collect debts sooner.
- Forecast plus: spots shortages early, plans big payments, helps get a loan, allows control.
- Forecast minus: estimates can be wrong; unexpected events; less accurate further ahead.
