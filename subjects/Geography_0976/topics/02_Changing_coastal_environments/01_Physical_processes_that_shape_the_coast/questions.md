## Answers and marking guidance

*Marking points written for this repository — not an official Cambridge mark scheme.*

**1.1** Swash: water moving up the beach after a wave breaks (1). Backwash: water returning down
the beach to the sea, under gravity (1).

**1.2** Hydraulic action, corrasion, attrition, corrosion (1 each).

**1.3** The distance of open water over which the wind blows (1).

**1.4** Saltation (1).

**2.1** Waves hit the cliff with force (1); water forces air into cracks/joints (1); compressed
air increases pressure and widens the cracks (1); rock breaks off (1). Max 3.

**2.2** Any two contrasts, 2 marks each (1 per side): constructive low / destructive high and
steep; long / short wavelength; 6–8 / 10–14 per minute; swash stronger / backwash stronger;
build beach up / remove material.

**2.3** Wave refraction spreads wave energy out in bays (1); water is sheltered and low-energy (1);
waves lose the energy to carry sediment, so it is dropped (1); constructive waves build a beach (1).
Max 3.

**3.1** Storms bring destructive waves (1); backwash stronger than swash (1) drags sand offshore (1);
frequent, high-energy waves (1). Max 3.

**3.2** Prevailing wind from the west/south-west (1); waves approach at an angle so swash carries the
pebble up the beach at an angle (1); backwash pulls it straight back down under gravity (1);
repeated zigzag gives net movement east = longshore drift (1).

**4.1** Prevailing wind drives waves at an angle (1); swash moves sediment up the beach at that
angle (1); backwash moves it straight down, at right angles to the shore (1); repeated → zigzag,
net movement along the coast (1). A correctly labelled diagram can earn all 4.

**4.2** Water shallower off headlands, so waves slow there first (1); crests bend/refract (1);
energy concentrated on headlands → erosion (1); energy spread in bays → deposition/beaches (1).

**4.3** High and steep (1); short wavelength / frequent, 10–14 per minute (1); backwash stronger
than swash (1); remove material, eroding the beach (1); associated with storms (1). Max 3.
