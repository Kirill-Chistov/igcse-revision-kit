# Business 4BS1 — Subject Rules

**Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Business (4BS1)**.

## Paper structure

| Paper | Code | Duration | Marks | Weighting | Business context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper 1 | 4BS1/01 | 1 h 30 min | 80 | 50% | A **small** business (up to 49 employees), local/national market |
| Paper 2 | 4BS1/02 | 1 h 30 min | 80 | 50% | A **large** business (250+ employees), multinational/global market |

Each paper has four compulsory 20-mark questions: multiple-choice, short-answer, data-response
and open-ended. Calculators are allowed. Papers may be sat on paper or onscreen.

## The content is not split between papers

All five content sections are examinable on **both** papers. Only the **business context**
differs. Study material must therefore be able to apply every concept to both a small local
business and a large multinational — worked examples should routinely do both.

## No tiering

All students sit the same two papers.

## Assessment objectives — this drives how material is written

| AO | Description | Weighting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AO1 | Recall, select and communicate knowledge and understanding | 27–28% |
| AO2 | Apply knowledge and understanding in specific contexts | 38–39% |
| AO3 | Select, organise and interpret business information from sources | 22–23% |
| AO4 | Evaluate to make reasoned judgements and draw conclusions | 11–12% |

**A maximum of 15% of marks assess knowledge recall.** Around 70% of marks need application,
analysis or evaluation. Topics must therefore teach *how to apply and judge*, with practice on
data response and extended evaluation, not lists of definitions.

## Quantitative content

Section 3 (Business finance) is calculation-heavy and is assessable: cash flow forecasts,
break-even, costs, statements of comprehensive income, statements of financial position, and
accounting ratios. Students must be able to calculate **and interpret** each one.

## Watch out for

- UK GCSE Business (Edexcel 1BS0) material — different specification and assessment structure.
- Cambridge IGCSE Business Studies (0450/0986) material.
- This specification is **Issue 2, February 2026**; older editions of textbooks may not match.
