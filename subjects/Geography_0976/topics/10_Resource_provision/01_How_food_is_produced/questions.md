## Marking guidance

*Written for this revision pack — not an official Cambridge mark scheme.*

1. Farming to produce food mainly for the farmer and their family, with little or none to sell.
2. Arable = growing crops (1); pastoral = rearing animals (1).
3. Aeroponics.
4. Physical input: rainfall, soil depth; human input: fertiliser; process: harvesting; output: milk, profit. (3 for all six, 2 for four–five, 1 for two–three.)
5. Fish produce waste (1) which bacteria convert into nitrates that feed the plants (1); the plants clean the water, which returns to the fish tank, so both fish and plants are produced (1).
6. Any two, developed: high capital cost of equipment/greenhouses; high energy use for pumps and lighting; technical knowledge needed — so only worth it if produce is sold for profit.
7. Subsistence: mainly family labour, simple tools, seed saved from last harvest, little capital (1–2). Commercial: large capital inputs — machinery, fertiliser, pesticides, bought seed, hired labour (1–2).
8. Level marking. *Level 1 (1–2):* lists inputs or outputs. *Level 2 (3–4):* physical and human inputs, named processes and outputs linked in sequence. *Level 3 (5–6):* all four elements with examples, including feedback (profit reinvested in machinery/seed, manure reused) and why outputs are sold.
