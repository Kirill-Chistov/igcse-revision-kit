# Electricity — units — Questions

Specification point 2.1 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1.

> These questions and the marking guidance are **written for this repository by AI**. They are not
> official Pearson questions and the guidance is not an official mark scheme.

## Questions

1. Give the unit and unit symbol of (a) charge, (b) resistance, (c) power. (3)
2. Convert: (a) 450 mA to A, (b) 3.5 kW to W, (c) 2.2 kΩ to Ω, (d) 4 minutes to s. (4)
3. State what quantity is measured in joules per coulomb, and its unit. (2)
4. A student writes "*E* = 2 × 230 × 5 = 2300 J" for a current of 2 A at 230 V for 5 minutes. Identify the mistake and give the correct answer. (2)

## Answers and marking guidance

One mark per creditable point (1).

1. (a) coulomb, C (1); (b) ohm, Ω (1); (c) watt, W (1).
2. (a) 0.45 A (1); (b) 3500 W (1); (c) 2200 Ω (1); (d) 240 s (1).
3. Voltage (1); volt, V (1).
4. Time was not converted to seconds (1); *E* = 2 × 230 × 300 = 138 000 J (1).
