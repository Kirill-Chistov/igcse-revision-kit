## Essentials

- **Aim** = long-term goal. **Objective** = specific, measurable target that helps achieve the aim.
- **Financial:** survival, profit, sales, market share, financial security.
- **Non-financial:** social objectives, personal satisfaction, challenge, independence and control.
- Social objectives: + ethical customers, reputation, easier recruitment; − higher costs, can conflict with profit.
- Typical path: start-up → survival; growing → sales, profit, market share; large → market share, financial security, social objectives.
- Objectives change because of **market conditions, technology, performance, legislation, internal reasons**.

## Traps

- Survival is not only for new businesses — also in a recession or when a strong rival arrives.
