## Light

- Light is **transverse**; it reflects and refracts.
- **Reflection:** *i* = *r*, both from the **normal** (drawn at 90° to the surface).
- **Refraction:** into glass (slower) → towards the normal; out → away; along the normal → no bend.
  Rectangular block: emerges **parallel**, shifted sideways.
- **Ray diagrams:** ruler, arrows on rays, normal at the point of incidence, angles from the normal.
- **Practical (3.17):** ray box, draw round block, two crosses per ray, join, protractor.
  Semi-circular block: aim at centre of flat face, no bend at curved side. Prism: turned towards
  base, white light → spectrum.
- ***n* = sin *i* / sin *r*** (recall); *i* in air; no unit; *n* > 1.
- **Practical (3.19):** measure *r* for ~6 values of *i*; plot sin *i* (y) vs sin *r* (x) —
  gradient = *n*.
- **Critical angle *c*:** angle of incidence in the denser material giving *r* = 90°.
  ***sin c* = 1/*n*** (recall); glass *n* = 1.5 → *c* ≈ 42°.
- **TIR** needs denser → less dense **and** *i* > *c*. Optical fibres: repeated TIR carries light
  and information along the fibre. 45° prisms: TIR at 45° (> 42°) turns light 90° (periscope) or
  180° (binoculars, reflectors).

## Sound

- Sound is **longitudinal**; it reflects (echo) and refracts (speed change between media).
- **Hearing range 20–20 000 Hz.** (P2)
- **Speed of sound (P2):** two microphones *d* apart + oscilloscope → *v* = *d*/*t*; or echo from a
  wall ≥ 50 m, time 20 claps → *v* = 2*d* / (time for one echo). ≈ 330–340 m/s.
- **Oscilloscope (P2):** microphone turns sound into a voltage of the same frequency; screen shows
  voltage vs time. *T* = divisions per wave × time-base; *f* = 1/*T*.
- **Higher frequency** of vibration → **higher pitch** (more waves on screen); **larger amplitude**
  → **louder** (taller trace). (P2)

## Common traps

- *i* is always the angle in air in *n* = sin *i* / sin *r*.
- Critical angle is an angle of **incidence**.
- Echo: sound travels **2*d***.
- The oscilloscope trace is a voltage–time graph — sound is still longitudinal.
