## 1. The units of Topic 1 (1.1, 1.2P)

| Quantity | Unit | Symbol |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mass | kilogram | kg |
| distance, displacement, extension | metre | m |
| time | second | s |
| speed, velocity | metre/second | m/s |
| acceleration | metre/second² | m/s² |
| force (including weight) | newton | N |
| gravitational field strength, *g* | newton/kilogram | N/kg |
| moment of a force **[P2]** | newton metre | N m |
| momentum **[P2]** | kilogram metre/second | kg m/s |

Each unit comes from the equation that defines the quantity: (m/s) ÷ s = m/s²; N × m = N m;
kg × m/s = kg m/s.

N/kg and m/s² are the same unit: on Earth *g* = 10 N/kg, so an object falling with no air
resistance accelerates at 10 m/s².

## 2. Convert before you substitute

The equations in this topic only work in kg, m and s. Convert first:

| From | To | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| g | kg | ÷ 1000 (450 g = 0.45 kg) |
| cm | m | ÷ 100 (15 cm = 0.15 m) |
| mm | m | ÷ 1000 |
| km | m | × 1000 |
| minutes, hours | s | × 60, × 3600 |
| km/h | m/s | ÷ 3.6 (72 km/h = 72 000 m ÷ 3600 s = 20 m/s) |

::: traps
| Wrong | Right |
| --- | --- |
| Mass in grams in *F* = *m* × *a* or *p* = *m* × *v*. | Mass in **kg**. |
| Acceleration written as m/s. | **m/s²** (m/s per second). |
| Moment in N/m or in J. | **N m** (newton metre). |
| Momentum in N or kg/m/s. | **kg m/s**. |
:::
