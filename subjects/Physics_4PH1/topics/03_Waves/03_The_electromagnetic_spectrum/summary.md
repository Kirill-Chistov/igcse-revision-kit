## Essential facts

- Light is part of a **continuous** EM spectrum; all EM waves are transverse and travel at the
  **same speed in free space**, 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s.
- Order (λ decreasing, *f* increasing): **radio, microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-ray,
  gamma**. Visible: **red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet** (red = longest λ).
- Higher frequency → more energy → more dangerous.

## Uses

| Radiation | Use — reason |
| --- | --- |
| Radio | broadcasting, communications — travel far, carry signals |
| Microwave | cooking — absorbed by water in food; satellites — pass through atmosphere |
| Infrared | heaters — absorbed, warms; night vision — detects warm bodies |
| Visible | optical fibres — TIR along glass; photography — focused onto sensor/film |
| Ultraviolet | fluorescent lamps — coating absorbs UV, emits visible light |
| X-ray | internal structure — pass through soft tissue, absorbed by bone/metal |
| Gamma | sterilising food and medical equipment — kills microorganisms, penetrates packaging |

## Dangers and protection

| Radiation | Effect | Protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microwave | internal heating of body tissue | metal case and mesh door of oven |
| Infrared | skin burns | distance, heat-resistant gloves |
| Ultraviolet | damage to surface cells, blindness | sunscreen, cover skin, UV sunglasses |
| Gamma (X-ray) | mutation, cancer | lead shielding, short time, large distance |
