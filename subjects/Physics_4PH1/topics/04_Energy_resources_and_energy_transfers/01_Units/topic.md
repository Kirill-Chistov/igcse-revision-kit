# Units

## Specification coverage

| Point | Section |
| --- | --- |
| 4.1 use the units kg, J, m, m/s, m/s², N, s, W | §1, §2 |

## 1. The units (4.1)

| Quantity | Unit | Symbol |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mass *m* | kilogram | kg |
| energy, work done *W* | joule | J |
| distance *d*, height *h* | metre | m |
| speed *v* | metre/second | m/s |
| acceleration | metre/second² | m/s² |
| force *F*, weight | newton | N |
| time *t* | second | s |
| power *P* | watt | W |

Two links come straight from the equations in this topic:

- **1 J = 1 N m** — the work done when a force of 1 N moves an object 1 m in the direction of the
  force (*W* = *F* × *d*).
- **1 W = 1 J/s** — one joule transferred every second (*P* = *W* / *t*).

Gravitational field strength *g* is in N/kg (→ 1.1); on Earth use the value the question gives,
usually 10 N/kg.

## 2. Conversions

Convert to the units above **before** substituting; the answer then comes out in J, W or m/s.

| Given as | Convert | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kJ | × 10³ → J | 4.5 kJ = 4500 J |
| MJ, MW | × 10⁶ → J, W | 3 MW = 3 × 10⁶ W |
| GW | × 10⁹ → W | 1.2 GW = 1.2 × 10⁹ W |
| g | ÷ 1000 → kg | 250 g = 0.25 kg |
| cm | ÷ 100 → m | 80 cm = 0.80 m |
| minutes, hours | × 60, × 3600 → s | 5 min = 300 s |

## Common traps

| Wrong | Right |
| --- | --- |
| Mass in grams in ½*mv*² or *mgh*. | Convert to kg first. |
| Time in minutes in *P* = *W* / *t*. | Convert to seconds; power is then in W. |
| Power written in J. | Power is in **W** (J/s); energy is in J. |
| "kw", "Mw". | kW and MW: lower-case k, capital M and W (mW is a milliwatt). |
