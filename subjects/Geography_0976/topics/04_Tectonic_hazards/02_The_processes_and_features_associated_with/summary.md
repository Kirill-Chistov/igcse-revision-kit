# The processes and features associated with earthquakes and volcanoes — Summary

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Specification points | 4.2.1–4.2.6 |
| Paper | Paper 1 — Physical Geography |

## Processes (4.2.1)

- **Every earthquake:** friction locks plates → pressure builds → rock suddenly slips → energy
  released as seismic waves.
- **Divergent:** plates apart → pressure falls → mantle partly melts → magma rises → new crust,
  gentle eruptions.
- **Destructive:** denser oceanic plate subducts → friction (EQ shallow to deep) → plate melts →
  magma rises → explosive strato-volcanoes; trench.
- **Collision:** two light continental plates → crumple into fold mountains; EQ, no volcanoes.
- **Conservative:** slide past → lock, stick, slip → EQ; no volcanoes.

## Earthquakes (4.2.2)

- **Focus:** point underground where rock breaks. **Epicentre:** point on the surface directly above.
- **Seismic waves:** energy spreading from the focus; strongest at the epicentre.
- Shallow focus (< ≈70 km) → stronger shaking.

## Volcanoes (4.2.3–4.2.5)

| | Strato (composite cone) | Shield | Cinder cone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shape | steep, tall cone | wide, gentle | small, steep, one vent |
| Magma | sticky, gassy | runny basalt | gassy; cinders |
| Eruption | explosive | gentle | short-lived |
| Example | Mayon | Mauna Loa | Parícutin |

- **Active** (recent, will erupt) · **Dormant** (long quiet, could erupt) · **Extinct** (≥10 000
  years, will not erupt).
- Features: crater, main vent, magma, magma chamber, secondary cone (side vent).
  Magma underground → lava at the surface.

## Hazards (4.2.6)

- Lava flow (slow, property) · ash fall (spreads far; roofs, crops, aircraft) · tephra (all ejected
  fragments) · volcanic rocks/bombs (near vent) · pyroclastic flow (hot, >100 km/h, deadliest) ·
  lahar (mudflow along valleys) · toxic gases (CO₂ in hollows, SO₂).
- **Speed** = escape time; **size** = area and people affected; **frequency** = preparedness;
  **spread** = how far.

## Common traps

- Epicentre ≠ focus. Magma ≠ lava. Dormant ≠ safe.
