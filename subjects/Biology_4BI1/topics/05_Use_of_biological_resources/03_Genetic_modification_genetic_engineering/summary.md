# Genetic modification — Summary

**Specification points 5.12–5.16**

- **Restriction enzyme**: cuts DNA at a specific base sequence → sticky ends. Same enzyme on gene
  and plasmid → matching ends. **Ligase**: joins them → **recombinant DNA**.
- **Vectors** carry recombinant DNA into cells: **plasmids** (taken up by bacteria, copied when they
  divide) and **viruses** (infect a cell, inject the gene).
- **Insulin**: cut insulin gene from human DNA; cut plasmid; ligase joins; plasmid into bacterium;
  grow in fermenter — bacteria multiply rapidly, all make human insulin; extract and purify.
- **GM crops**: herbicide resistance (weeds killed, crop not), pest resistance (own toxin, less
  insecticide), drought/disease resistance, better nutrition (vitamin A rice). Concerns: gene
  spread to weeds, harm to other insects, unknown long-term effects, cost.
- **Transgenic**: transfer of genetic material from one species to a different species.
