# The Periodic Table — Questions

Specification points 1.18–1.24 · Topic 1 Principles of chemistry. Marks in brackets. Use a
Periodic Table.

> All questions and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository; they are not
> official Pearson questions or mark schemes.

## 1. Recall

1. In what order are the elements arranged in the Periodic Table? (1)
2. What name is given to a horizontal row of the Periodic Table? (1)
3. Give the electronic configurations of aluminium, oxygen and calcium. (3)

## 2. Understanding and explanation

4. An element has the electronic configuration 2.8.5. State its group and period, and identify it. (3)
5. Explain why lithium, sodium and potassium have similar chemical properties. (2)
6. Explain why argon is unreactive. (2)
7. Element X is a solid that does not conduct electricity. Its oxide dissolves in water to give a
   solution of pH 3. Is X a metal or a non-metal? Give two reasons. (3)

## 3. Application

8. Describe how you could use an element's oxide to decide whether the element is a metal. (3)
9. Using only its position, identify magnesium as a metal or non-metal and give its electronic
   configuration. (2)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. Increasing atomic number (1).
2. Period (1).
3. Al 2.8.3 (1); O 2.6 (1); Ca 2.8.8.2 (1).
4. Group 5 (1); Period 3 (1); phosphorus (1).
5. They are all in Group 1 / all have one outer electron (1); chemical reactions involve the outer
   electrons (1).
6. Full outer shell (of 8 electrons) (1); does not lose, gain or share electrons / stable (1).
7. Non-metal (1); does not conduct electricity (1); its oxide is acidic (1).
8. Burn the element in oxygen (1); add the oxide to water with universal indicator / test with
   pH paper (1); alkaline (pH > 7) → metal; acidic (pH < 7) → non-metal (1). (Accept: a basic oxide
   reacts with / neutralises an acid.)
9. Metal — left of the metal/non-metal line / Group 2 (1); 2.8.2 (1).
