# Electromagnetic induction — Summary

Specification points 6.15–6.20 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1 · 6.17P–6.20P Paper 2 only.

- **Voltage induced** when a conductor **cuts field lines** or the **field through a coil changes**. Complete circuit → current.
- Nothing changing (magnet still) → **no voltage**. Reverse the motion or the field → voltage reverses.
- **Bigger induced voltage:** move/turn **faster**, **stronger** magnet, **more turns**.
- **Generators:** coil turned in a field (**slip rings + brushes**) or magnet turned in a coil → voltage reverses each half turn → **a.c.** Peak when coil sides cut lines at right angles; zero when moving along them. Faster → bigger peak **and** higher frequency.
- **Transformer (6.17P):** primary and secondary coils on a **soft iron core**, not connected. a.c. in primary → changing field in core → changing field through secondary → alternating voltage induced. **d.c. does not work.**
- **Step-up:** *N*s > *N*p → voltage up, current down. **Step-down:** *N*s < *N*p.
- **Recall:** *V*p / *V*s = *N*p / *N*s and *V*p*I*p = *V*s*I*s (100% efficient).
- **Transmission (6.18P):** step-up to very high voltage → small current → less heating of cables → less energy wasted; step-down to 230 V for safe use.
