# Gases in the atmosphere — Summary

Specification points 2.9–2.14 · Topic 2 Inorganic chemistry.

## Essential facts

- Dry air: **N₂ 78%, O₂ 21%, Ar 0.9%, CO₂ 0.04%**.
- % O₂: a substance removes O₂ from a trapped volume of air; decrease in volume = O₂.
  **% O₂ = (start − final) ÷ start × 100**.
  - damp **iron wool** in an inverted measuring cylinder over water; ~1 week, until level constant;
  - **phosphorus** burning in a bell jar over water (4P + 5O₂ → P₄O₁₀), water rises ~1/5;
  - **copper** heated between gas syringes (2Cu + O₂ → 2CuO, turns black), 100 → 79 cm³;
    **cool before reading**.
  - Result < 21%: not all O₂ reacted, or read while warm.
- Combustion: **Mg** bright white flame → white MgO (basic); **H₂** → water; **S** blue flame →
  SO₂, choking (acidic).
- Metal carbonate → metal oxide + CO₂: **CuCO₃ → CuO + CO₂** (green → black; limewater milky).
  Remove delivery tube before stopping heating (suck-back).
- CO₂ is a **greenhouse gas** (absorbs heat from Earth's surface); more CO₂ (fossil fuels) **may
  contribute to climate change**.

## Common traps

- Third most abundant gas is **argon**, not CO₂.
- Water rises because O₂ is **used up**, not dissolved.
