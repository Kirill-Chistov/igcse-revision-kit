# Alkenes — Questions

Specification points 4.23–4.28 · Topic 4 Organic chemistry. Marks in brackets.

> All questions and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository; they are not
> official Pearson questions or mark schemes.

## 1. Recall

1. Give the functional group of the alkenes. (1)
2. Give the general formula of the alkenes. (1)
3. Explain why alkenes are described as unsaturated. (1)

## 2. Formulae and names

4. Draw the displayed formula of propene. (1)
5. Draw and name the two unbranched isomers of C₄H₈. (4)
6. Give the structural formula of but-1-ene. (1)

## 3. Reactions

7. Ethene reacts with bromine. Give the displayed formula and name of the product. (2)
8. Write an equation for the reaction of propene with bromine. (1)
9. Describe how bromine water can be used to show which of two liquids is hexane and which is
   hex-1-ene. (3)
10. Explain why the reaction of ethene with bromine is an addition reaction. (1)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. C=C / carbon–carbon double bond (1).
2. CₙH₂ₙ (1).
3. They contain a C=C double bond (1).
4. CH₂=CH–CH₃ drawn with all bonds; C=C between carbons 1 and 2; six H (1).
5. But-1-ene: C=C between C1 and C2 (1) named (1); but-2-ene: C=C between C2 and C3 (1) named (1).
6. CH₂=CHCH₂CH₃ (1).
7. Two C single-bonded, each with one Br and two H (1); 1,2-dibromoethane (1).
8. C₃H₆ + Br₂ → C₃H₆Br₂ (1).
9. Add bromine water to each and shake (1); hex-1-ene turns it from orange to colourless (1); with
   hexane it stays orange (1).
10. Two molecules join to form one product / bromine adds across the C=C (1).
