# Characteristics of the tropical rainforest ecosystem — Summary

Specification points 3.3.1–3.3.4 · Cambridge IGCSE (9–1) Geography 0976 · Paper 1.

## Distribution (3.3.1)

- A belt either side of the **equator**, mostly within ~10° N and S, all between the tropics.
- Largest: **Amazon Basin**, **Congo Basin**, **South-East Asia**. Smaller: Central America, West Africa,
  Madagascar, NE Australia. Found where it is hot and wet all year.

## Equatorial climate (3.3.2)

- **Latitude** 0–10°: 12-hour days all year, no seasons.
- **Overhead sun** at the equinoxes, always high → concentrated energy, intense heating.
- **Temperature** 26–28 °C every month; annual range under 3 °C (smaller than the daily range).
- **Low pressure**: heated air rises; trade winds converge (ITCZ).
- **Convectional rainfall**: heating → evaporation and transpiration → moist air rises → cools,
  condenses → cumulonimbus → afternoon storms. Over **2,000 mm** a year, no dry season, humid.

## Characteristics and structure (3.3.3)

- Layers: **emergents** (to 50 m) · **canopy** (25–35 m, most light and animals) · **under-canopy**
  (10–20 m) · **shrub layer** (to 5 m) · **forest floor** (about 2% of light).
- ~**6%** of land, over **half** of all species; evergreen; huge biomass; poor, leached red latosols.

## Interrelationships and adaptations (3.3.4)

- Nutrients: stored mainly in **biomass**; leaf fall all year → decomposed in weeks → taken up fast by
  shallow roots; rain leaches the rest.
- Water: rain → canopy interception → soil → transpiration → humid air → more rain.
- Canopy shades the floor; gaps let young trees grow. Animals pollinate and spread seeds.
- Plants: **buttress roots** (support, shallow roots) · **drip tips** (shed rain) · **lianas** and
  **epiphytes** (reach light) · thin smooth bark.
- Animals: **sloth** (slow metabolism, algae camouflage) · **spider monkey** (prehensile tail) ·
  **poison dart frog** (warning colours).

## Common traps

- Lush forest ≠ fertile soil: nutrients are in the plants.
- Rain comes as afternoon storms, not all day.
- No temperature seasons at the equator.
