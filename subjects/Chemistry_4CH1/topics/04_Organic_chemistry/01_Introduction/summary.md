# Introduction to organic chemistry — Summary

Specification points 4.1–4.6 · Topic 4 Organic chemistry.

## Essential facts

- **Hydrocarbon**: compound of hydrogen and carbon only.
- Formulae (butane): molecular C₄H₁₀ · empirical C₂H₅ (simplest ratio) · general CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ (whole
  series) · structural CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₃ · displayed (every atom and bond drawn).
- **Homologous series**: same general formula, same functional group, similar chemical properties,
  trend in physical properties, members differ by CH₂.
- **Functional group**: atom/group of atoms that determines the characteristic reactions.
- **Isomers**: same molecular formula, different structural/displayed formulae.
- Stems: meth 1 · eth 2 · prop 3 · but 4 · pent 5 · hex 6. Endings: -ane, -ene, -anol, -anoic acid,
  alkyl alkanoate.
- Number from the end giving the lowest number (but-1-ene, propan-1-ol); prefixes methyl-, chloro-,
  bromo-, fluoro-; di-/tri-/tetra- for repeats.
- Isomers: longest chain first, then branches on inner carbons, then move C=C; discard duplicates.
  C₄H₁₀: 2 isomers; C₅H₁₂: 3.
- **Substitution**: atom replaced (two products). **Addition**: two molecules → one (C=C opens).
  **Combustion**: with oxygen, releases heat.

## Common traps

- A bent or flipped chain is not a new isomer; a branch on an end carbon just lengthens the chain.
- Alcohols, acids and esters are not hydrocarbons.
