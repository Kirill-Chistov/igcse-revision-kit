# Organisation structure and employees — Summary

Specification points 2.5.1–2.5.3.

- **Organisational chart:** roles, who reports to whom, lines of authority and communication.
- **Chain of command:** line of authority top → bottom. **Span of control:** number of people
  reporting directly to one manager.
- **Tall:** many levels, narrow spans, long chain → close control, promotion chances / slow,
  distorted communication, costly managers. Large businesses.
- **Flat:** few levels, wide spans, short chain → fast communication, lower cost, responsibility /
  overloaded managers, few promotions. Small businesses.
- **Centralised:** head office decides → consistency, control / slow, ignores local needs.
- **Decentralised:** authority delegated to branches/regions → local fit, speed, motivation /
  inconsistency, less control. Multinationals.
- **Compliance** = following laws, rules, instructions. **Accountability** = answerable to the
  person above. Directors → shareholders; managers → directors; supervisors → managers;
  operatives → supervisors.
- **Delegation:** authority passed down, accountability stays → frees managers, motivates /
  mistakes, overload.

## Functional areas

- **HR:** workforce planning, recruitment and selection, training, health and safety, welfare,
  employment issues, industrial relations, disciplinary and grievance procedures, dismissal,
  unfair dismissal, redundancy.
- **Finance:** wages/salaries, cash-flow forecasting, budgets, accounting.
- **Marketing:** research, product planning, pricing, promotion, advertising, customer service,
  PR, packaging, distribution.
- **Production:** manufacturing, designing new products, quality control, stock control.
- Disciplinary: verbal → written → final written warning → dismissal. Grievance = employee's
  complaint. Redundancy = job not needed; dismissal = employee's fault; unfair dismissal = no
  fair reason / procedure not followed.

**Traps:** narrow span = tall; delegate authority, not accountability.
