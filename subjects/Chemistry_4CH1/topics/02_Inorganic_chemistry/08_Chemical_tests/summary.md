# Chemical tests — Summary

Specification points 2.44–2.50 · Topic 2 Inorganic chemistry.

## Gases

H₂ lighted splint → squeaky pop · O₂ glowing splint → relights · CO₂ limewater → milky ·
NH₃ damp red litmus → blue · Cl₂ damp blue litmus → red then bleached.

## Flame tests

Clean nichrome loop (conc. HCl, blue flame); dip in acid then solid; edge of blue flame.
**Li⁺ red · Na⁺ yellow · K⁺ lilac · Ca²⁺ orange-red · Cu²⁺ blue-green.**

## Cations (add NaOH(aq))

Cu²⁺ **blue** ppt · Fe²⁺ **green** ppt · Fe³⁺ **orange-brown** ppt.
NH₄⁺: NaOH + **warm** → NH₃ turns damp red litmus blue.

## Anions

- Halides: dilute **HNO₃** then **AgNO₃**: Cl⁻ white, Br⁻ cream, I⁻ yellow.
- Sulfate: dilute **HCl** then **BaCl₂**: white.
- Carbonate: dilute HCl → fizzing, gas turns limewater milky.
- Acid first removes carbonate ions (false precipitate).

## Water

Anhydrous CuSO₄ **white → blue** (water present). Pure water boils at **100 °C** / melts at 0 °C.

## Common traps

- HNO₃ (not HCl) before AgNO₃. Fe²⁺ green, Fe³⁺ orange-brown.
