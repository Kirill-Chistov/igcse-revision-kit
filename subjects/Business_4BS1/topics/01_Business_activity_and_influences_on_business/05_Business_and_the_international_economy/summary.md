## Essentials

- **Globalisation:** world economies becoming more connected through trade and the movement of money, people and ideas.
- Opportunities: bigger market, cheaper materials/production, economies of scale. Threats: foreign competition, exchange-rate risk, cultural/legal differences.
- Multinational — benefits to business: bigger market, lower costs, avoids tariffs, spreads risk.
- Host country — benefits: jobs, tax revenue, investment, skills, choice, exports. Drawbacks: profits leave, local firms close, low-paid jobs, environmental damage, may leave suddenly.

## Calculation

- £1 = $1.25: £ → $ **multiply** by 1.25; $ → £ **divide** by 1.25.

## Exchange rate changes

| | Appreciation | Depreciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exports | dearer abroad → fewer sales | cheaper abroad → more sales |
| Imports | cheaper → lower costs | dearer → higher costs |
| Competitiveness | falls | rises |
