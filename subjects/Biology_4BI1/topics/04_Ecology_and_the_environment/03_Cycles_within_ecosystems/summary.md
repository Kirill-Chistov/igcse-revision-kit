# Cycles within ecosystems — Summary

Specification points 4.10, 4.11B (Paper 2 only).

- **Carbon cycle:** photosynthesis removes CO₂ → carbon compounds in plants → feeding → animals.
  Respiration (plants, animals, decomposers) returns CO₂. Decomposers break down dead matter and
  waste and respire. No decay → fossil fuels → combustion releases CO₂.
- **Nitrogen cycle (P2):**
  - nitrogen-fixing bacteria (root nodules of legumes, soil): N₂ → nitrogen compounds;
  - plants absorb nitrate (active transport) → amino acids → proteins; animals eat them;
  - decomposers: proteins, urea, faeces → ammonium;
  - nitrifying bacteria (need O₂): ammonium → nitrite → nitrate;
  - denitrifying bacteria (waterlogged soil): nitrate → N₂.
- Aerated soil (ploughing, drainage) favours nitrifying bacteria and keeps nitrate in the soil.
