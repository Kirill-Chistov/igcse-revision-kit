# How our energy is produced — Summary

Specification point 10.4.1 · Cambridge IGCSE (9–1) Geography 0976

- **Renewable** = naturally replaced, will not run out (flow): biomass, geothermal, HEP, solar,
  tidal, wave, wind.
- **Non-renewable** = finite stock, will run out: fossil fuels (coal, oil, gas), nuclear (uranium).
- **Fuelwood** = renewable if trees regrow/are replanted as fast as cut; non-renewable (and causes
  deforestation) if cut faster.
- How energy is produced: coal/oil/gas, nuclear, biomass, geothermal → **heat → steam → turbine →
  generator**; HEP, tidal, wave, wind → **moving water/air → turbine → generator**; solar PV →
  electricity directly; fuelwood → burned for cooking and heating.
- Trap: nuclear is low-carbon but **non-renewable**.
