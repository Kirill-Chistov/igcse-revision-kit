# Costs and break-even analysis — Summary

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Specification points | 3.3.1–3.3.3 |
| Paper notes | both papers |

## Formulae

- Revenue = price × quantity sold
- Total costs = fixed costs + (variable cost per unit × output)
- Profit (loss) = revenue − total costs
- **Break-even output = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost per unit)** — units; round up
- Margin of safety = actual output − break-even output

## Terms

- **Fixed costs** don't change with output (rent, salaries); **variable costs** do (materials).
- **Break-even:** total revenue = total costs; no profit, no loss.

## Chart

- Break-even point = where TR crosses TC; profit/loss = vertical gap between TR and TC.
- Price ↑ → TR steeper → break-even output ↓. Fixed costs ↑ → FC and TC shift up → ↑.
  Variable cost per unit ↑ → TC steeper → ↑. Opposite changes, opposite effect.

## Uses and limitations

- Uses: output needed to avoid a loss; "what if" price/cost changes; margin of safety; loan applications.
- Limits: assumes all output sold; constant price and variable cost per unit; fixed costs may
  change; only as good as the estimates; ignores demand and competitors.
