## Essential facts and ideas

- **Development gap** = difference in wealth and quality of life between the richest and poorest
  countries. HIC average GNI per capita ≈ 65 × LIC (2025); the dollar gap still widens.
- **Environmental:** drought, malaria · hazards (rebuilding) · landlocked (transport costs) · poor
  resources.
- **Economic:** primary exports (low, unstable prices) · unfair trade · debt · colonial legacy,
  corruption.
- **Social/political:** poor health · low education · high birth rates, gender inequality · conflict.
- **Cycle of poverty:** low income → little saving/tax → little investment → weak workforce →
  low productivity → low income. HICs run the reverse cycle.

## Global pattern (World Bank, GNI per capita 2025, from July 2026)

- **LIC ≤ $1,175 (25):** 21 in sub-Saharan Africa (Niger, Chad, DR Congo, Ethiopia) + Afghanistan,
  Syria, Yemen, North Korea.
- **MIC $1,176–14,375 (106):** Latin America, Asia (India, China, Vietnam), North Africa, Middle
  East, rest of Africa.
- **HIC > $14,375 (87):** North America, most of Europe incl. Russia, Japan, South Korea,
  Australia, NZ, Gulf states, Chile, Uruguay.
- Brandt line (North/South) now too simple.

## Common traps

- *Describe* a pattern = regions, examples, exceptions, figures — no reasons.
- Each reason needs its mechanism ("debt → less money for schools"), not just a name.
