## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Sustainable development | meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs (Brundtland, 1987) |
| … three-pillar version | balances economic growth, social well-being and environmental protection |
| Strategy / technique | plan or agreement / the method used |
| Appropriate technology | simple, affordable technology locals can build, run and repair |
| Fair trade | guaranteed minimum price + community premium + safe conditions |
| Debt relief | cancelling or reducing debts or interest |
| Tied aid | aid that must be spent on the donor's goods or services |

## Essential facts and ideas

- **SDGs**: 17 UN goals, agreed 2015, target 2030.
- **Social** techniques: schools, girls' education grants, community health workers, wells and hand
  pumps. **Economic**: microfinance, skills training, rural roads. **Environmental**: solar
  mini-grids, drip irrigation, rainwater harvesting, agroforestry.
- **Top-down** (government, big projects: national gains, may miss the poorest, debt) vs
  **bottom-up** (community + NGO, small, appropriate technology: lasts, but small reach).
- **Evaluation:** free trade (big gains; subsidies, TNC profits) · fair trade (stable price; small
  share) · emergency aid (saves lives; symptoms only) · top-down aid (scale; tied, debt, corruption)
  · bottom-up aid (sustainable; small) · debt relief (HIPC from 1996, 35+ countries; frees money;
  conditions, corruption, new borrowing).
- **Judgement:** tackle causes (earnings, debt) > treat symptoms; aid best long-term, untied,
  bottom-up; combine strategies.

## Vietnam (MIC) — core numbers

- **Reasons:** Đổi Mới 1986 → export manufacturing → productive farming → better health,
  education, infrastructure. Extreme poverty 14% → under 4%. Not yet HIC: productivity,
  technology, skills; climate risk; goal HIC by 2045.
- **Economic strategies:** Đổi Mới (GDP per capita < $700 → ~$4,500, 1986–2023) · export
  manufacturing (trade ≈ 170% of GDP) · rural roads 2016–2024 (51,000 km, 11 million people) ·
  VnSAT 2015–2023 (182,000 ha, profits +30%).
- **QoL / SoL strategies:** health insurance 93% (2023), infant mortality 32.6 → 12.1 · electricity
  14% (1993) → nearly all (2019) · education: universal primary early 2000s, enrolment 95% / 80%
  (2024) · Can Tho flood project 2016–2024 (420,000 residents protected).

## Common traps

- Each strategy needs **point → evidence → how it works → link** to economic development, quality
  of life or standard of living.
- "Evaluate" needs strengths, weaknesses **and** a judgement.
