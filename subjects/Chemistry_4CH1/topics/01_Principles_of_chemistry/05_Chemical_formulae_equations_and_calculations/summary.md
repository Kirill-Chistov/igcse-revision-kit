# Chemical formulae, equations and calculations — Summary

Specification points 1.25–1.36 · Topic 1 Principles of chemistry. 1.34C and 1.35C are
**Paper 2 only**.

## Equations

- Balance by changing numbers in front only. State symbols (s), (l), (g), (aq).
- e.g. 4Al(s) + 3O₂(g) → 2Al₂O₃(s).

## Core formulae

```
Mr = sum of Ar (multiply out brackets and ·xH₂O)
amount (mol) = mass ÷ Mr
% yield = actual ÷ theoretical × 100
concentration (mol/dm³) = mol ÷ volume (dm³)        [P2]   cm³ ÷ 1000 = dm³
gas volume (dm³) = mol × 24  (24 000 cm³ per mol at rtp)   [P2]
```

- The **mole** is the unit for amount of substance.
- Reacting quantities: given → **mol** → × mole ratio from equation → mol → required quantity.
- Gas volumes react in the same ratio as moles.

## Formulae

- **Empirical formula**: simplest whole-number ratio of atoms. **Molecular formula**: actual atoms
  in one molecule. Method: mass ÷ Ar → ÷ smallest → whole numbers (double if x.5); molecular =
  empirical × (Mr ÷ empirical-formula mass).
- **Mg combustion**: crucible + lid, lift lid now and then, heat to constant mass; O = oxide − Mg.
- **CuO reduction**: H₂ or CH₄ flow before heating (explosion risk), burn excess, cool in the gas
  stream; O = CuO − Cu.
- **Water of crystallisation**: heat hydrated salt to constant mass; water = mass lost.

## Common traps

- Use mole ratios, not mass ratios. Convert cm³ to dm³. Do not round 2.5 to 3.
