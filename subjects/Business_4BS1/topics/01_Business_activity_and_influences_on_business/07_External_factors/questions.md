## Practice

1. **[1]** Define the term *external factors*.
2. **[1]** State one social factor that could change the demand for fast food.
3. **[3]** Explain one way that technological change can affect a retailer.
4. **[6]** Analyse the impact on a coffee-shop chain of customers becoming more concerned about the environment.
5. **[9]** A juice manufacturer is reviewing its packaging. Option 1: switch to fully recyclable packaging. Option 2: keep its cheaper plastic packaging. Justify which one of these two options it should choose.

## Marking guidance (not an official mark scheme)

1. Influences from outside a business that it cannot control but which affect its decisions.
2. E.g. growing concern about healthy eating.
3. More customers buy online (1) → the retailer needs a website and delivery service (1) → extra costs, but it can reach customers beyond its local area (1).
4. Demand for reusable cups/sustainable coffee → must change products and packaging → higher costs; but greener image → attracts customers → more sales and loyalty.
5. For (e.g. recyclable): reputation with environmentally aware customers, ahead of future laws. Against: higher cost per bottle → higher price or lower profit. Judgement specific to its customers and competitors.
