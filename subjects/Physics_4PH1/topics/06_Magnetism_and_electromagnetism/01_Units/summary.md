# Magnetism and electromagnetism — units — Summary

Specification point 6.1 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1 · both papers.

- **A (ampere)** — current: in wires, coils, motors; *I*p, *I*s in transformers.
- **V (volt)** — voltage: induced voltage; *V*p, *V*s.
- **W (watt)** — power; 1 W = 1 V × 1 A (*P* = *V* × *I*).
- 1 kV = 1000 V · 1 kW = 1000 W · 1 MW = 10⁶ W · 1 mA = 0.001 A. Convert before using *P* = *V* × *I*.
- Turns ratio: same voltage unit top and bottom; the ratio itself has no unit.
