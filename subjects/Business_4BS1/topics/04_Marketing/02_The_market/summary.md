# The market — Summary

Specification points 4.2.1–4.2.3 · both papers (apply to a small or a large business)

## Importance of marketing (4.2.1)

- **Marketing** — identifying, anticipating and satisfying customer needs profitably.
- Satisfy needs → repeat sales. Build relationships (service, complaints, personal offers) →
  trust, recommendations. Loyalty → steady repeat sales, cheaper than winning new customers.
- **Market orientation**: research first, then product → lower risk; research is costly.
  **Product orientation**: product first, then find customers → quality/innovation; risk no one wants it.
- **Market share (%) = business sales ÷ total market sales × 100** (value or volume).
- Market analysis: size, growth, competitors, shares, trends.
- **Niche**: small specific segment → less competition, higher margins; small, risky, attracts rivals.
  **Mass**: whole market → high volume, lower unit costs; fierce competition, costly promotion.

## Responding to change (4.2.2)

- Changes: customer needs · spending patterns · increased competition.
- Response: research the change → adapt the mix (new products, price, quality/service,
  promotion, cut costs, new channels). No response → lost sales, market share, profit.

## Segmentation (4.2.3)

- **Segment** = group with similar characteristics. **Target market** = group aimed at.
- Bases: location · demographics (gender, ethnicity, religion, family size…) · lifestyle ·
  income · age.
- Segment → choose target → tailor the 4Ps.
- + matched needs, less wasted promotion, finds gaps, price by segment.
  − research and extra campaigns cost more; ignores other customers; can misjudge a segment.

## Common traps

- Sales can rise while market share falls, if the market grows faster.
