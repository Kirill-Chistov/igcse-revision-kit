# Acids, bases and salt preparations — Questions

Specification points 2.34–2.43C · Topic 2 Inorganic chemistry. **Q9–Q10 are Paper 2 only.** Marks
in brackets.

> All questions and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository; they are not
> official Pearson questions or mark schemes.

## 1. Recall

1. Which of these are insoluble in water: lead(II) nitrate, barium sulfate, sodium carbonate,
   copper(II) hydroxide, silver chloride? (3)
2. Define an acid and a base in terms of protons. (2)
3. What is an alkali? (1)
4. Name the salt formed when zinc oxide reacts with (a) hydrochloric acid; (b) nitric acid. (2)

## 2. Reactions

5. Write the equation for magnesium carbonate reacting with sulfuric acid. (2)
6. In NH₃ + HCl → NH₄⁺ + Cl⁻, identify the base and explain. (2)

## 3. Practical

7. Describe how to prepare pure, dry crystals of hydrated copper(II) sulfate from copper(II) oxide
   and dilute sulfuric acid. (6)
8. Explain why the solution is not evaporated to dryness in Q7. (1)

## 4. Paper 2

9. Explain why sodium chloride must be made by titration rather than by adding an excess of one
   reactant. (2)
10. Describe how to prepare a pure, dry sample of lead(II) sulfate. Include an ionic equation. (5)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. Barium sulfate (1); copper(II) hydroxide (1); silver chloride (1).
2. Acid: proton donor (1); base: proton acceptor (1).
3. A base that is soluble in water (1).
4. (a) zinc chloride (1); (b) zinc nitrate (1).
5. MgCO₃ + H₂SO₄ → MgSO₄ + H₂O + CO₂ — products (1), balanced (1).
6. NH₃ (1); it accepts a proton / H⁺ (1).
7. Warm the acid (1); add copper(II) oxide until in excess / some remains (1); filter to remove
   excess (1); heat filtrate to crystallisation point / until crystals form on a glass rod (1);
   leave to cool and crystallise (1); filter and dry crystals between filter paper (1).
8. It would remove the water of crystallisation / would not give hydrated crystals (1).
9. Both reactants are soluble (1); excess cannot be removed by filtering, so exact amounts needed
   (1).
10. Mix solutions of lead(II) nitrate and sodium sulfate / sulfuric acid (1); filter (1); wash the
    residue with distilled water (1); dry in a warm oven / between filter papers (1);
    Pb²⁺ + SO₄²⁻ → PbSO₄ (1).
