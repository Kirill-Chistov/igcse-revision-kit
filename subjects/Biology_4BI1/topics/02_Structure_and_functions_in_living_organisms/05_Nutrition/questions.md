# Nutrition — Questions

**Specification points 2.18–2.33B** · questions marked (P2) are Paper 2 only

Marking guidance below is written for this repository; it is not an official mark scheme.

## Plants

1. Write the balanced symbol equation for photosynthesis.
2. Explain the energy change that happens in photosynthesis.
3. A graph of rate against light intensity levels off. Suggest two factors that could be limiting
   the rate at the plateau.
4. Explain why the rate of photosynthesis falls above about 40 °C.
5. Give three adaptations of a leaf for photosynthesis and explain each.
6. A plant is grown in soil lacking magnesium. Explain why its growth is poor.
7. Why is a plant kept in the dark for 48 hours before a starch-test experiment?
8. Why is a leaf boiled in water, then placed in ethanol, during a starch test?
9. In the pondweed experiment, why is a glass tank of water placed between the lamp and the plant?

## Humans

10. Name the nutrient and give one source for: (a) growth and repair, (b) making haemoglobin,
    (c) absorbing calcium.
11. Explain why a pregnant woman needs more energy in her diet.
12. Name the organ that (a) produces bile, (b) stores bile, (c) absorbs most water.
13. Describe how food is moved along the oesophagus.
14. Describe the digestion of starch to glucose, naming the enzymes and where they are produced.
15. Explain two ways in which bile helps the digestion of lipids in the small intestine.

## Exam-style

16. **[4 marks]** *Explain* how the structure of a villus is adapted for the absorption of the
    products of digestion.
17. (P2) **[3 marks]** 0.8 g of a peanut heated 25 cm³ of water from 19 °C to 51 °C. *Calculate*
    the energy content per gram. Show your working.
18. (P2) **[3 marks]** The value in question 17 is lower than the value on the food label.
    *Explain* why, and suggest one improvement.

## Answers and marking guidance

1. 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (formulae correct, 1; balanced, 1).
2. Light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll (1) and converted to chemical energy stored in glucose (1).
3. Carbon dioxide concentration; temperature.
4. Photosynthesis is controlled by enzymes (1); above the optimum the active sites change shape /
   enzymes are denatured (1).
5. Any three with explanation: broad — large area to absorb light; thin — short diffusion distance
   for CO₂; palisade cells packed with chloroplasts near upper surface — absorb most light; air
   spaces — CO₂ diffuses to cells; stomata — CO₂ enters; transparent epidermis — light reaches
   mesophyll; xylem brings water.
6. Magnesium is needed to make chlorophyll (1); less chlorophyll means less light absorbed / less
   photosynthesis (1); less glucose for growth (1).
7. To remove existing starch (1), so any starch found was made during the experiment (1).
8. Boiling kills the cells / breaks membranes so iodine can enter (1); ethanol removes chlorophyll
   so the colour change can be seen (1).
9. To absorb heat from the lamp (1) so temperature stays constant / only light intensity changes (1).
10. (a) protein — meat/fish/eggs/beans; (b) iron — red meat/liver/spinach; (c) vitamin D — oily
    fish/eggs/sunlight.
11. Energy for the growth of the fetus (1) and placenta / larger body mass to support (1).
12. (a) liver; (b) gall bladder; (c) colon (large intestine).
13. Peristalsis (1): circular muscles contract behind the food (1), squeezing it forward while muscles
    ahead relax (1).
14. Amylase from the salivary glands and pancreas (1) digests starch to maltose (1); maltase from the
    small intestine wall (1) digests maltose to glucose (1).
15. Bile emulsifies lipids into small droplets (1), increasing surface area for lipase (1); bile is
    alkaline / neutralises stomach acid (1), giving the optimum pH for lipase (1).
16. Villi and microvilli give a large surface area (1); wall one cell thick — short diffusion
    distance (1); dense capillary network carries away glucose and amino acids, maintaining the
    concentration gradient (1); lacteal absorbs fatty acids and glycerol (1); many mitochondria for
    active transport (1). Max 4.
17. Energy = 25 × 4.2 × 32 = 3360 J (2); per gram = 3360 ÷ 0.8 = 4200 J/g (1).
18. Heat lost to the surroundings / tube, or food not fully burnt (1), so less energy reaches the
    water (1). Improvement: insulate the tube / keep food close / relight until burnt / use a
    calorimeter (1).
