## Evidence (5.1.1)

- **Global temperature data** — thermometers since ~1850. Now ~1.3–1.4 °C above 1850–1900; 2024
  warmest (~1.55 °C); fastest rise since 1980. *Limit:* few early stations; towns warm stations.
- **Ice cores** — layers date the ice; bubbles → past CO₂; oxygen isotopes → past temperature.
  800 000 years. CO₂ and temperature move together; CO₂ now ~424 ppm, past peaks ~300 ppm.
  *Limit:* polar ice only; deep layers hard to date.
- **Sea ice positions** — satellites since 1979. Arctic September extent ~7.0 → ~4.4 million km²;
  record low 2012. *Limit:* short record, big year-to-year swings.
- **Historic writing and paintings** — diaries, harvest dates, ships' logs; Thames frost fairs
  1608–1814 (Little Ice Age, c. 1300–1850). *Limit:* descriptive, local, may exaggerate.

## Natural factors (5.1.2)

- **Milankovitch cycles** — eccentricity (orbit shape) ~100 000 yr; tilt 22.1°–24.5° ~41 000 yr;
  precession (wobble) ~26 000 yr → glacial and interglacial periods. Too slow for recent warming.
- **Sunspots** — more sunspots = more active Sun = slightly more energy; 11-year cycle. Maunder
  Minimum 1645–1715 ↔ Little Ice Age. No rise in solar output since the 1980s.
- **Volcanic activity** — SO₂ into stratosphere → aerosols reflect sunlight → cooling for 1–3 years.
  Pinatubo 1991 → world ~0.5 °C cooler in 1992.

## Human influence (5.1.3)

Chain: **activity → greenhouse gas → absorbs infrared from the surface → re-emits it back down → more
heat kept → global warming.**

| Activity | Gas | Key point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Burning fossil fuels | CO₂ | largest source |
| Deforestation | CO₂ | stored carbon released + carbon sink lost |
| Agriculture | methane, nitrous oxide | cattle, rice fields, fertiliser; methane ~30× CO₂ |

CO₂: ~280 ppm before industry → ~424 ppm (2024).

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Climate change | Long-term change (decades+) in average weather conditions of a place or the Earth |
| Greenhouse effect | Natural trapping of infrared from the surface by atmospheric gases, keeping Earth warm |
| Enhanced greenhouse effect | Extra warming caused by humans adding greenhouse gases |
| Global warming | Rise in the Earth's average surface temperature |

## Common traps

- Natural greenhouse effect is needed; the **enhanced** effect is the problem.
- Gases absorb outgoing **infrared**, not incoming sunlight. Ozone hole ≠ global warming.
