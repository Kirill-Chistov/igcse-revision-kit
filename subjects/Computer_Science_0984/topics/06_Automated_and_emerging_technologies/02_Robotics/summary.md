# 6.2 Robotics — summary

Cambridge IGCSE (9–1) Computer Science 0984 · points 6.2.1–6.2.3 · Paper 1

## Definition and examples

- **Robotics** — branch of computer science: **design, construction and operation** of robots.
- Examples: factory equipment (welding arm), domestic robots (robot vacuum), drones.

## Characteristics (all three)

1. **Mechanical structure / framework** — base, arms, joints, end effector.
2. **Electrical components** — sensors (sense surroundings), microprocessor (decides), actuators
   (move it).
3. **Programmable** — runs a program that can be changed for a new task.

## Advantages / disadvantages

- **+** 24/7, higher productivity · consistent · hazardous conditions · good at repetitive tasks ·
  no wages.
- **−** expensive to buy and maintain · poor at non-standard tasks · unemployment · deskilling.

## Roles

- **Industry** — welding, painting, assembling, warehouses.
- **Transport** — autonomous cars/buses/trains, delivery drones (hacking, trust).
- **Agriculture** — picking ripe crops, weeding, seeding, drones spraying.
- **Medicine** — surgery, blood samples, disinfecting, prosthetic limbs.
- **Domestic** — vacuuming, mowing, cleaning, personal assistants (privacy).
- **Entertainment** — theme parks, camera control, special effects, stunts.

## Traps

- Chatbots aren't robots (no mechanical structure). Robots needn't be AI.
