# Stellar evolution — Summary

Specification points 8.7–8.12P · Topic 8 Astrophysics. **8.11P and 8.12P are Paper 2 only.**

## Essential facts and ideas

- Colour ↔ **surface temperature**: hotter → more short-wavelength light → bluer.
  Hottest **blue → white → yellow (Sun) → orange → red** coolest. So colour classifies stars.
- **Every star:** nebula (dust and gas) → gravity collapses it (protostar) → hydrogen fuses to
  helium → **main sequence**, stable because **inward gravity = outward pressure** from fusion.
- **Sun-like:** main sequence → **red giant** (core hydrogen runs out; swells, surface cools)
  → **white dwarf** (outer layers drift away; small, hot, dense core cools).
- **Much more massive:** main sequence → **red supergiant** → **supernova** → **neutron star**,
  or **black hole** if the core is massive enough.

## Paper 2

- **Absolute magnitude:** brightness a star would have at a standard distance (10 parsecs).
  **Lower / more negative = brighter.** Sun ≈ +5.
- **HR diagram:** absolute magnitude up the side (brighter upwards, negative at top) against
  surface temperature (**hotter on the left**). Main sequence diagonal top-left → bottom-right;
  supergiants along the top; red giants upper right; white dwarfs bottom left.

## Common traps

- Red stars are the coolest, not the hottest.
- HR temperature axis decreases to the right.
- The Sun ends as a white dwarf, not a supernova.
