## Answers and marking guidance

*Marking points written for this repository — not an official Cambridge mark scheme.*

**1.1** Stack: isolated pillar of rock standing above high tide (1). Stump: the eroded remains of a
stack, covered at high tide (1).

**1.2** Long, narrow ridge of sand/shingle (1) joined to the land at one end, other end in open
water (1).

**1.3** Marram grass (1).

**1.4** A body of water cut off from the sea (1) behind a bar that has grown across a bay and
joined two headlands (1).

**2.1** Flat/gently sloping (1); rocky surface at the foot of a cliff (1); covered at high tide,
exposed at low tide (1); rock pools (1). Max 3.

**2.2** Water drains (percolates) into gaps between pebbles (1), so the backwash is weak (1) and
pebbles are left high up the beach, building a steep slope (1); sand is less permeable so the
backwash is stronger and carries sand down (1). Max 3.

**2.3** Discordant: rock bands at right angles to the coast (1) → irregular coast of headlands
and bays (1). Concordant: bands parallel to the coast (1) → straight coast, coves where the outer
hard band is breached (1).

**3.1** Wave-cut notch (1); destructive waves attack the base between high and low tide (1) by
hydraulic action/corrasion (1).

**3.2** Spit (1); longshore drift carries sediment east (1); where the coast turns north the
sediment is deposited in calmer water, building out from the land (1); river current stops it
crossing; possible hooked end/salt marsh behind (1).

**4.1** Headland of hard rock with a joint/fault (1); widened by hydraulic action/corrasion (1)
into a cave (1); cave eroded through the headland → arch (1); arch roof weakened by erosion and
weathering collapses → stack (1). Max 5. Sequence and named processes are needed for full marks.

**4.2** Longshore drift moves sediment along the coast (1); coast changes direction (1); sediment
deposited in sheltered water, extending the spit (1); secondary wind/waves curve the end (1);
salt marsh forms behind in low-energy water (1); river current prevents it crossing the estuary (1).
Max 5.

**4.3** Large, wide, flat beach and plentiful sand (1); onshore winds blow dry sand inland (1);
trapped by obstacles → embryo dunes (1); marram grass binds sand and dunes grow (1); humus builds
up → grey/mature dunes (1). Max 4.

**4.4** Alternating bands of hard and soft rock meet the coast at right angles (1); soft rock
eroded faster (1) → bays (1); hard rock resists → headlands (1).
