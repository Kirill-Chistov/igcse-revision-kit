# Electricity — units — Summary

Specification point 2.1 · Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Physics 4PH1 · both papers.

- Current *I* — **A** · charge *Q* — **C** · voltage *V* — **V** · resistance *R* — **Ω** · energy *E* — **J** · power *P* — **W** · time *t* — **s**.
- 1 A = 1 C/s · 1 V = 1 J/C · 1 Ω = 1 V/A · 1 W = 1 J/s.
- m = ÷ 1000 · k = × 1000 · M = × 1 000 000 · 1 min = 60 s · 1 h = 3600 s.
- Convert to A, V, Ω, W, J and **seconds** before substituting.
