# Solids, liquids and gases — units

## Specification coverage

| Point | Section |
| --- | --- |
| 5.1 use the following units: degree Celsius (°C), Kelvin (K), joule (J), kilogram (kg), kilogram/metre³ (kg/m³), metre (m), metre² (m²), metre³ (m³), metre/second (m/s), metre/second² (m/s²), newton (N) and pascal (Pa) | 1, 2 |
| 5.2P use the following unit: joules/kilogram degree Celsius (J/kg °C) — Paper 2 only | 1 |

## 1. The units (5.1, 5.2P)

| Quantity | Unit | Symbol | Used for in this topic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| temperature | degree Celsius / Kelvin | °C / K | temperature of a gas must be in **K** in *p*₁/*T*₁ = *p*₂/*T*₂ |
| energy | joule | J | thermal energy transferred, Δ*Q* |
| mass | kilogram | kg | density, specific heat capacity |
| density | kilogram/metre³ | kg/m³ | ρ = *m*/*V* and *p* = *h*ρ*g* |
| length, height, depth | metre | m | dimensions of a block; depth *h* in a liquid |
| area | metre² | m² | *p* = *F*/*A* |
| volume | metre³ | m³ | ρ = *m*/*V*; gas volume |
| speed | metre/second | m/s | speed of gas molecules |
| acceleration | metre/second² | m/s² | *g* = 10 m/s² = 10 N/kg in *p* = *h*ρ*g* |
| force | newton | N | *p* = *F*/*A* |
| pressure | pascal | Pa | **1 Pa = 1 N/m²** |
| specific heat capacity — Paper 2 only | joules/kilogram degree Celsius | J/kg °C | Δ*Q* = *m* × *c* × Δ*T* |

## 2. Conversions

```
1 kPa = 1000 Pa              1 g = 0.001 kg
1 m² = 100 × 100 cm²         = 10 000 cm²      (cm² → m²: ÷ 10 000)
1 m³ = 100 × 100 × 100 cm³   = 1 000 000 cm³   (cm³ → m³: ÷ 1 000 000)
1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³         (water: 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³)
```

A temperature **change** of 1 °C is the same as a change of 1 K, so Δ*T* has the same value on
both scales. Converting a temperature reading between the scales → 5.17 (Ideal gas molecules).

::: traps
| Wrong | Right |
| --- | --- |
| 250 cm² = 2.5 m² (dividing by 100) | 250 cm² = 250 ÷ 10 000 = **0.025 m²** |
| Leaving mass in g in ρ = *m*/*V* with *V* in m³ | kg with m³ (→ kg/m³), or g with cm³ (→ g/cm³) — never mixed |
| "kelvins degrees" or °K | just **K** |
:::
