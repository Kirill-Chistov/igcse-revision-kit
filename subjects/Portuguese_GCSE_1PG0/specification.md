## Course distinctions that affect what can be assessed

- **Tier:** one tier for *all four* papers. At Higher (1H–4H) the additional Higher Tier grammar
  in Appendix 2 is **required**; at Foundation it is not.
- All papers draw on vocabulary and structures from **all five themes** — no theme is tied to
  a single paper.
- All topics must be studied in the context of **both** the student's home country **and**
  countries/communities where Portuguese is spoken.
- **Translation** is assessed in both directions: Portuguese→English (Paper 3, Section C) and
  English→Portuguese (Paper 4).
- Paper 2 has three fixed tasks in order: role play (topic allocated by Pearson), picture-based
  questions (topic allocated by Pearson), conversation on two themes (one chosen by the student
  in advance, one allocated by Pearson).
- **Appendix 2 (grammar list)** and **Appendix 3 (vocabulary list)** are assessable content.
  Structures marked `(R)` in the grammar list require **receptive** knowledge only. The grammar
  list is split into Foundation and additional Higher Tier requirements — **at Higher tier both
  parts apply**.
- Both European and Brazilian Portuguese variants appear in the vocabulary list (`EP`/`BP`).

## Note on identifiers

Pearson numbers the **themes** but names the **topics** without numbers. This repository uses
local identifiers of the form `T4.2` (= Theme 4, second topic) so that content can be mapped
and tracked. `G1`… identify grammar categories and `S1`… identify the assessed skills/papers.
These are **repository-local** identifiers, not official Pearson codes.


## How to read this file

- This file is a **curriculum representation**, not revision notes. It exists so that AI
  agents can plan and trace study material without re-parsing the official PDF.
- Official identifiers (section numbers, specification point numbers, topic names) are
  preserved so every statement can be traced back to `subjects/Portuguese_GCSE_1PG0/Specification.pdf`.
- Requirement wording is condensed/paraphrased from the official objective statements.
  **The official PDF is always authoritative** where this file is ambiguous.
- `specification_map.csv` maps each specification point to its planned folder in `Portuguese_GCSE_1PG0/`.
- `coverage_checklist.md` tracks which points have study material written.
