# Ideal gas molecules — Summary

Specification points 5.15–5.22 · Topic 5 · both papers.

## Essential facts and ideas

- **Gas pressure:** molecules move randomly → hit the walls → each collision exerts a force on the
  wall → total force per unit area = pressure.
- **Kelvin temperature ∝ average kinetic energy** of the molecules. Hotter → more KE → higher
  average speed (KE = ½*mv*², mass fixed).
- **Absolute zero = −273 °C = 0 K:** molecules have no kinetic energy; KE cannot go lower, so no
  lower temperature exists; a gas would exert no pressure.
- **Kelvin scale:** starts at absolute zero; 1 K step = 1 °C step. *T* = θ + 273.
- **Smaller volume, constant *T*:** same speed, more collisions per second on each unit area → higher *p*.
- **Higher *T*, constant *V*:** faster molecules → collisions more often **and** harder → higher *p*.

## Equations (both given in the exam; fixed mass of gas)

| Equation | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| *p*₁*V*₁ = *p*₂*V*₂ | constant temperature |
| *p*₁/*T*₁ = *p*₂/*T*₂ | constant volume — *T* in **K** |

## Common traps

- °C in *p*/*T* → convert to K.
- Compressing at constant *T*: molecules hit more often, **not** harder.
