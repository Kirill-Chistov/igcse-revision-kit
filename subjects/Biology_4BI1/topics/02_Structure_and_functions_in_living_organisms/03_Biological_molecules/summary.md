## Molecules

| Molecule | Elements | Built from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starch (plants), glycogen (animals) | C, H, O | simple sugars (glucose) |
| Protein | C, H, O, N | amino acids |
| Lipid | C, H, O | glycerol + 3 fatty acids |

## Food tests

- Glucose: Benedict's + heat → blue to brick-red.
- Starch: iodine → orange-brown to blue-black.
- Protein: biuret → blue to purple.
- Fat: ethanol, then water → cloudy white emulsion.

## Enzymes

- Proteins; biological catalysts — speed up metabolic reactions, not used up.
- Substrate fits the complementary active site → enzyme–substrate complex → products.
- Rising temperature → more collisions → faster, up to optimum (~40 °C for amylase).
- Above optimum or away from optimum pH → active site changes shape → substrate does not fit → denatured.
- Cold: slow, not denatured.

## Practical (amylase + starch, iodine every 30 s)

- IV: temperature (or pH via buffers — P2). DV: time for iodine to stay orange-brown. Rate = 1/time.
- Control: concentrations, volumes, pH (temperature for the pH practical). Boiled amylase = control.
- Improve: sample more often, repeats, colour reference.
