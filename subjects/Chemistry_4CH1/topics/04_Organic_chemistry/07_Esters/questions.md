# Esters — Questions

Specification points 4.38C–4.43C (Paper 2 only) · Topic 4 Organic chemistry. Marks in brackets.

> All questions and marking guidance in this file are written for this repository; they are not
> official Pearson questions or mark schemes.

## 1. Recall

1. Draw the functional group of the esters. (1)
2. Name the ester formed from ethanol and ethanoic acid, and give the catalyst. (2)
3. Give two uses of esters and the properties that make them suitable. (3)

## 2. Formulae and names

4. Write the equation for the formation of ethyl ethanoate using structural formulae. (2)
5. Draw the displayed formula of ethyl ethanoate. (1)
6. Name the ester formed from methanol and butanoic acid and give its structural formula. (2)
7. Give the displayed formula of propyl methanoate. (2)
8. Name the alcohol and the carboxylic acid that form ethyl propanoate, CH₃CH₂COOCH₂CH₃. (2)

## 3. Practical

9. Describe how to prepare a small sample of ethyl ethanoate in the laboratory. (4)
10. Explain why the mixture is poured into sodium carbonate solution at the end. (2)
11. Give one safety precaution and its reason. (2)

## Answers and marking guidance

1. C double-bonded to O and single-bonded to an O that is bonded to another C (–COO–) (1).
2. Ethyl ethanoate (1); (concentrated) sulfuric acid / an acid catalyst (1).
3. Food flavourings (1); perfumes (1); volatile with distinctive smells (1).
4. CH₃COOH + CH₃CH₂OH → CH₃COOCH₂CH₃ + H₂O — ester formula (1), rest correct (1).
5. CH₃–C(=O)–O–CH₂–CH₃ with all bonds shown (1).
6. Methyl butanoate (1); CH₃CH₂CH₂COOCH₃ (1).
7. H–C(=O)–O– (1) joined to a CH₂CH₂CH₃ chain with all bonds shown (1).
8. Ethanol (1); propanoic acid (1).
9. Mix ethanol and ethanoic acid (1); add a few drops of concentrated sulfuric acid (1); warm in a
   (hot) water bath (1); pour into sodium carbonate solution and smell by wafting (1).
10. Neutralises the remaining acids (1) so their smell does not mask the ester / the ester forms a
    separate layer (1).
11. Water bath, no naked flame (1) because ethanol is flammable (1); or eye protection / gloves (1)
    because concentrated sulfuric acid is corrosive (1).
