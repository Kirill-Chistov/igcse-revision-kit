## Marking guidance

*Written for this repository — not an official Pearson mark scheme.*

1. The number of employees who report directly to one manager.
2. Passing authority to a subordinate to carry out a task or make a decision.
3. Any two: paying wages/salaries, cash-flow forecasting, budgets, accounting.
4. B.
5. Identify (long chain of command / many managers / remote senior managers) + two links, e.g.
   messages pass through many levels → distorted or slow → poor or late decisions.
6. Identify (decisions suit local markets / faster decisions) → e.g. local managers know local
   customers → products and prices fit → higher sales.
7. Dismissal is for a reason linked to the employee (misconduct, poor performance); redundancy is
   because the job is no longer needed → not the employee's fault, redundancy pay usually given.
8. Wide span → she cannot supervise or support each employee closely → mistakes or low
   motivation / she is overloaded.
