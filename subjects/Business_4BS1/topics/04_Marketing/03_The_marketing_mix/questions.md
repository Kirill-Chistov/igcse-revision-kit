## 3. Calculate

11. A shoe shop buys trainers for €56 each and uses cost-plus pricing with a 35% mark-up.
    Calculate the selling price. (2)
12. A hotel room costs AED 480 a night. For one weekend it offers a 12.5% promotional discount.
    Calculate the discounted price. (2)

## 4. Applied

13. A small bakery sells only in its shop. Outline one benefit of also selling through an online
    marketplace. (2)
14. A games-console maker is launching a new console. It is considering (1) price skimming or
    (2) penetration pricing. Justify which one it should choose. (9)

## Marking guidance

1. Setting a high price when a new (unique) product is launched, then lowering it over time.
2. A business that sells products to consumers online / through e-commerce.
3. Promotion that does not use mass media, where the business targets chosen customers
   directly (e.g. special offers, sponsorship, PR).
4. Development, introduction, growth, maturity, decline.
5. Cash cow.
6. Identify a strategy (e.g. update the product with new features) → product appeals again /
   to new customers → sales rise again, delaying decline.
7. Identify (shows balance of portfolio / where to invest) → cash from cash cows directed to
   stars and question marks → future sales and profit protected.
8. Identify (low profit per unit at first) → revenue may not cover development costs → or
   customers leave when the price rises.
9. Identify (customers share the content) → reaches very many people at low cost → awareness
   and sales rise without paying for mass media.
10. Identify (recognition/loyalty) → customers choose it over rivals and buy again → can charge
    a premium price → higher profit margins.
11. 56 × 35 ÷ 100 = €19.60 (1); 56 + 19.60 = **€75.60** (1).
12. 480 × 12.5 ÷ 100 = AED 60 (1); 480 − 60 = **AED 420** (1).
13. Identify (reaches customers beyond the local area / 24/7) (1) → more orders and revenue
    for the bakery without opening a second shop (1).
14. Level-marked. Skimming: new, innovative console, keen early buyers pay more → high profit
    per unit recovers R&D; premium image; but low early sales, rivals attracted, early buyers
    resent cuts. Penetration: fast market share, attracts price-sensitive buyers, locks in
    games sales; but low early profit, price rises lose customers. Judgement in context
    (e.g. how unique the console is, strength of rivals, brand reputation).
