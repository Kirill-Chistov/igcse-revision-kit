## Key terms

- **Unlimited liability:** owners pay all debts from personal assets. **Limited liability:** lose only what was invested (company = separate legal identity).
- **Stakeholder:** anyone affected by the business. **Shareholder:** part-owner of a company.
- **Franchise:** franchisor lets franchisee use its name/brand/products for a fee + **royalties** (% of sales).
- **Social enterprise:** main objectives social/environmental; profits reinvested in the cause.
- **Multinational:** operations in more than one country (→ 1.5.2).

## Traps

- plc = private sector; public corporation = public sector.
- Franchise definition must say *agreement*, not "chain with the same logo".
