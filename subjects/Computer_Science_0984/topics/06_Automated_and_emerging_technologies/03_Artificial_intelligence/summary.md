# 6.3 Artificial intelligence — summary

Cambridge IGCSE (9–1) Computer Science 0984 · points 6.3.1–6.3.3 · Paper 1

## Definition and characteristics

- **AI** — branch of computer science dealing with the **simulation of intelligent behaviours** by
  computers.
- Characteristics: **data + rules** for using it · **reasoning** (draws conclusions) · **can include**
  **learning and adapting** (changes its own rules/data).

## Expert system

- Simulates a human expert in one area (diagnosis, fault-finding, prospecting, chess).
- **Knowledge base** = facts · **rule base** = IF…THEN rules · **inference engine** = applies rules to
  facts and answers, picks next question, concludes · **interface** = questions in, conclusion +
  probability + reasons out.
- Does not learn by itself.

## Machine learning

- A program that **automatically adapts its own processes and/or data**.
- Training data → finds patterns → decides on new data → feedback → adapts itself → repeats; no
  reprogramming (e.g. spam filter).

## Traps

- Facts in the knowledge base, rules in the rule base, reasoning in the inference engine.
