# Excretion — Questions

**Specification points 2.70–2.79B** · questions marked (P2) are Paper 2 only

Marking guidance below is written for this repository; it is not an official mark scheme.

## Questions

1. Explain why oxygen is an excretory product of a plant in bright light.
2. Name the excretory products of (a) the lungs, (b) the skin.
3. Why is the removal of faeces not excretion?
4. (P2) State the two roles of the kidney.
5. (P2) Put these in the order that urine passes through them: bladder, collecting duct, urethra,
   ureter.
6. (P2) Name the parts of a nephron in order from where filtration takes place.
7. (P2) **[3 marks]** *Describe* how ultrafiltration occurs in the Bowman's capsule.
8. (P2) Name two substances in the blood that are not present in the glomerular filtrate, and
   explain why.
9. (P2) **[2 marks]** Explain why glucose is found in the glomerular filtrate but not in urine.
10. (P2) **[5 marks]** A person runs on a hot day and sweats a lot. *Explain* how ADH helps to keep
    the water content of their blood constant.
11. (P2) A person drinks a large volume of water. Describe how the volume and concentration of their
    urine changes.
12. (P2) Name three substances found in urine.

## Answers and marking guidance

1. Photosynthesis produces more oxygen than respiration uses (1); the excess diffuses out through
   the stomata (1).
2. (a) carbon dioxide (and water vapour); (b) sweat — water, ions/salts, a little urea.
3. Faeces are undigested food that has not been made by / taken part in metabolism in cells.
4. Excretion (removal of urea) and osmoregulation (control of blood water content).
5. Collecting duct, ureter, bladder, urethra.
6. Glomerulus / Bowman's capsule, proximal convoluted tubule, loop of Henle, distal convoluted
   tubule, collecting duct.
7. Blood enters the glomerulus at high pressure (1) because the arteriole in is wider than the one
   out (1); small molecules such as water, glucose, urea, ions are forced into the capsule (1).
8. Proteins and blood cells (1); too large to pass through the capillary wall / capsule (1).
9. Glucose is small enough to be filtered (1); it is all selectively reabsorbed in the proximal
   convoluted tubule because the body needs it for respiration (1).
10. Water lost in sweat makes the blood too concentrated (1); detected by the brain (1); pituitary
    releases more ADH (1); collecting ducts become more permeable to water (1); more water
    reabsorbed into the blood, small volume of concentrated urine (1).
11. Less ADH is released (1); a large volume of dilute urine is produced (1).
12. Water, urea, ions (salts).
