## Practice

1. **[1]** Define the term *relocation*.
2. **[3]** Explain one reason why proximity to the market is important for a bakery.
3. **[3]** Explain one disadvantage for a clothing retailer of selling only through e-commerce.
4. **[6]** A car manufacturer is planning a new factory. Analyse the benefits of locating the factory inside a trade bloc.
5. **[9]** A small hair salon is choosing new premises. Option 1: a busy city-centre shopping street. Option 2: cheaper premises in a quiet residential area. Justify which one of these two options it should choose.

## Marking guidance (not an official mark scheme)

1. Moving all or part of a business to a new location.
2. Bread is perishable (1) → it must reach customers fresh and quickly (1) → being near customers cuts delivery time and waste, and makes it easy for customers to call in (1).
3. Customers cannot try clothes on (1) → more are returned (1) → higher delivery and returns costs reduce profit (1).
4. In context: no tariffs on cars sold to member countries → lower prices than rivals outside the bloc → more sales; lower transport costs to bloc customers → higher profit per car.
5. For (e.g. city centre): passing trade → more walk-in customers → higher revenue. Against: higher rent → higher costs; more competing salons. Judgement specific to the salon, e.g. whether customers book in advance or walk in.
