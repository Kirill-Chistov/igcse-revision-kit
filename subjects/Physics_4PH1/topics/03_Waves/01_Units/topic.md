# Units

## Specification coverage

| Point | Section |
| --- | --- |
| 3.1 use the following units: degree (°), hertz (Hz), metre (m), metre/second (m/s) and second (s) | 1, 2 |

## 1. The units (3.1)

| Unit | Symbol | Used for in this topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| degree | ° | angles of incidence *i*, reflection and refraction *r*; critical angle *c* |
| hertz | Hz | frequency *f* |
| metre | m | wavelength λ, amplitude, distance |
| metre/second | m/s | wave speed *v* |
| second | s | time period *T*, time |

**1 Hz = one complete wave (oscillation) per second**, so 1 Hz = 1/s. That is why *f* = 1/*T*
gives hertz when *T* is in seconds, and why *v* = *f*λ gives m/s when λ is in metres.

## 2. Prefix conversions

Convert to Hz, m and s **before** substituting; give answers in standard form when they are very
large or small.

| Given as | Multiply by | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kHz · MHz · GHz | 10³ · 10⁶ · 10⁹ → Hz | 98 MHz = 9.8 × 10⁷ Hz |
| cm · mm · nm | 10⁻² · 10⁻³ · 10⁻⁹ → m | 600 nm = 6.0 × 10⁻⁷ m |
| km | 10³ → m | 1.5 km = 1500 m |
| ms · µs | 10⁻³ · 10⁻⁶ → s | 2.5 ms = 2.5 × 10⁻³ s |

::: traps
| Wrong | Right |
| --- | --- |
| Substituting kHz or MHz straight into *v* = *f*λ. | Convert to Hz first. |
| Calculator in radians when finding sin *i* or sin⁻¹. | Set it to **degrees** (D) — every wave angle is in °. |
| Measuring angles from the surface of a mirror or block. | Angles are always measured from the **normal**. |
:::
