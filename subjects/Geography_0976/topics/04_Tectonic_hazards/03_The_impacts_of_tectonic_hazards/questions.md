## Questions

1. **[5]** *Explain* why people continue to live in areas at risk from volcanic eruptions.
2. **[2]** *Define* primary and secondary impacts.
3. **[4]** *Describe* the secondary impacts of earthquakes.
4. **[6]** *Explain* why earthquakes of similar magnitude can cause very different numbers of deaths.
5. **[5]** *Describe* the positive and negative impacts of volcanic eruptions.
6. **[4]** *Compare* the Richter scale and the Mercalli scale.
7. **[2]** An earthquake measures magnitude 6. *Explain* how a magnitude 8 earthquake differs.
8. **[3]** *Describe* how the volcanic explosivity index (VEI) is measured.

## Marking guidance

1. Fertile volcanic soils → high yields (1); geothermal energy (1); minerals / mining jobs (1);
   tourism income (1); poverty — cannot afford to move (1); family / lived there for generations
   (1); eruptions rare, risk seen as low (1); confidence in monitoring (1). Max 5; development of
   a point may earn a second mark.
2. Primary: caused directly by the hazard (1). Secondary: result from the primary impacts, often
   later (1).
3. Fires from broken gas pipes (1); disease from contaminated water / crowded shelters (1);
   homelessness (1); landslides / tsunami (1); aid delayed by blocked roads (1); loss of jobs /
   rebuilding costs (1). Max 4.
4. Depth of focus (1); distance from epicentre / population density (1); building quality (1);
   level of development — LICs less money for protection, fewer emergency services, slower aid (1–2);
   time of day (1); preparation / education (1). Explanation needed, not a list, for full marks.
5. Negative: deaths from pyroclastic flows (1); homes, crops buried (1); flights disrupted by ash
   (1); lahars / acid rain (1). Positive: fertile soil (1); tourism / geothermal (1). Need both
   sides for full marks. Max 5.
6. Richter measures magnitude — size of seismic waves on a seismometer (1); Mercalli measures
   intensity — observed effects and damage (1); Richter logarithmic numbers, Mercalli I–XII (1);
   Richter one value per quake, Mercalli varies with distance from epicentre (1).
7. Logarithmic scale: 100 times bigger waves (1); about 1 000 times (32 × 32) more energy (1).
8. Volume of material erupted (1); height of the eruption/ash column (1); 0–8, each step from VEI 2
   ten times more material (1).
