## 1. Recall

1. Give the unit of: (a) frequency (b) wave speed (c) time period (d) critical angle.
2. What does 1 Hz mean?

## 2. Application

3. Convert: (a) 2.4 GHz to Hz (b) 450 nm to m (c) 0.8 ms to s (d) 15 kHz to Hz.
4. A student works out sin 30° and gets −0.988. What has gone wrong?

## Answers and marking guidance

*Not an official mark scheme.*

1. (a) hertz, Hz (b) metre/second, m/s (c) second, s (d) degree, °
2. One complete wave (oscillation) per second.
3. (a) 2.4 × 10⁹ Hz (b) 4.5 × 10⁻⁷ m (c) 8 × 10⁻⁴ s (d) 1.5 × 10⁴ Hz
4. The calculator is in radians; set it to degrees (sin 30° = 0.5).
