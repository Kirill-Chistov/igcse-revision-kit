# Movement of substances into and out of cells — Summary

**Specification points 2.15–2.17** · Papers 1 and 2

## Processes

| | Direction | Energy | Membrane |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Diffusion | high → low concentration | none (passive) | not needed |
| Osmosis | water: dilute → concentrated | none (passive) | partially permeable |
| Active transport | low → high (against gradient) | respiration | carrier proteins |

## Cells in solutions

- Animal cell: dilute → swells, bursts; concentrated → shrinks.
- Plant cell: dilute → turgid (wall prevents bursting); concentrated → flaccid → plasmolysed.

## Faster movement when

- larger SA:V (cube: SA = 6 × side², V = side³); shorter distance; higher temperature; steeper gradient.

## Practicals

- Agar cubes in acid: smallest cube decolourises first.
- Visking tubing with sucrose in water: level rises (osmosis).
- Potato cylinders: % change in mass = (final − initial) ÷ initial × 100; zero crossing = same concentration as cells. Blot dry; same size, time, temperature.
