# Extraction and uses of metals — Summary

Specification points 2.22C–2.27C · Topic 2 Inorganic chemistry. **All Paper 2 only.**

## Essential facts

- Most metals: compounds in **ores** in the Earth's crust. Unreactive (gold, some silver):
  **uncombined**. Extraction = **reduction**.
- **Above carbon** (K → Al): **electrolysis** of molten compound — expensive (electricity).
  **Below carbon** (Zn, Fe, Cu): **heat oxide with carbon** — cheaper.
- Iron: 2Fe₂O₃ + 3C → 4Fe + 3CO₂ (in furnace also Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂); carbon = reducing agent.
- Aluminium: electrolysis of molten Al₂O₃ — cathode Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al; anode 2O²⁻ → O₂ + 4e⁻.
- Commenting: method vs reactivity; energy cost; raw materials; environment (CO₂, mining);
  recycling.
- **Alloy** = mixture of a metal with other elements (usually metals or carbon). Harder:
  different-sized atoms **distort layers** → layers can't slide.

## Uses

| Metal | Key property → use |
| --- | --- |
| Aluminium | low density, corrosion-resistant → aircraft, cans; conductor + light → power cables |
| Copper | excellent conductor, ductile → wiring; unreactive → water pipes |
| Iron | soft, rusts → made into steel |
| Mild steel | strong, malleable → car bodies, ships |
| High-carbon steel | hard, brittle → cutting tools |
| Stainless steel | resists corrosion → cutlery, sinks, surgical instruments |

## Common traps

- Alloy is a mixture, not a compound. Al is protected by its oxide layer, not unreactive.
